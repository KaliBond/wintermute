How results are treated

Every submission is checked for the required CSV columns, all 128 rows, the case and metric order, the score and evidence-tier format, and the SESSION code. An identical reply submitted twice is rejected.

Passing these checks means structurally valid, not historically verified. Before any submission is used in research it is reviewed for evidence quality, independence, model version and possible coordinated submissions. Public submissions are kept as a separate cohort and are never pooled with the original controlled 105-session study.

What is stored

  • The model reply you paste, the assignment it answers, and the model name and version you enter.
  • The server's time of receipt (UTC).
  • Your local time and city or country, only if you choose to enter them. They appear on your receipt.

No name, email address, IP address or device location is stored. Submissions are kept as research data for the CAMS project, which publishes its data openly, so submitted replies may be included in a future open dataset. To ask for a submission to be removed, email kari.freyr.4@gmail.com with its receipt ID.
