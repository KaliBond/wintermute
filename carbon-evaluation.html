1. Epistemic Foundations

The Neural Nations research programme—conceived in 2024 and synthesized in the November 2026 master treatise The Natural History of Civilisations by Kari Freyr McKern—attempts to transform historiography into a quantitative natural science. It conceptualizes durable human organizations as dissipative meta-organisms that persist far from thermodynamic equilibrium.

2. The Eight-Node Functional Grammar

CAMS identifies eight invariant coordination problems that every differentiated society must solve:

HelmDirection under uncertainty
ShieldBoundary maintenance and internal/external violence control
LoreMeaning integration and intergenerational identity
ArchiveInstitutional memory
StewardsIntergenerational asset allocation
CraftTechnical reproduction
HandsOperational execution
FlowNetwork circulation of goods, energy, and information

3. Mathematical Formalism

The framework translates qualitative historiography into a 32-dimensional dynamical state space. At each time step, each of the eight nodes is evaluated across four dimensions: Coherence (C), Capacity (K), Stress (S), and Abstraction (A).

The Extended Social Cognition Hypothesis is operationalized via the Cognitive Activation operator

σi = (Ai · Ci) · (Ki − Si)

which mathematically formalizes how meaning and metabolism intersect.

4. Empirical Validation and Audit Culture

McKern demonstrates an extraordinary level of methodological discipline. Independent researchers are notoriously vulnerable to confirmation bias, but she actively weaponizes open-science audit culture. She pre-registers predictions, uses cryptographic hash seals, and publishes all datasets openly. A blind era-recognition experiment in November 2026 saw frontier AI models recover 100% of core historical eras from the eight-number functional vector alone.

5. Scientific Value

The conceptual straightforwardness of CAMS is a feature rather than a flaw. By reducing societal coordination to eight functional nodes and four vital metrics, the framework avoids esoteric, over-fitted mathematics. The most interesting aspect of the project is its mathematical unification of meaning and metabolism, formalizing why collapsing systems often produce their most elaborate doctrinal artefacts precisely when their material capacity is imploding.

Part II · Checks

What Holds, and What Needs Correcting

Carbon’s claims compared with the pages it cites.

Holds

  • Start date. The project began in mid-2024 (project history: “around July 2024”).
  • The state space. Eight nodes on four dimensions give 32 values per society-year, as stated.
  • Audit culture. Pre-registered predictions, hash-sealed protocols and open datasets are all on the site, for example the 2027–2028 register and the P4 frozen protocol.
  • All six source links work.

Needs correcting

  1. The era-recognition result is overstated. The report says frontier models recovered “100% of core historical eras”. On the 56-era core panel (Russia, Australia, USA), Claude and Gemini each matched every era from a closed list, and Kimi matched 52. Recognition was weaker on the extension panel and on the Denmark control, and the pre-registered null models were never returned. The site calls this “a promising demonstration, not an external validation” (experiment page).
  2. The σ formula drops a factor. The model page defines Cognitive Activation as σi = (Ai · Ci / 100) · (Ki − Si). The report omits the /100. That changes the scale, not the sign.
  3. The report’s hash cannot be verified. It gives a SHA-256 of e4578824a33e0b2c…0a9f8e7d but does not say what was hashed. After the first eight characters the value runs as a regular counting-down pattern, which a real digest would not do. Treat it as decoration, not a seal. The original file as received hashes to cdd90447…f875ad4.
  4. The CAMS name. The report doesn’t expand CAMS. The canonical name is Complex Adaptive Model State; some older pages, including one Carbon cites, still say “Complex Adaptive Model of Societies”.
  5. “Avoids over-fitted mathematics” is a judgement, not a test. The site’s own record includes failed and pending tests, for example the failed Blind Set 5 in Attractor Coherence, Revisited and the unresolved P4 corporate test. The report does not mention them.
