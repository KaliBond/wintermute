CAMS · synthetic experiment · version 2.2 · Shannon layer, audited

When agreement stops being learning

Compare two eight-function societies exposed to exactly the same environmental change and observations. Change only the extent to which operational feedback reaches decision-making. Does coherence guarantee adaptive success? Version 2.0 added the information-theoretic layer; 2.1 asked what a node is in information-processing terms. Version 2.2 incorporates an independent audit by GPT (OpenAI), November 2026: transfer entropy is now reported against a muted-source baseline and conditioned on the shared environment, so common drive is no longer sold as message; Archive gets a real record and a recurring environment; the authority echo is measured; and the dictionary is corrected (η_loop, Stress, the redundancy ceiling, and node value's candidate analogue).

2 · What happened?

At each round the societies form beliefs about an external condition. At the shock that condition changes from +1 to −1. A positive institutional belief after the shock is therefore inaccurate, even when everybody agrees.

The coherence dip is the learning. After the shock, coherence and decision fit fall and rise together (r ≈ +0.7 to +0.8 across seeds): agreement dips while the operators switch first and Helm lags, then recovers as Helm catches up. Agreement without correctness exists only in the first rounds, while everyone still agrees on the old state. A society that never dips either never heard the news or suppressed it, which makes the dip a better diagnostic than the level.

3 · Shannon analysis: what is shared, what moves, and what is only echoed

A long paired run (length set above, same seed) in which the environment reverses at the rate set by the slider, so reality itself carries entropy rather than switching once. The belief streams are quantised into discrete symbols. From the symbol streams: mutual information between functions, the informational analogue of bond strength Bij; transfer entropy at lag 1, directed flow between functions; the bits of the true environment carried by Helm's belief; and total correlation, the informational face of cross-node agreement. The 2026 audit forced two controls that change what these numbers mean. Every transfer entropy now stands beside its muted-source baseline (the same quantity with the source cut off from everyone: whatever remains cannot be a message, only common drive), and beside its value conditioned on the environment. Only the surplus over the baseline is flow in the strong sense. Plug-in estimators with Miller–Madow correction; circular-shift nulls that preserve autocorrelation, with the achievable p-floor printed. Descriptive, not validated.

How the Shannon quantities are estimated

Beliefs are quantised into the chosen symbol alphabet. Entropies are plug-in (maximum likelihood) with the Miller–Madow bias correction, Ĥ + (m−1)/(2N ln 2). Mutual information I(X;Y) = H(X) + H(Y) − H(X,Y), floored at zero. Transfer entropy TE(X→Y) = H(Y′|Y) − H(Y′|Y,X) at lag 1 under a first-order Markov assumption on the symbol stream. The environment-conditioned variant TE(X→Y|E) conditions every term on the next environmental state as well, removing what both streams merely share about the world. The muted-source baseline reruns the whole simulation with the source's outgoing weights zeroed and measures the same TE: what survives an intervention that makes messages impossible is common drive, and only the excess over it is flow.

The null is now 199 circular shifts of the source stream, which preserves each stream's autocorrelation (the earlier shuffle null destroyed it and was lenient); p is plus-one corrected, so the achievable floor is p ≥ 1/200 = 0.005, printed beside every value. Redundancy is the total correlation ΣH(Xᵢ) − H(X₁,…,X₈) on binary symbols: with larger alphabets the eight-variable joint is too sparse at these run lengths. Eight binary streams share at most 7 bits (eight marginals of at most 1 bit, minus a joint entropy of at least 1 bit); the ceiling printed in earlier versions was wrong. Conditioning the same quantity on the environment, ΣH(Xᵢ|E) − H(X₁,…,X₈|E), leaves the agreement that is more than everyone watching the same world. O-information (Rosas et al. 2019) summarises the whole system: positive means redundancy-dominated, negative synergy-dominated. This toy is always positive, and has to be: weighted averaging copies information but cannot create joint-only information, so an emergence model would need nonlinear coupling.

Channel capacity treats the gate as a binary symmetric channel with crossover probability (1 − gate)/2, so C = 1 − h₂(e) bits per message; this is an illustrative model of the gate, not a measured property. The entropy rate tile is the mean per-function conditional entropy H(Xᵢ′|Xᵢ), the uncertainty each function generates per round after accounting for its own past.

Limits: quantisation discards magnitude; plug-in entropies remain biased at short run lengths; lag-1 first-order transfer entropy misses longer or higher-order structure; conditioned estimators are noisier still. The random-reversal design is necessary: with a single scheduled shock the post-shock environment is a constant, and a constant carries zero bits: mutual information with it is degenerate by construction. All of it describes a constructed toy.

4 · What is a node? An information-processing signature

Treat each function not as a label but as a processing role, measured five ways: how much it fluctuates (entropy H), how much novelty it generates given its own past (entropy rate), how much it is informed (transfer entropy in), how much it informs others (transfer entropy out), and how much it knows about the environment (mutual information with the true state). Two functions with identical signatures under every intervention would be one node wearing two names; a partition that merges dissimilar signatures destroys real processing distinctions. That is the Compression Theorem stated in bits, and here it is testable.

Methods, and the caveat that matters

Signatures use the same quantised streams and estimators as Section 3. Clustering is agglomerative average-linkage on the five standardised signature features; the three-cluster cut is compared with the LAPH layer assignment (cognitive: Archive, Lore, Stewards; executive: Helm, Shield; material: Craft, Flow, Hands) under the best of the six label permutations. Silencing zeroes one function's outgoing network weights and reruns the identical seeded stream.

The caveat that matters: in this toy the signatures were written by the chosen reliabilities and weights, so layer recovery here validates the measurement programme, not the eightfold partition. On scored historical data the parameters are not imposed, and that is where this programme would bite. The transferable pieces are the signature vector, the emergence check and the intervention, not the numbers.

5 · The authority echo: control is not news

The audit's most robust finding. Helm's broadcast is the heaviest weight in the network, yet it adds essentially nothing over its own muted baseline: a broadcast that repeats what everyone already believes carries almost no new bits, though it can fix the level everyone sits at. What it does instead is subtler. Sweep the authority slider and two quantities move in opposite directions: agreement beyond what the shared environment explains (conditioned redundancy) rises, while Helm's own knowledge of the environment falls. The mechanism: the broadcast pulls the other nodes towards Helm, so their reports back to Helm carry Helm's own past. Authority contaminates the channels it depends on. This is the masking state in miniature: agreement up, information about the world down. And it is why the silencing chart in Section 4 shows negative bars for Helm and Archive: cutting Helm's own echo improves its accuracy in every audited seed.

6 · What memory is worth: give Archive a record and the world a cycle

The lab's original Archive had the weakest access to evidence and took most of its social input from Helm's broadcast: an echo of Helm, not a record, in a memoryless environment where the present says everything the past can. Under that design "memory is a drag" was guaranteed. An archive can help only if the past holds information about the future beyond what the present already gives (predictive information), and a memoryless world offers none. Here you can repair both defects: let the environment recur (a 60-round cycle with random phase slips, a season or a flood year) and let Archive keep a record (what the operators saw one cycle ago, read a little ahead). The control matters: the same record read a quarter-cycle out of phase separates the record's content from its mere presence as another voice.

Per round (÷1000). The record's worth should fall as the slips' entropy rate approaches the environment's own.

Discipline note: CAMS has been burned before by recombinations of its own quantities that redescribed value without adding predictive purchase. The Shannon layer earns its place only if bits predict something the raw scores do not, for instance, whether environment-conditioned upward transfer entropy forecasts post-shock correction across settings beyond what the gate weights alone already say. 8 · Turn a demonstration into a research question

Hypothesis

For the same starting institutional condition, stronger upward transmission of disconfirming operational evidence should improve adjustment after environmental change, although it need not increase agreement.

Proposed discriminating observation

A society can maintain high coherence while Helm remains wrong. If observed feedback quality predicts correction beyond CAMS node attributes, an informational layer might earn a place in the empirical model.

What could defeat the idea?

If varying the feedback gate does not reliably improve the post-shock decision fit over a sensible range of model assumptions; or if simpler baselines explain independent historical outcomes just as well, the proposed extension has not earned complexity.

Rival explanations

Selection by power, differences in observation quality, simple decentralisation, economic slack, or changes in the environment may explain outcomes without relying on the CAMS partition.

What this cannot establish: the model is a constructed mechanism demonstration. Its eight labels, network weights, noise distribution and update rule were chosen for exploration; no historical evidence calibrated them. A positive simulation result demonstrates conditional behaviour inside these assumptions, not empirical validation of CAMS or van der Leeuw and Dirks.

Transfer to a historical case

Choose one documented institutional surprise, then build a timestamped evidence chain: (1) who first observed the problem; (2) what was transmitted, to whom, and when; (3) what organisational filters changed the message; (4) whether a policy or practice was corrected; and (5) the independently assessed consequence. Code these records before interpreting them through CAMS.

Compare a model with ordinary resources and institutional attributes against one adding independently observed direction, fidelity, delay and decision impact of information flows. Keep some cases out of model development for a genuine test. Compare against non-CAMS functional partitions as a negative control.

9 · Methods and limitations

Open the mechanism (auditable, deliberately simple)

There are eight functional labels: Helm, Shield, Lore, Stewards, Craft, Hands, Archive and Flow. Each has a numerical belief bᵢ between −1 and +1. Each round, it receives a noisy observation of the same environmental state with function-specific access to evidence.

Every node updates from (a) direct evidence and (b) a weighted mean of incoming neighbours' prior beliefs. The direct evidence step is dᵢ = bᵢ + 0.55 × evidence × rᵢ × (signalᵢ − bᵢ), where rᵢ is an illustrative observation-access factor. The next belief is bᵢ′ = 0.62 × dᵢ + 0.38 × mean(incoming beliefs), capped to [−1,+1].

Both societies receive identical observations generated from the same seed. Directed links include top-down Helm broadcasts and operational reports from Hands, Craft and Flow. The upward feedback slider varies the weights of their messages to Helm and Lore. Helm influence changes the weight of outgoing Helm messages. All other weights remain fixed. There is no fitted model or scoring calibration; the Shannon layer computes entropies from the simulated belief streams but does not alter the dynamics.

Coherence = 1 − (max belief − min belief)/2. Decision fit = 1 − |Helm belief − true state|/2. Post-shock fit is the average decision fit after the shock. Reorientation round is the first post-shock round with a negative Helm belief, if any. Thresholds are descriptive, not validated.

For stochastic sensitivity, the 100-seed check repeats the entire comparison with seeds 1–100, keeping all shared inputs constant between A and B within each seed. It reports descriptive means and the fraction in which B has better post-shock decision fit.

What would a next-generation model need?

Explicit uncertainty distributions rather than scalar beliefs; independently observed directed interactions; multiple competing information types; signal distortion and strategic manipulation; changes in topology over time; social costs of dissent; costly sensing and implementation; richer Archive records than the one-cycle calendar added in v2.2; nonlinear coupling that could generate synergy rather than only copy redundancy; heterogeneity within nodes; more realistic policies and externally defined outcome measures. The selection of eight functions itself needs comparison with rival partitions and continuous models. On the Shannon side: continuous-valued entropy estimators (k-nearest-neighbour rather than quantised plug-in), longer lags, and an explicit compression step so that Abstraction has something to be.
