02 · The deal, plainly

What a cookie is — and is not

The fortune rides on top. The evidence rides at the foot.

Every number in the ledger carries a tier and a dated fact. The read is what one model produced under one instrument on one day — checkable, because the window closed in 2010. A reading you can argue with is the point.

Structure, not virtue.

The lens judges how a nation was organised, not whether it was good. A high Stress score means pressure, not failure. Identical standards apply to every nation on the list — Washington, London, Beijing and Moscow alike.

NA is an honest answer.

Where the model cannot name evidence, it must say NA — and where any vital is NA, that paladin's Zeitgeist number is declared uncomputable rather than guessed. The cookie says so.

Want the recent years?

This lens stops at 2010, where the record is settled and the machines' memories run deep. If you want the present, the scripts are public — modify them, run the protocol, and you are doing CAMS research. The ceiling of this tool is the floor of the programme.
