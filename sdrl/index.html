The Soft Machine

A soft machine, a trireme without a hull; eight toiling prisoners of a chain gang.

Eight thermodynamic requirements — a mesh of distributed functions, not a pattern in the sky. No hull carries the rowers: each function toils under its neighbours’ stress, the machine holds together or flies apart, and every deficit is partly outside any single function’s control.
