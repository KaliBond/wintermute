HOW TO READ THE INSTRUMENT

Eight nodes, one value.

A society here is not a mood and not a morality. It is eight institutions, each scored for coherence, capacity, stress and abstraction. The atlas shows the JUNO panel with the names on. Blind Prophet covers the names and asks you to call the break.

V = C + K − S + ½A

C is coherence, K capacity, S stress, A abstraction. V is that node’s value. V̄ is the mean of the eight. Vmin is the worst of them. Under 4, the year is strained. Through zero, it is severe.

Bonds

A bond is strong when both nodes are coherent and abstract, and it weakens as either one is stressed.

h = (0.6C + 0.4A) / 10

Bᵢⱼ = √(hᵢ hⱼ) · 2^(−(Sᵢ+Sⱼ)/10)

  • B̄ is the mean of the 28 unique bonds.
  • λ₂ is the algebraic connectivity of that graph. Low, and the society can come apart.
  • η is the thinner of Lore’s and Archive’s bond strength, divided by how stressed Hands are. That is the memory floor.
  • ω is how unevenly the eight nodes moved on this step.

Signatures

  • Executive decoupling. Helm’s value is under 6 and its bond to Archive is thin.
  • Praetorian condition. Shield outvalues both Helm and the Stewards. A high V̄ in that state is not health.
  • Mythic-material decoupling. Lore is carrying the system while Hands have failed.
  • Thermodynamic freeze. Mean value is at or below zero and the memory floor has collapsed.

Four shapes

Freeze / fracture collapse
Coordination value falls to zero or below. The library attractor folds.
Chronic cyclic instability
Systemic crises repeat. Recovery never regains a high ceiling.
High-coherence mobilisation
Strong bonds take the shock. A sharp fall, then fast reconstitution.
Absorptive buffer
Shocks are damped. No systemic onset inside the window.

Calling a crisis

In Blind Prophet the years are an index, not a date. Two calls. A call one to five samples before an onset is a hit, worth 60 plus 25 for each year of lead, capped at 160. Inside five samples after, it is late, worth 40. Anything else is a false call, minus 50. Guessing the society is 100. Guessing the shape is another 100. If the window never onset and you never called, the discipline bonus is 120.
