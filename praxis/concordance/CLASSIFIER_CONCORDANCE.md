# Classifier concordance: JUNO v1.2-Final (canonical) versus v1.0.1 (legacy comparator)

Generated by `praxis/concordance/build_concordance.py`. Do not edit by hand; re-run the script.

## What this report is

Both classifiers were applied afresh to the stored raw scores of every in-range society-year in `data/v1.0_final_recompute/`. The two are separate instruments. This report counts where their labels coincide and where they do not. It does not translate one label set into the other, and it changes no stored output and no lodged prediction.

It says nothing about which classifier is right. Neither has been validated against independently scored outcomes. Agreement here is agreement between two rule sets on the same model-scored inputs.

## Why they disagree

The two cascades differ in four places (exact rules in `praxis/CLASSIFIER_SPECIFICATIONS.md`):

1. **Phantom Type II.** v1.2-Final fires on `s_min < −0.65` alone, after Systemic Crisis. v1.0.1 needs `3 ≤ V̄ ≤ 6 AND V_min < 0 AND s_min < −0.7`, and tests it before Systemic Crisis.
2. **Local Node Failure.** v1.2-Final: `V_min < 4.0`. v1.0.1: `V_min < 4.5 OR s_min < −0.75`.
3. **Stable Adaptive.** v1.2-Final needs four conditions (`V̄ > 10, V_min > 5.0, B̄ > 0.28, s_min > −0.25`). v1.0.1 needs two (`V̄ > 10, B̄ > 0.25`).
4. **Rounding.** 6 decimal places against 4.

## Limits

- All inputs are model-scored. Concordance measures rule-set agreement, not accuracy.
- The 6,778 rows include repeated society-years from different scoring runs. Rates on all rows weight heavily re-scored societies more.
- Ukraine's series is out of the 1–10 range and is therefore outside the 6,778. Its row above is computed for information only.
- v1.2-Final thresholds were chosen by inspection of a 3,002 society-year blind corpus (`juno/JUNO_v1.2-Final_Formalism.md` section 6). The provenance of the v1.0.1 thresholds is not documented in this repository.
