Same columns as the JUNO datasets and the JUNO Calculator. Node Value and Bond Strength use the Calculator’s JUNO v1.2-Final formulas: V = C + K − S + 0.5·A; bond strength is the mean of √(qiqj)·2−(Si+Sj)/10 over the other seven nodes, with q = (0.6·C + 0.4·A)/10.


        
        
Wide CSV (one line)

        
Society, year, the 32 raw values (Helm_C, Helm_K, Helm_S, Helm_A, then Shield and onward), model, session code and date.

        

        
        
One reply is one reading by one scorer. The site’s panels use ensembles of independent passes, so treat a single score as a hypothesis: run the same prompt in two or three different models and compare. Read the result as stress levels and coupling across the eight nodes, not as a grade. Nothing is sent anywhere; to contribute to the public cohort, use the Citizen Scoring Observatory.
