# CAMS lean node definitions (tester packet)

Eight functions only. Use these meanings when reading Cog, Aff and sigma. Do not invent extra traits, Big Five labels, crisis typologies, or Juno language.

## Helm
Executive direction and political authorship: the capacity to set goals, decide, and hold a centre of command for the entity.

## Lore
Shared explanation and public speech: narrative, legitimacy, teaching, and the stories an entity tells about itself and its world.

## Craft
Making and technical invention: productive technique, design skill, and the organised capacity to build or transform things.

## Hands
Everyday work and labour: the practical workforce that executes ordinary tasks at scale.

## Flow
Circulation and exchange: markets, mobility, logistics, cash and goods moving through the entity and its environment.

## Stewards
Provisioning and care: maintenance, welfare, tenancy, long-horizon upkeep, and the organised tending of people and assets.

## Shield
Protection and security: defence, policing, border and coercive capacity. Read relationally (see algebra_and_rules.md): strong neighbours make Shield look like effective protection; weak or split neighbours make it look like coercion by default or protective custody.

## Archive
Record and procedure: memory, documentation, bureaucracy, and institutional continuity through rules and files.

## Notes for testers
- A complete portrait does not require every node to lead. Persistent weakness is often what makes a period intelligible.
- Leadership may come from genuine strength, or by default because other nodes are weak or split.
- Keep pocket language functional (what the nodes are doing together). Avoid mood adjectives that are not grounded in the sign pattern of Aff and sigma.
