# Commentary — Entity_A (Centrelink) and Entity_B (ATO) Series, with Extensions

**Separation of record:** the CSVs are the score record; this note is commentary. The protocol note holds the blinding disclosure, the addendum hash, and the unmasking key. Scores follow CAMS Universal v1.4; each quarter reflects the evidenced condition at the quarter's end.

## Entity_A (Centrelink) — Stewards, agency resolution

**2013 (5,5,4,4) flat.** DHS/Centrelink delivering payment services within a constrained but functioning system; APS efficiency dividends beginning to bite. No scored events.

**2014 (5,5,4,4)→(5,5,5,4)→(5,5,5,4)→(5,5,4,4).** Commission of Audit and budget-tightening rhetoric place the agency's mission under political pressure, but recipient-facing functioning unchanged. S briefly 5 mid-year on welfare-policy uncertainty.

**2015 (5,5,4,4),(5,5,5,4),(4,4,5,5),(4,4,5,5).** E1 (12 May 2015 budget) announces the integrity measure; E2 (1 July 2015) interim manual process applies income averaging at 5× previous volume. K drops to 4 from 2015Q3 and stays: the first scored deterioration, one quarter after the executive policy change. A rises to 5 — proxy-based assessment displacing individual verification. This is the Q01 test window.

**2016 (4,4,5,5),(4,4,5,5),(4,4,6,6),(4,3,7,6).** OCI launch (E3, July limited, September full). Volume multiplies ~40×; recipient-facing harm scales with it. 2016Q4: media surge, ministerial announcement, complaint flood (E4). K to 3 at Q4; S to 7.

**2017 (4,3,7,6),(3,3,7,6),(3,3,6,6),(3,3,6,6).** Call-centre failure peaks over the summer (28m busy signals in six months; 42m FY to April, 55m by October — contemporaneous figures). W1 (Ombudsman, 19 April), W2 (Senate report, 21 June), W3 (Carney AAT judgments). C drops to 3 at Q2: the agency's legal method is now publicly contested and it continues regardless. K stays 3 — the year's modest S easing reflects procedural patches (EIC, review letters to 114,653 recipients), not correction.

**2018 (3,3,6,6)→(4,3,6,6).** CUPI rollout changes the interface, not the method. Debts continue at scale; AAT losses accumulate inside the agency. K holds at 3.

**2019 (4,3,6,6)×3,(4,4,5,5).** W4 (first successful court challenge, February). W5 (19 November): income-averaged raising ceases. K recovers to 4 at Q4 — the first genuine uptick since 2015 — as the unlawful mechanism stops.

**2020 (5,4,7,5),(5,3,9,4),(5,4,7,4),(5,4,6,4).** C1: 1.3m JobSeeker claims in 55 days, myGov records, queues of a kind never measured. S peaks at 9 (2020Q2) — the agency's highest stress in the series — yet C rises to 5: mission coherence restored by the COVID response (supplement simplified processing, debts paused). W6 (May unlawfulness accepted; July refunds commence) lifts K partially; refund execution keeps S elevated at 7 through Q3.

**2021 (5,4,5,4),(5,4,5,4),(5,5,5,5),(5,5,5,5).** W7 (June settlement). K back to 5 from Q3 — the first baseline recovery since 2014. Compliance activity resuming under lawful methods.

**2022–2023.** Normalisation at K5. W8 (August 2022, RC established): C 4, S 6 at Q3 — renewed institutional threat, agency now subject of inquiry rather than author of policy. W9 (July 2023 report): S 6 at Q3; A holds 5 — institutional learning finally encoded in reform commitments. Q4 2023: stabilising.

**Trajectory:** K 5 (2013–14) → 4 (2015Q3) → 3 (2016Q4) → 3 (held to 2019Q3) → 4 (2019Q4) → 3 (COVID trough) → 5 (2021Q3). A mirror image of the executive timeline in the frozen addendum.

## Entity_B (ATO) — Archive, agency resolution (control)

**2013 (5,5,4,4).** Stable custodian of the fiscal record.
**2014.** Budget cuts reduce K to 4 from Q3 (sustained under-resourcing through 2015). A rises to 5 with digital-channel expansion (myTax) — abstraction increasing while service holds, the benign abstraction profile the register distinguishes from Entity_A's.
**2016.** K back to 5; STP build-out.
**2017 (5,5,4,5),(4,4,6,5),(4,4,6,5),(5,5,5,5).** B1: garnishee-notice treatment of small-business debts (April) — a genuine conduct failure, contained: two quarters at C4/K4/S6, resolved by Q4. Plutus fraud scandal (May) adds S. Contrast with Entity_A: a two-quarter dip against a four-year custody failure.
**2018–2019.** Stable at (5,5,4,5).
**2020 (5,5,6,5),(6,6,8,5),(6,6,8,5),(5,6,6,5).** B2: under the identical COVID shock that drove Entity_A to K3/S9, the ATO administers JobKeeper and early-super release at K6 with 97% of payments inside four business days. The control agency's capacity rose under the load Entity_A buckled under — the Q05 contrast in one year.
**2021–2023.** Normalisation at (5,5,5,5). Global minimum K over the whole panel: 4.

## Helm and Archive extensions 2013Q1–2017Q4

**Helm.** 2013Q2–Q3 score the Rudd return and election campaign (C4, S6); 2013Q4 the Abbott government's orderly start (OSB). 2014: Hockey budget (Q2) and Senate blockage (Q3, C3) — coherence trough. 2015Q1 spill motion (C3); 2015Q3 Turnbull (S6); 2015Q4 recovery. 2016: election (Q2–Q3), then OCI defensive handling (Q4, S6). 2017: citizenship crisis and High Court judgment (Q3–Q4, C3 at Q4). Join to 2018Q1 (5,5,6,4) is continuous — 2018's spill and robodebt-year stress then follow in the existing panel.

**Archive (national).** 2014Q2 OAIC funding cut (K4, held through 2017); 2015 metadata retention; recovery begins 2016 (C5). Joins 2018Q1 (5,5,5,4) cleanly. The national Archive custody failure scored in the 2020–22 panel is a distinct, later phenomenon (Tune review era), which matters for the Q03 verdict.

## Reading the two series together

Entity_A's K path is the register's "agency signature": a two-step fall beginning exactly at the executive policy change, a four-year floor, recovery at cessation, a shock trough, and full repair only after the 2021 settlement. Entity_B's path is the control: contained 2017 conduct dip, capacity rising under COVID load, never below K4 in eleven years. The asymmetry is scored evidence for constituency-conditional state capacity (HELM-X1) at agency resolution, not inference from national aggregates.
