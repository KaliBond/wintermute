## Baseline end-2020 (sources dated ≤ 2021-06-30)

### Board
- CATL's 2020 annual report results presentation states 2020 revenue of CNY 50.32 billion and net profit attributable to shareholders of CNY 5.58 billion (+22.43% YoY); it describes CATL as No. 1 globally in EV battery consumption volume for four consecutive years. CATL (company publication), 10 June 2021. https://www.catl.com/en/uploads/1/file/public/202106/20210610141952_6c9o51xur0.pdf

### RD
- CATL announced a battery cell designed to last one million miles (≈1.6 million km), based on NMC chemistry co-developed for Tesla-class duty cycles; positioned as an energy-density and durability leadership claim before the EV volume boom. BBC News, 8 June 2020. https://www.bbc.com/news/business-52977216 (as archived; also Bloomberg, 7 June 2020, https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2020-06-07/a-million-mile-battery-from-china-could-power-your-electric-car)

- Same item as Board: 2020 revenue CNY 50.32 bn, net profit CNY 5.58 bn (+22.43%); power battery systems the dominant segment. CATL (company publication), 10 June 2021. https://www.catl.com/en/uploads/1/file/public/202106/20210610141952_6c9o51xur0.pdf

### Markets

## 2021 (sources dated ≤ 2022-06-30)

### Board
- Tesla became CATL's largest single customer in 2021 with confirmed transactions of CNY 13.04 bn (10% of CATL revenue), ahead of a broadened OEM base (Hyundai, Ford, Mercedes-Benz, Great Wall, Li Auto, NIO). Gasgoo (Shanghai), April 2022. https://autonews.gasgoo.com/new_energy/70020168.html

### Risk
- In July 2021 CATL sued rival CALB in the Fuzhou Intermediate People's Court over five patents (three invention, two utility model), claiming CNY 185 m damages and alleging CALB cells were installed in tens of thousands of vehicles; CALB filed invalidation counter-petitions. Pandaily, 22 July 2021. https://pandaily.com/tag/battery (item "CATL Sues CALB for Patent Infringement", 7/22/2021)

### RD
- CATL unveiled its first-generation sodium-ion battery (160 Wh/kg cell, 15-minute 80% charge, >90% retention at −20 °C, AB sodium+li-ion pack concept), the first major maker to launch Na-ion; commercialisation targeted from 2022. Reuters, 29 July 2021 (see also 3 August 2021 follow-up). https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/catls-new-sodium-ion-battery-help-ease-lithium-shortages-2021-08-03/ ; https://www.reuters.com/article/us-china-batteries-catl/chinas-catl-unveils-sodium-ion-battery-a-first-for-a-major-car-battery-maker-idUSKBN2EZ15D
- 2021 R&D expense reached CNY 7.69 bn (+115.5% YoY, 5.9% of revenue), directed at sodium-ion, the AB battery system, third-generation CTP and non-thermal-diffusion technology. Gasgoo, April 2022. https://autonews.gasgoo.com/new_energy/70020168.html

### Finance
- 2021 results: revenue CNY 130.36 bn (+159.06%); net profit attributable to shareholders CNY 15.93 bn (+185.34%); operating cash flow CNY 42.91 bn; power battery gross margin 22.0% (−4.56 pp on raw-material costs); lithium battery sales 133.41 GWh (+184.8%). EqualOcean, 25 April 2022. https://equalocean.com/news/2022042517401 ; Gasgoo, April 2022. https://autonews.gasgoo.com/new_energy/70020168.html
- Q1 2022 strain indicator: net profit fell 23.6% YoY to CNY 1.49 bn on lithium cost surge despite revenue growth — margin pressure carried from late 2021. Forbes, 1 May 2022. https://www.forbes.com/sites/russellflannery/2022/05/01/profit-falls-at-worlds-largest-ev-battery-maker-catl-even-as-sales-soar-136-in-first-quarter/

### Operations
- SNE Research: CATL installed 96.7 GWh in 2021 (+167%), global share 32.6% (up from 24.6% in 2020), No. 1 for a fifth straight year; global market doubled to 296.8 GWh. TechNode, 8 February 2022. https://technode.com/2022/02/08/catl-the-biggest-ev-battery-maker-in-the-world-in-2021-with-32-6-of-market-share/

### Workforce
- Engineering-base growth proxy: R&D spending +115.5% to CNY 7.69 bn and named next-generation programmes (sodium-ion, AB system, CTP 3.0) indicate rapid technical headcount build-out; no public attrition or headcount figure found dated within this window. Gasgoo, April 2022. https://autonews.gasgoo.com/new_energy/70020168.html — EVIDENCE PARTIAL on attrition/headcount specifics.

### Brand

### Markets

### Risk
- CALB dispute partial reversal: in February 2023 the Fuzhou court ordered CALB to pay CNY 57.1 m and cease selling affected products; on 3 August 2023 China's NIPA invalidated two of CATL's five asserted patents, undercutting parts of the case while a separate "busbars" ruling had gone CATL's way.
