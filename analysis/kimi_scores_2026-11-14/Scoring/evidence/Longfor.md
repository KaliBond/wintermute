# Longfor Group Holdings Limited (00960.HK) — CAMS Corporate Evidence File

**Entity:** Longfor Group Holdings Ltd, HK-listed Chinese property developer (founded Chongqing 1993; founders Wu Yajun and ex-husband Cai Kui, ~43%/23% stakes respectively at end-2024). Segments: property development; investment properties (Paradise Walk / 天街 malls, Goyoo 冠寓 rental apartments); property management and smart construction (Longfor Intelligent Living 龙湖智创生活; Longfor Longzhizao 龙湖龙智造).

**Functional condition dates:** 31 December 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, plus baseline end-2020.

**Date discipline applied:** evidence for year Y published on or before 30 June Y+1 (2021 → ≤2022-06-30; 2022 → ≤2023-06-30; 2023 → ≤2024-06-30; 2024 → ≤2025-06-30; baseline end-2020 → ≤2021-06-30). Dated sources only; outlet, date and URL recorded per item. Post-cutoff items discarded and logged at end of file. No C/K/S/A scoring content in this file.

**Terminology note for panel:** "C2/C3 bonds" in the panel brief maps to the PBOC/NAFR support-mechanism instruments: the "second arrow" (第二支箭) CBIC-guaranteed mid-term notes and the credit-protection-tool corporate bonds of 2022; items tagged accordingly below.
