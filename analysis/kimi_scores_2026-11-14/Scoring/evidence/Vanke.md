# Vanke Co., Ltd. (000002.SZ / 02202.HK) — CAMS Evidence Record

Evidence-gathering record for corporate scoring panel. NO SCORES. Functional condition at 31 December of each year 2021–2024, plus baseline end-2020.

Date discipline: evidence for year Y published/dated on or before 30 June of Y+1. Dated sources only; outlet + date recorded per item. Post-cutoff items discarded (see end). Tags: **[P]** functional performance, **[S]** strain/failure/limitation, **[M]** mechanism linking to node function.

Monetary figures in RMB unless noted. "Onewo" = Vanke's property services arm (02602.HK, listed 29 Sep 2022). Auditor throughout was KPMG Huazhen (毕马威华振) — NOT PwC; KPMG gave standard unqualified opinions on FY2021, FY2022 and FY2024; switch to Deloitte announced 29 Apr 2025 for FY2025.
