# Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. — CAMS Evidence Dossier

Entity: Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd. (private, employee-owned via trade union + Ren Zhengfei). Scope: functional condition at 31 December 2020 (baseline), 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. Cutoff discipline: evidence for year Y dated on or before 30 June Y+1. Dated sources only; outlet + date + URL recorded per item. No scoring content.

---

## Baseline end-2020

(all sources dated ≤ 2021-06-30)

### Board
- Rotating-chair system (est. 2018) had Guo Ping, Xu Zhijun (Eric Xu) and Hu Houkun (Ken Hu) as the three rotating chairmen; Ren Zhengfei remained founder/CEO and board member. Huawei 2021-dated coverage confirms this was the pre-2022 composition. (Pandaily, 2022-01-16, https://pandaily.com/huaweis-2022-dividends-exceed-employee-expectations)

### Risk
- 15 May 2019: BIS added Huawei and 68 non-U.S. affiliates to the Entity List (May 2019 listing; licence requirement for all EAR items, presumption of denial). (Federal Register final rule, 2020-08-20, https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2020/08/20/2020-18213/addition-of-huawei-non-us-affiliates-to-the-entity-list-the-removal-of-temporary-general-license-and)
- 17 Aug 2020: BIS expanded the Foreign-Produced Direct Product rule against Huawei, added 38 further non-U.S. affiliates (incl. Huawei Cloud entities, Toga Networks) and let the Temporary General License expire — cutting Huawei off from foreign chips made with U.S. technology. (U.S. Commerce Dept press release, 2020-08-17, https://2017-2021.commerce.gov/news/press-releases/2020/08/commerce-department-further-restricts-huawei-access-us-technology-and.html)
- 3 Dec 2020: BIS issued updated Huawei Entity List FAQs (public guidance on the licensing posture, incl. 5G-related items). (BIS, 2020-12-03, https://www.bis.gov/media/documents/dec-3-2020-update-huawei-entity-listing-faqs-posting.pdf)

### RD
- HarmonyOS on smartphones announced for 2021; HMS Core had 96,000 apps, up from 81,000 in July 2020 — response to loss of Google services. (TechCrunch, 2020-09-10, https://techcrunch.com/2020/09/10/huawei-debut-harmonyos-android-alternative-phones-2021/)

### Operations / Markets
- Honor smartphone unit sale to a Shenzhen-led consortium announced 17 Nov 2020 to preserve supply-chain access; by mid-2021 Huawei had shipped just 15M smartphones in Q1 2021 vs 66.8M in Q3 2019 (Counterpoint data cited). (Wired, 2021-06-05, https://www.wired.com/story/huawei-harmony-os-20-tablets-watches/)

### Workforce
- Ren Zhengfei (27 Oct 2020 speech): 2021–22 "most challenging two years for survival"; graduate recruitment to expand to ≥8,000 in 2021 plus extra billions in R&D funding; "Genius Youth" programme paying ~2M yuan salaries. (EEWorld translation, 2020-11-01, https://en.eeworld.com.cn/mp/s/a98400.jspx)

## 2021

(cutoff ≤ 2022-06-30)

### Board
- At the 28 Mar 2022 annual-report event, Meng Wanzhou (CFO, returned from detention Sept 2021) presented strategy — heavy R&D and talent investment — while rotating chair Guo Ping announced 10,000+ graduate hires for 2022; Meng was named to the three-person rotating-chair rotation (replacing Guo Ping, who moved to chair the supervisory board), her first on-duty term to begin 1 Apr 2023. (Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance, 2022-03-28, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/huawei-profit-surges-deals-revamp-083500740.html; Pandaily, 2022-03-28, https://pandaily.com/meng-wanzhou-to-serve-as-rotating-chairwoman-of-huawei)

### Risk
- 24 Sept 2021: Meng Wanzhou released to China after a U.S. Deferred Prosecution Agreement in which she admitted the "statement of facts" on misleading HSBC about Huawei-Iran dealings; the underlying U.S. criminal case against Huawei itself continued. (American Journal of International Law, vol. 116, Jan 2022, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-journal-of-international-law/article/huaweis-meng-wanzhou-released-to-china-after-entering-into-deferred-prosecution-agreement-with-us-justice-department/2D1E2F6D6278467B65A4BCB0DF109CE8)
- 17 June 2021: FCC voted to advance a proposed ban on authorising Huawei/ZTE equipment (implementing what became the Secure Equipment Act, signed 11 Nov 2021). (Reuters, 2021-06-17, https://www.reuters.com/technology/us-fcc-votes-launch-further-crackdown-huawei-zte-equipment-2021-06-17/)

### RD
- HarmonyOS 2 launched for smartphones 2 June 2021; target 300M devices by end-2021 (200M Huawei devices). (China Daily/Xinhua, 2021-06-03, https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202106/03/WS60b82bb7a31024ad0bac3464.html)
- By 11 June 2021: 134,000 apps on HMS Core, 4M+ developers signed on. (GSMArena, 2021-06-11, https://www.gsmarena.com/huaweis_harmonyos_already_has_134000_apps_over_4_million_developers_have_signed_on-news-49552.php)
- R&D spend 2021: CNY 142.7bn, 22.4% of revenue; statements audited by KPMG. (Fierce Network, 2022-03-28, https://www.fierce-network.com/wireless/huawei-revenue-drops-profits-jump-2021)

### Finance
- 2021 annual report (released 28 Mar 2022): revenue CNY 636.8bn (−28.6% YoY, first annual decline), net profit CNY 113.7bn (incl. ~CNY 57.4bn disposal gains from Honor and x86 server sales); cash and short-term investments CNY 416.3bn (+16.5%); China revenue CNY 413.2bn. (Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance, 2022-03-28, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/huawei-profit-surges-deals-revamp-083500740.html; Pandaily, 2022-03-28, https://pandaily.com/huawei-holds-2021-annual-report-in-shenzhen-recording-revenue-of-100b)

### Operations
- Sold its x86 server business to a Chinese consortium in 2021 after blacklisting blocked Intel processor supply. (Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance, 2022-03-28, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/huawei-profit-surges-deals-revamp-083500740.html)
- German subsidiary audited accounts (KPMG, signed 30–31 Mar 2022): 2021 revenue −26.3% to €1,390.1M; consumer segment −68.7% while enterprise +32.5% and carrier −1.0% — showing equipment lines holding while handsets collapsed. (Huawei Technologies Deutschland GmbH annual financial statements, 2022-03-30, https://www.bayern.landtag.de/fileadmin/lobbyregister/DEBYLT0218/Huawei%20Jahresabschluss.pdf)

### Workforce
- Plan to hire 10,000+ college graduates in 2022 announced at the March 2022 report event (continuing the 2020–21 talent push). (Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance, 2022-03-28, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/huawei-profit-surges-deals-revamp-083500740.html)
- ESOP: 131,507 participants as of 31 Dec 2021, all current or retired employees; Huawei "100% owned by employees" via trade union + Ren Zhengfei. (Pandaily, 2022-01-16, https://pandaily.com/huaweis-2022-dividends-exceed-employee-expectations)

### Brand
- Meng Wanzhou's return greeted as a national vindication in China ("national hero" coverage), a brand-strengthening event domestically. (Global Times, 2021-09-25, via compilation at https://rightways.wordpress.com/tag/laws/)
- HarmonyOS's global potential "severely limited" outside China without Google services, per IDC's Bryan Ma. (CNET, 2021-06-03, https://www.cnet.com/tech/mobile/huaweis-answer-to-googles-android-harmonyos-is-coming-to-smartphones/)

### Markets
- China smartphone share fell to ~16% in Jan 2021 (from 41% Q1 2020); Oppo became #1 in China for the first time; Huawei rationed scarce Kirin/5G parts for premium low-volume models. (Counterpoint via Engadget, 2021-03-05, https://www.engadget.com/huawei-market-share-decline-helps-oppo-153318473.html)
- Full-year 2021 China: Vivo 22%, Oppo 21%, Apple 16%, Xiaomi 15%, Honor 10%, Huawei 10%; Huawei shipments −68% YoY. (Counterpoint via TMTPost, 2022-01-26, https://en.tmtpost.com/post/5994648)
- Q4 2021 China: Apple #1 (21.7%); Honor #2 (16.7%) — the divested Huawei brand outsold Huawei itself. (Counterpoint via China Daily, 2022-02-19, https://global.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202202/19/WS62103bb6a310cdd39bc87a61.html)

## 2022

(cutoff ≤ 2023-06-30)

### Board
- Ken Hu (Hu Houkun) served as on-duty rotating chair 1 Apr–30 Sep 2022, Eric Xu 1 Oct 2022–31 Mar 2023; Meng Wanzhou confirmed for 1 Apr–30 Sep 2023 at the 31 Mar 2023 board statement. Ren Zhengfei set the "survival" policy direction (see below). (AFP via SpaceDaily, 2023-03-31, https://spacedaily.com/meng-wanzhou-huaweis-princess-claims-her-crown-999/)

### Risk
- 7 Oct 2022: BIS imposed sweeping controls on advanced computing chips, supercomputing end-uses, SME for ≤16/14nm logic and "U.S. persons" support for Chinese advanced fabs — raising the ceiling of restrictions surrounding Huawei's supply chain. (BIS press release, 2022-10-07, https://www.bis.doc.gov/index.php/documents/about-bis/newsroom/press-releases/3158-2022-10-07-bis-press-release-advanced-computing-and-semiconductor-manufacturing-controls-final/file)
- 25 Nov 2022: FCC banned authorisation of new Huawei/ZTE equipment sales in the U.S. under the Secure Equipment Act — first such equipment-authorisation ban in FCC history. (Reuters, 2022-11-25, https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/us-fcc-bans-equipment-sales-imports-zte-huawei-over-national-security-risk-2022-11-25/)

### RD
- TechInsights teardown (20 Jul 2022) found SMIC's 7nm (N+1) process in the MinerVa bitcoin-miner ASIC — evidence a sanctioned Chinese fab could produce 7nm-class chips with DUV multi-patterning, the precursor to the Kirin comeback. (TechInsights, 2022-07-20, https://www.techinsights.com/blog/disruptive-technology-7nm-smic-minerva-bitcoin-miner; Bloomberg, 2022-07-21, https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-07-21/china-s-top-chipmaker-makes-big-tech-advances-despite-us-curbs)
- R&D spend 2022: CNY 161.5bn, 25.1% of revenue — highest ratio in company history to that point. (Huawei 2022 annual report event, AP News, 2023-03-31, https://apnews.com/article/huawei-meng-wanzhou-eric-xu-d786d6cf7cc3aabd01bdb545323eb7b4)
- HarmonyOS installed on 320M devices by end-2022 (+113% YoY); Mate 50 returned with BeiDou satellite messaging. (Eric Xu New Year message via Pandaily, 2022-12-31, https://pandaily.com/huawei-expects-92b-in-revenue-in-2022-same-as-the-previous-year)

### Finance
- 2022 annual report (released 31 Mar 2023): revenue CNY 642.3bn (+0.9%); net profit CNY 35.6bn (−68.7%, lowest in a decade); operating cash flow CNY 17.8bn (−70.2%); net cash CNY 176.3bn (−26.9%); liability ratio 58.9%. Meng Wanzhou: results "in line with forecast despite substantial pressures"; profit fall driven by terminal decline, commodity costs, R&D rise. (AP News, 2023-03-31, https://apnews.com/article/huawei-meng-wanzhou-eric-xu-d786d6cf7cc3aabd01bdb545323eb7b4; TMTPost, 2023-04-01, https://www.tmtpost.com/6472698.html?rss=jinrongjie)
- Segment revenue 2022: carrier CNY 284bn, enterprise CNY 133.2bn, consumer CNY 214.5bn. (TechFinancials, 2023-03-31, https://techfinancials.co.za/2023/03/31/ceos-daughter-meng-wanzhou-appointed-huawei-rotation-chair-as-company-reports-stable-business-in-2022/)
- H1 2022: revenue CNY 301.6bn (−5.9%); carrier +4.2%, enterprise +27.5%, terminal −25.4%; net margin 5.0%. (TMTPost, 2022-08-24, https://www.tmtpost.com/6230693.html)

### Operations
- Mate 50 series launched 6 Sept 2022 with Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 restricted to 4G only (no 5G) — flagships continued on hampered silicon; satellite texting via BeiDou. (PhoneArena, 2022-09-07, https://www.phonearena.com/news/huawei-announces-satellite-connectivity_id142360)
- Intelligent-vehicle business: ~US$3bn cumulative investment, 7,000+ R&D staff, but only 0.3% of 2022 revenue — heavy cost, minimal return. (Pandaily, 2022-12-31, https://pandaily.com/huawei-expects-92b-in-revenue-in-2022-same-as-the-previous-year)
- Ericsson took 39% of RAN revenue outside China in 1H 2022 (Dell'Oro) — Huawei structurally excluded from Western carrier buildouts. (ComSoc Technology Blog, 2022-08-19, https://techblog.comsoc.org/2022/08/19/delloro-ran-market-disappoints-in-2q-2022/)

### Workforce
- Ren Zhengfei internal article (22 Aug 2022): shift "from the pursuit of scale to the pursuit of profits and cash flow"; "survival as the main principle" for 2023–25; shrink/close marginal businesses; "the chill will be felt by everyone"; surplus personnel to reserve teams. (Reuters via The Business Standard, 2022-08-23, https://www.tbsnews.net/tech/founder-chinas-huawei-urges-focus-cash-flow-survival-downturn-482470; China Daily, 2022-08-24, https://www.chinadailyhk.com/article/287047)
- 2022 ESOP dividend raised to CNY 1.61/share (from 1.58) — internal yield ~20.5% on virtual restricted shares; employees said it "exceeded expectations" in a hard year. (Pandaily, 2023-01-16, https://pandaily.com/huaweis-2022-dividends-exceed-employee-expectations)

### Brand
- Eric Xu (31 Dec 2022): "In 2022 we successfully pulled ourselves out of crisis mode. US restrictions are now our new normal, and we're back to business as usual." (Pandaily, 2022-12-31, https://pandaily.com/huawei-expects-92b-in-revenue-in-2022-same-as-the-previous-year)
- U.S. and allied equipment bans (FCC Nov 2022; earlier UK/Canada/Nordic exclusions) cemented the brand's exclusion from Western infrastructure markets. (Reuters, 2022-11-25, URL above)

### Markets
- 2022 China smartphone share: Q4 2022 Huawei 9% (Counterpoint quarterly series) — stabilising after the 2021 collapse. (Counterpoint China share series, captured 2024-06, https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/china-smartphone-share)
- Huawei forecast flat 2022 revenue (~CNY 636.9bn) in Xu's New Year speech; device decline "abated", digital power and cloud growing fast. (Pandaily, 2022-12-31, https://pandaily.com/huawei-expects-92b-in-revenue-in-2022-same-as-the-previous-year)

## 2023

(cutoff ≤ 2024-06-30)

### Board
- Board re-elected 27–28 Mar 2023; Meng Wanzhou on-duty rotating chair 1 Apr–30 Sep 2023, Ken Hu 1 Oct 2023–31 Mar 2024, Eric Xu from 1 Apr 2024. (AFP via SpaceDaily, 2023-03-31, https://spacedaily.com/meng-wanzhou-huaweis-princess-claims-her-crown-999/; board list via handsetcat, 2023-08-23, https://www.handsetcat.com/news/36526.html)

### Risk
- After the Mate 60 Pro launch, the U.S. Commerce Department opened an investigation into whether the Kirin 9000s violated export controls; NSA adviser Sullivan said the U.S. "needs more information"; congressional hawks demanded a total cut-off of technology exports to Huawei and SMIC. (CNBC Indonesia compilation of Reuters/Bloomberg reporting, 2023-09-14, https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/tech/20230914104015-37-472381/fakta-kirin-9000s-chip-hp-huawei-mate-60-pro-bikin-as-waswas)
- 19 Sept 2023: Commerce Secretary Raimondo said the U.S. had "no evidence" Huawei could produce advanced chips at scale. (Reuters, 2023-09-19, as compiled in https://www.alestiklal.net/en/article/mate-60-pro-this-is-how-huaweis-latest-smartphone-sparked-a-political-storm-in-the-u.s — see Discarded re page date)
- Huawei sued the Portuguese state (Sept 2023) after its 5G "ban", and later Romania (Apr 2024) — litigating its exclusion from EU markets. (Data Center Dynamics, 2023-09-07, https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/?tag=huawei&term=government-military)

### RD
- 29 Aug 2023: Mate 60 Pro went on sale unannounced during Commerce Secretary Raimondo's Beijing visit; TechInsights teardown (reported 4 Sept 2023) found HiSilicon Kirin 9000s fabricated by SMIC on a 7nm (N+2) DUV process — Huawei's first 5G SoC since 2020. (TechInsights, Sept 2023, https://www.techinsights.com/blog/techinsights-confirming-smic-n2-7nm-huawei-mate-60-pro; Reuters via wheresthe.ai, 2023-09-04, https://wheresthe.ai/d/cn-huawei-mate-60-pro-smic-7nm-2023-09/)
- Third-party benchmarking (Geekerwan, Sept 2023): Kirin 9000s performs near Qualcomm Snapdragon 888 — roughly two generations behind; first mobile CPU with hyperthreading; Huawei Maleoon 910 GPU. (Baiguan, 2023-09-22, https://www.baiguan.news/p/dancing-in-chains-huaweis-comeback)
- Dissenting technical view: Fomalhaut Techno Solutions told SCMP the Kirin 9000s was 14nm with double-patterning (minority claim contradicting TechInsights/Yole findings). (El Chapuzas Informático, 2023-10-02, https://elchapuzasinformatico.com/2023/10/huawei-kirin-9000s-14-nm-soc/)
- R&D spend 2023: CNY 164.7bn, 23.4% of revenue; decade total > CNY 1.11tn; KPMG audited. (Huawei press release, 2024-03-29, https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2024/3/huawei-annual-report-2023)
- Ascend 910B AI chip: CSET analysis (June 2024) documents 910B's improvements over 910 (2021) and notes U.S. controls likely hindering its production — Huawei's Nvidia A100-class substitute. (CSET, 2024-06-17, https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/pushing-the-limits-huaweis-ai-chip-tests-u-s-export-controls/)

### Finance
- 2023 annual report (released 29 Mar 2024): revenue CNY 704.2bn (+9.6%, largest growth since 2019); net profit CNY 87.0bn (+144.5%); R&D CNY 164.7bn. (Huawei press release, 2024-03-29, https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2024/3/huawei-annual-report-2023; TechNode, 2024-04-01, https://technode.com/2024/04/01/huaweis-2023-global-sales-revenue-hits-nearly-98-billion-dollars-up-by-9-63-y-o-y/)
- Segments 2023: ICT infrastructure CNY 362bn (+2.3%); terminal CNY 251.5bn (+17.3%); cloud CNY 55.3bn (+21.9%); digital power CNY 52.6bn (+3.5%); intelligent automotive CNY 4.7bn (+128.1%). (C114, 2024-03-30, http://en.c114.com.cn/577/a1258692.html)

### Operations
- Mate 60 series supply constrained by Kirin 9000s chipset shortages (TechInsights, Jan 2024) — production ramping but yield-limited. (TechInsights via Yahoo Finance, 2024-01-03, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/huaweis-harmonyos-beat-apples-ios-093000125.html)
- Mate 60 series sold 2.4M units in first eight weeks (Counterpoint); Nova 12 series (Dec 2023) also Kirin-powered with 5G — domestic silicon pipeline broadening. (Gizchina, 2023-12-17, https://www.gizchina.com/huawei/breaking-boundaries-huaweis-mate-60-5g-sets-the-stage-for-revolutionary-products-in-2024)
- HarmonyOS NEXT developer preview scheduled Q1 2024; ~4,000 native apps in preparation — ecosystem pivot away from AOSP/Android. (Gizchina, 2023-12-17, URL above)

### Workforce
- EVIDENCE THIN — would gate to NA. No pre-cutoff dated item located specifically on headcount/retention for 2023 (annual-report-level staffing detail not captured within cutoff). Indirect: R&D intensity at record 23.4% and continued "talent without restrictions" rhetoric at the 2023 report event (TechNode, 2024-04-01, URL above).

### Brand
- Mate 60 Pro triggered "a wave of patriotic fervour among Chinese consumers" with first batches selling out within hours. (TechInsights via Yahoo Finance, 2024-01-03, URL above)
- Huawei re-entered China's smartphone top five in Q4 2023 (IDC: +36.2% YoY, 13.9% share, #4) after more than two years out; Huawei ranked #1 in China sales in the first two weeks of 2024. (IDC via TMTPost, 2024-02-08, https://en.tmtpost.com/post/6931844)

### Markets
- Q3 2023 China: Huawei sales +37% YoY, share 9.1% → 12.9% (Counterpoint) — fastest-growing maker, driven by Mate 60; Mate 60 opening-month sales >2× its predecessor (~1.5M units, GfK via Bloomberg). (Tech Wire Asia, 2023-10-31, https://techwireasia.com/10/2023/what-is-making-huawei-grow-quicker-than-apple-in-china/)
- Huawei forecast ~40M smartphone shipments for 2024 (raised 33% from earlier target; local media June 2023); it shipped 28M units in 2022 (Omdia). (Mobile World Live, captured 2023-10, https://www.mobileworldlive.com/devices/apple-loses-share-in-china-huawei-gains/ — page displayed a later crawl date; see Discarded)

## 2024

(cutoff ≤ 2025-06-30)

### Board
- Meng Wanzhou began her second on-duty rotating-chair term 1 Oct 2024 (to 31 Mar 2025), succeeding Eric Xu, during which she was to oversee the Mate 70 launch; three rotating chairs: Xu, Ken Hu, Meng. (SCMP via Yahoo Finance, 2024-10-01, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/huaweis-meng-wanzhou-back-helm-093000012.html)

### Risk
- 7–8 May 2024: U.S. Commerce revoked licences allowing Intel and Qualcomm to ship laptop/handset chips to Huawei, effective immediately — ending legal U.S. processor supply (Intel CPUs in MateBooks; Qualcomm 4G chips). (Reuters, 2024-05-08, https://www.reuters.com/technology/us-revoked-some-export-licenses-chinas-huawei-2024-05-07/; full text mirror: https://wallstreetobserver.com/us-revokes-intel-qualcomms/)
- 2 Dec 2024: BIS third package — controls on 24 types of SME, 3 software tools, high-bandwidth memory (HBM); 140 entities added to the Entity List (incl. SMIC affiliates and Huawei-linked fabs such as SwaySure, PXW), 14 modified — targeting the domestic chip network feeding Huawei. (Covington & Burling, 2024-12-09, https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2024/12/us-department-of-commerce-strengthens-export-controls-on-advanced-computing-and-semiconductor-manufacturing-items; Korea JoongAng Daily, 2024-12-04, https://koreajoongangdaily.joins.com/news/2024-12-04/opinion/editorials/Composure-amid-US-export-control-on-HBM/2192954)
- Oct 2024: reported U.S. investigation of TSMC over alleged production of AI chips for Huawei (Sophgo affair). (Data Center Dynamics, 2024-10-21, https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/?tag=huawei&term=government-military)

### RD
- 18 Apr 2024: Pura 70 series (rebadged P-series) launched with new Kirin 9010 (and 9000S1) on SMIC 7nm; Pro/Ultra sold out in one minute. (GSMArena, 2024-04-18, https://www.gsmarena.com/huawei_unveils_pura_70_lineup_on_the_12th_birthday_of_p_series-news-62490.php; Wccftech, 2024-04-18, https://wccftech.com/huawei-pura-70-pro-and-pura-70-ultra-sold-out-in-one-minute/)
- 22 Oct 2024: HarmonyOS NEXT commercially launched — fully independent of AOSP/Android (own kernel, database, programming language, AI framework); >1bn HarmonyOS devices, 6.75M registered developers; >15,000 native apps; claimed +30% performance, +56min battery. (DIGITIMES Asia, 2024-10-24, https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20241023VL203/huawei-harmonyos-mobile-operating-system-harmony.html; Global Times, 2024-10-22, https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202410/1321670.shtml)
- 26 Nov 2024: Mate 70 series launched on HarmonyOS NEXT with new Kirin 9020; Richard Yu (9 Dec 2024) said Mate 70 components are "100% made in China". (Cybernews, 2024-11-26, https://cybernews.com/gadgets/huawei-unveils-mate-70-flagships-in-shift-away-from-android/; GSMArena, 2024-12-09, https://www.gsmarena.com/)
- R&D spend 2024: CNY 179.7bn, 20.8% of revenue; decade total > CNY 1.249tn; 150,000+ active granted patents. (Huawei 2024 Annual Report, released 2025-03-31, https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2025/3/annual-report-2024)

### Finance
- 2024 annual report (released 31 Mar 2025): revenue CNY 862.1bn (+22.4%, near the 2020 record); net profit CNY 62.6bn (−28%); operating cash flow CNY 88.4bn; cash and short-term investments CNY 372.2bn. (Xinhua via china.org.cn, 2025-04-01, http://www.china.org.cn/business/2025-04/01/content_117799246.htm; Huawei, 2025-03-31, https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2025/3/annual-report-2024)
- Segments 2024: ICT infrastructure CNY 369.9bn (+4.9%); consumer CNY 339.0bn (+38.3%); cloud CNY 38.5bn (+8.5%); digital power CNY 68.7bn (+24.4%); intelligent automotive CNY 26.4bn (+474.4%, first annual profit). (Huawei 2024 Annual Report, 2025-03-31, https://www.huawei.com/en/annual-report/2024)
- H1 2024 revenue CNY 417.5bn (+34% YoY). (SCMP via Yahoo Finance, 2024-10-01, URL above)

### Operations
- Mate XT (10 Sept 2024): world's first triple-fold smartphone; shipment estimates raised to 1M units (Kuo, Sept 2024) — China-exclusive premium halo product built on domestic components. (GSMArena, 2024-09-19, https://www.gsmarena.com/; ANTARA News, 2024-11-04, https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4441405/huawei-mate-70-diwartakan-bakal-dirilis-november-2024)
