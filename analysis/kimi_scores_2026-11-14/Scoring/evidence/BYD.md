### Risk
- BYD repositioned as a major mask maker during COVID-19 (capacity ~50 million masks/day by May 2020), demonstrating rapid re-tooling but also dependence on ad hoc pandemic revenue lines. Reuters, 2021-03-29. https://finance.yahoo.com/news/chinese-ev-maker-byd-profit-142234882.html

### RD
- Blade Battery formally launched 2020-03-29: LFP cell-to-pack design (>50% space utilisation gain over conventional LFP blocks), passed nail-penetration test with no fire/smoke (30–60°C surface). Wang Chuanfu framed it as ending EV spontaneous combustion. Electric Cars Report, 2020-04-01. https://electriccarsreport.com/2020/03/byd-launches-blade-battery-pack-set-to-redefine-ev-safety-standards/ ; Gasgoo (2020). https://autonews.gasgoo.com/m/70017858.html
- Han EV, first model with Blade Battery (605 km NEDC), launched 2020-07-12 as flagship of the Dynasty family. Gasgoo (2020). https://autonews.gasgoo.com/m/70017858.html

## 2022 (sources ≤ 2023-06-30)

BYD USA press release, 2022-04-04. https://en.byd.com/news/byd-discontinues-gas-only-auto-line-to-focus-on-phev-and-pure-electric-tech/

- Tang DM-i recalls: 9,663 units recalled April 2022 (battery-pack fire risk); further 52,928 units (built 2021-04-05–2022-04-18) recalled over battery-tray water-ingress risk; customers told Reuters dealers were replacing batteries in non-recalled cars, prompting demands to widen the recall; "more than a dozen" social-media videos of BYD EV fires in the prior six months. India Empire (carrying S&P Global Ratings research), 2022-10. https://www.indiaempire.com/article/193457/byd_india_targets_15000_ev_sales_in_2023
