# SMIC (00981.HK / 688981.SH) — Dated Evidence Record for CAMS Corporate Panel

Entity: Semiconductor Manufacturing International Corporation. Functional condition at 31 December 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024.
Date discipline: evidence for year Y dated on or before 30 June Y+1. Baseline end-2020: sources dated on or before 30 June 2021. Dated sources only; SMIC annual results for year Y published February–March Y+1 are within cutoff. No scoring content below.

---

## Baseline end-2020 (sources dated ≤ 2021-06-30)

### Risk
- US Commerce Department BIS added SMIC to the Entity List on 18 December 2020, citing military-civil fusion and SMIC's alleged links to China's military-industrial complex; licence requirements presumption of denial for items uniquely required for 10nm-and-below production. US Department of Commerce press release, 2020-12-18. https://2017-2021.commerce.gov/news/press-releases/2020/12/commerce-adds-chinas-smic-entity-list-restricting-access-key-enabling.html
- SMIC's own exchange announcement (20 December 2020) stated the listing would not significantly impact operations short-term but would have "a significant adverse impact on the research and development and capacity construction of advanced processes of 10nm and below". EEWorld (carrying SMIC announcement), 2020-12-21. https://en.eeworld.com.cn/mp/EEWorld/a101273.jspx
- US DoD had identified SMIC as a "Chinese Communist Party military company" earlier in December 2020; Republican House Foreign Affairs members wrote to Commerce on 21 December 2020 calling the entity-list rules "utterly ineffective". House Foreign Affairs Committee letter, 2020-12-21. https://foreignaffairs.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/republicans-foreignaffairs.house.gov/files/migrated/uploads/2020/12/20.12.21-Letter-to-Commerce-re-Entity-Listing-for-the-SMIC-Final.pdf

### Board / Workforce
- Co-CEO Liang Mengsong (Mong Song) abruptly resigned in mid-December 2020 (reported the week of the entity-listing), after leading SMIC's technology catch-up; the company faced a "high-level earthquake" in management. Gizmochina, 2020-12-18. https://www.gizmochina.com/2020/12/18/us-finally-bans-smic-and-other-chinese-companies-from-using-american-tech/ ; EEWorld, 2020-12-21 (URL above). (Liang subsequently stayed on as co-CEO; see 2021.)

### Finance
- SMIC raised over US$7.5 billion in its July 2020 Shanghai STAR Market IPO — China's biggest share sale in a decade — with the Big Fund (China Integrated Circuit Industry Investment Fund) and GIC as strategic investors; shares jumped 245% at the open. Fortune, 2020-12-23. https://fortune.com/2020/12/23/the-5-biggest-chinese-ipos-of-2020-and-what-to-watch-for-in-2021/ ; Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance, 2020-07-06. https://finance.yahoo.com/news/smic-seeks-raise-6-6-085809897.html
- In May 2020 SMIC had already raised US$2.25 billion from two state-backed funds ahead of the STAR listing. Caixin Global, 2020-07-06. https://www.caixinglobal.com/2020-07-06/smic-doubles-size-of-shanghai-listing-to-75-billion-101576170.html

## 2021 (sources dated ≤ 2022-06-30)

### Board
- Chairman Zhou Zixue resigned on 3 September 2021 citing personal health reasons; CFO Gao Yonggang became acting chairman. EEWorld, 2021-09-03. https://en.eeworld.com.cn/news/manufacture/eic546947.html
- On 11 November 2021 vice-chairman Chiang Shang-yi (former TSMC R&D VP), executive director Liang Mengsong, non-executive director Zhou Jie and INED Yang Guanglei all resigned; the board shrank from 15 to 11 members. Liang continued as co-CEO. IJIWEI, 2022-01-06. https://jw.ijiwei.com/n/803696

### Risk
- Co-CEOs Zhao Haijun and Liang Mengsong said at the Q2 2021 earnings call that since the entity-listing SMIC had worked with suppliers on "operational continuity" and "the uncertainty risks associated with mature processes have also been further reduced" — i.e., compliance risk concentrated on advanced nodes. Icmtia (carrying SMIC disclosure), 2021-09-15. https://en.icmtia.com/news/630.html
- SMIC planned a new 28nm Shenzhen fab (US$2.35bn, ~55% SMIC-owned with Shenzhen municipal partners); US suppliers Applied Materials, Lam Research, KLA and Axcelis had applied for entity-list licences for the fab, showing licence dependence for even mature-node expansion. AnandTech, 2021-03-20. https://www.anandtech.com/show/16568/smic-to-build-a-new-fab-in-shenzhen-production-to-start-in-2022

### RD
- Q2 2021: FinFET/28nm revenue share jumped from 6.9% (Q1) to 14.5%; co-CEO Zhao Haijun stated FinFET was at full production of 15,000 wafers/month with a diversified customer base — concrete 14nm-class output indicator. Icmtia, 2021-09-15 (URL above).
- Strain: the entity list's 10nm-and-below presumption of denial continued to block EUV access and advanced-node roadmap; SMIC's FY2021 letter to shareholders said entity-list "restrictions set many obstacles to the Company's development". PR Newswire (SMIC), 2022-02-10. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/smic-reports-2021-fourth-quarter-results-301479732.html

### Finance
- FY2021 revenue US$5,443.1 million, up 39.3% YoY; gross margin 30.8% (from 23.6%); EBITDA margin 70.2%; net profit attributable US$1,701.8 million. SMIC 2021 annual results announcement via Design & Reuse, 2022-03-30. https://www.design-reuse.com/news/12014-smic-announces-2021-annual-results/
- Capex was cut to about US$4.3 billion in 2021 (from US$5.7bn in 2020) even as revenue surged — capex cycle temporarily restrained. AnandTech, 2021-03-20 (URL above).

### Operations
- Q2 2021 capacity utilisation hit 100.4% ("full-load operations"); monthly capacity rose from 541,000 (Q1) to 562,000 8-inch-equivalent wafers; Q2 shipments 1.7452 million wafers, +21.6% YoY. Icmtia, 2021-09-15 (URL above).
- Management said supply–demand imbalance would persist at least through H1 2022, with the new Beijing and Shenzhen 12-inch fabs targeted to double Beijing's 12-inch capacity; equipment delivery faced permit and pandemic-logistics delays. Icmtia, 2021-09-15 (URL above).

### Workforce
- Strain/transition: the November 2021 board purge removed the company's most senior technology voices from governance (Chiang, Liang as director), though both co-CEOs continued in executive roles. IJIWEI, 2022-01-06 (URL above). Quantitative attrition/engineering data for 2021 not found in dated sources — partially thin.

### Brand
- Q2 2021 revenue by geography: Mainland China + Hong Kong 62.9% (up from 55.6% in Q1), North America 23.3%, Europe/Asia 13.8% — showing retained US/international customer base alongside domestic gains. Icmtia, 2021-09-15 (URL above).

### Markets
- SMIC was "the fastest growing company among the top four pure-play foundries in the world" in 2021 (+39%), with record gross, operating and net margins — market-share gains during the shortage. PR Newswire (SMIC), 2022-02-10 (URL above).

## 2022 (sources dated ≤ 2023-06-30)

### Board
- Gao Yonggang formally appointed chairman on 17–18 March 2022 (acting since September 2021); sixth chairman in company history; former CFO and finance-background executive now led the board. EqualOcean, 2022-04-01. https://equalocean.com/news/2022040117227-chipmaker-smic-grew-net-profit-138-usd-5-4-bn-2021 ; corroborated by Yicai Global, 2023-07-18. https://www.yicaiglobal.com/star50news/2023_07_186579497762830680120
- Strain: SMIC's August 2022 board re-election removed both co-CEOs (Zhao Haijun, Liang Mengsong) from the board; Arm co-founder Tudor Brown resigned in August 2022; Digitimes noted all US/UK-national engineers had left the decision-making core since end-2020, leaving a finance-background chairman in control. DIGITIMES Asia, 2022-10-18. https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20221014PD207/foundry-ic-manufacturing-smic.html

### Risk
- 7 October 2022 BIS interim final rules (87 FR 62186): new SME controls (ECCN 3B090), US-person restrictions on supporting advanced-node fabs in China (effective 12 October 2022), expanded FDPR, presumption of denial for advanced-node fab exports — a step-change beyond the company-specific entity list. Baker McKenzie Sanctions & Export Controls Update, 2022-10-07. https://sanctionsnews.bakermckenzie.com/bis-issues-new-export-controls-targeting-chinas-advanced-computing-and-semiconductor-sectors/ ; FD Associates summary, 2022-10-07. https://fdassociates.net/october-2022-export-controls-and-compliance-update/
- IP/litigation history in play: TechInsights' MinerVa analysis led to claims SMIC's 7nm resembled TSMC technology; TSMC had previously sued SMIC in 2002 and 2006 (settlements in 2005/2009 era) — live IP-contamination risk narrative. Gigazine, 2022-07-22. https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20220722-china-smic-chip/

### RD
- TechInsights found SMIC had manufactured a 7nm-class chip (MinerVa Bitcoin miner SoC, shipping since July 2021) without EUV — "the most advanced technology product we have seen from SMIC since we analyzed their 14nm technology" — via DUV multipatterning (N+1/N+2 class). Gigazine, 2022-07-22 (URL above); Light Reading via DefenceHub, 2022-07-25. https://defencehub.live/threads/china%E2%80%99s-top-chipmaker-achieves-breakthrough-despite-us-curbs.14124/
- R&D spending was US$733.1 million in 2022, equal to 10.1% of revenue — down for a third straight year from 11.7% (2021) and 17.3% (2020), though absolute spend rose with revenue. SCMP, 2023-03-29. https://finance.yahoo.com/news/chinas-top-chip-maker-smic-093000531.html
- Strain: the October 2022 rules explicitly targeted development/production of advanced ICs at PRC fabs and US-person support, directly constraining SMIC's N+1/N+2 engineering. Baker McKenzie, 2022-10-07 (URL above).

### Finance
- FY2022 revenue US$7,273.3 million (+33.6% YoY); gross margin 38% (record); profit attributable US$1,817.9 million (+6.8%); capex US$6.35 billion; cash US$18.66 billion. PR Newswire (SMIC), 2023-02-09. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/smic-reports-2022-fourth-quarter-results-301743116.html ; Xinhua, 2023-02-10. https://english.news.cn/20230210/014b7e16287c43ea8ec1ad9f7f3b4ae1/c.html
- Q4 2022 revenue fell 15% sequentially to US$1,621.3 million; Q1 2023 guided down 10–12% QoQ with gross margin 19–21% — cycle turning. PR Newswire (SMIC), 2023-02-09 (URL above).
- State shareholders: Xinxin (Hong Kong) Capital, wholly owned by the Big Fund, held 7.80% and was SMIC's third-largest shareholder (2022 annual report). TMTPost, 2023-07-18. https://en.tmtpost.com/post/6621112

### Operations
- FY2022: annual capacity utilisation 92%; monthly capacity reached 714,000 8-inch-equivalent wafers; shipments 7,098 thousand wafers. Q3 2022 utilisation 92.1% (down 5pp QoQ on maintenance and demand decline). PR Newswire, 2023-02-09; TechInsights (SMIC Q3 release), 2022-11-10. https://www.techinsights.com/blog/smic-reports-2022-third-quarter-results
- Fab buildout: SMIC Shenzhen entered production, SMIC Jingcheng (Beijing) entered pilot production, SMIC Lingang completed main fab shell, SMIC Xiqing (Tianjin) started construction by end-2022; Jingcheng mass production postponed 1–2 quarters due to delayed bottleneck equipment. PR Newswire, 2023-02-09 (URL above).

### Workforce
- The October 2022 US-person restrictions (effective 12 October 2022) forced US citizens and green-card holders out of China's advanced fabs — an external constraint on SMIC's access to US-passport engineering talent. BIS rule via Baker McKenzie, 2022-10-07; Wikipedia rule summary, 2022-10-18. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_New_Export_Controls_on_Advanced_Computing_and_Semiconductors_to_China
- Depth indicator: both co-CEOs (Zhao, Liang) remained in executive operation through 2022 even after leaving the board (Q1 and Q4 2022 earnings calls). StockAnalysis.com earnings-call transcripts (Q1 2022, Q4 2022), published 2022. https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hkg/0981/transcripts/119957-q1-2022/

### Brand
- The 7nm-without-EUV finding was "widely reported by the mainstream and industry press" and taken seriously by observers — a standing boost for SMIC as China's only advanced-node foundry, though on a simple low-volume mining chip. Light Reading via DefenceHub, 2022-07-25 (URL above).
- Strain: TechInsights' claim of TSMC-technology similarity kept IP-origination questions attached to SMIC's flagship achievement. Gigazine, 2022-07-22 (URL above).

### Markets
- FY2022 revenue by geography: China 74% of total (up 4pp on 2021); SMIC stated domestic fab capacity was still short of demand while its technology level lagged global peers. SCMP, 2023-03-29 (URL above).
- Global standing: revenue growth >30% for a second consecutive year; FY2022 margin record — but Q4 sequential decline signalled the down-cycle. PR Newswire, 2023-02-09 (URL above).

## 2023 (sources dated ≤ 2024-06-30)

### Board
- Chairman Gao Yonggang resigned on 17 July 2023 ("job adjustment"), under 1.5 years after appointment; vice-chairman Liu Xunfeng (ex-Sinopec/Shanghai Huayi chemicals executive, Big Fund-recommended since May 2023) took over immediately. Reuters via Yahoo Finance, 2023-07-18. https://finance.yahoo.com/news/china-smic-chairman-resigns-replaced-091215531.html ; TMTPost, 2023-07-18 (URL below).
- Board downsized from 15 members (December 2020) to 8, and executive directors from five to one — concentration of state-aligned control. TMTPost, 2023-07-18. https://en.tmtpost.com/post/6621112

### Risk
- ASML announced on 1 January 2024 that the Dutch government had partially revoked a licence for NXT:2050i and NXT:2100i DUV immersion shipments to "a small number of customers in China" — tightening the lithography constraint on SMIC's advanced production. ASML statement, 2024-01-01. https://investor.asml.com/news-releases/news-release-details/statement-regarding-partial-revocation-export-license ; The Guardian, 2024-01-02. https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2024/jan/02/asml-halts-hi-tech-chip-making-exports-to-china-reportedly-after-us-request
- 17 October 2023 BIS rules reinforced and expanded the October 2022 controls: broader SME parameters, refined US-person restrictions reaching R&D facilities, "small-yard high-fence" annual updates signalled. Covington & Burling client alert, 2023-10-19. https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2023/10/us-expands-october-7-2022-export-controls-restrictions-on-advanced-computing-and-semiconductor-manufacturing-items

### RD
- TechInsights' teardown of Huawei's Mate 60 Pro (reported 4 September 2023) found the Kirin 9000s manufactured by SMIC on its 7nm N+2 process — first commercial advanced SoC (with embedded SRAM bit cells) made in China without EUV; die 107mm², 7nm-class critical dimensions but larger than 5nm. TechNode, 2023-09-05. https://technode.com/2023/09/05/huawei-mate-60-series-may-achieve-sales-of-20-million-units/ ; Gizmochina, 2023-09-05. https://www.gizmochina.com/2023/09/05/huawei-mate-60-pro-smic-chip/
- Patent stock: by end-2023 SMIC had filed 19,443 patent applications with 13,450 granted — stated as leading mainland China's industry. SMIC 2023 Annual Report, HKEX, 2024-04-09. http://www.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2024/0409/2024040900356.pdf
- R&D expense 2023: US$707.7 million (vs US$733.1 million in 2022). Same annual report (URL above).
- Strain: N+2 achieved only via DUV multipatterning; analysts noted prohibitive yield/economic challenges and SMIC's own acknowledgement that its technology "lags behind global peers". SCMP, 2023-03-29 (URL above, within 2022 cutoff but the lag statement refers to early-2023 context — treat as 2023 corroboration only if dated within 2023 cutoff: it is not; use with care) → primary strain item for 2023: ASML revocation (2024-01-01, above) and Q1 2023 utilisation collapse (below).

### Finance
- FY2023 revenue US$6,321.6 million, down 13.1% YoY; gross margin 19.3%; profit attributable US$902.5 million, down 50.4% — company attributed to cycle bottom, high inventories, falling utilisation, rising depreciation in a high-investment period. SMIC Q4 2023 release via Design & Reuse, 2024-02-06. https://www.design-reuse.com/news/15563-smic-reports-2023-fourth-quarter-results/
- A-share-basis 2023: net profit RMB 4.823 billion, down 60.3% YoY; depreciation & amortisation rose to RMB 18.86 billion (from 15.39 billion in 2022); construction-in-progress jumped from US$45.8bn to US$77.0bn — heavy state-supported expansion ahead of converted-asset depreciation. Yicai Global, 2024-03-28. https://www.yicaiglobal.com/star50news/2024_03_286673892450999730185

### Operations
- FY2023: annualised capacity utilisation 75% (quarterly: 68.1%, 78.3%, 77.1%, 76.8%); shipments 5,867 thousand wafers (down 17.4%); monthly capacity 805,500–806,000 wafers. Yicai Global, 2024-03-28 (URL above); SMIC 2023 Annual Report, 2024-04-09 (URL above).
- Capacity milestones: SMIC Shenzhen in mass production; SMIC Jingcheng entering mass production in H2 2023; SMIC Oriental (Lingang) mini-line expected end-2023; SMIC Xiqing still under construction. PR Newswire (SMIC Q1 2023), 2023-05-11. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/smic-reports-2023-first-quarter-results-301822249.html
- Q1 2023 revenue US$1,462.3 million (down 20.6% YoY from US$1,841.9 million); gross margin 20.8% (from 40.7% in Q1 2022) — sharpest quarterly strain of the period. PR Newswire, 2023-05-11 (URL above).

### Workforce
- Chairman Gao at the June 2023 AGM: SMIC had 20,000+ employees including 10,000+ engineering talents — "the domestic semiconductor foundry with the highest accumulation of talents"; 2022 attrition "relatively low" and 2023 expected "in single digits"; claimed ability to retain key talent. DIGITIMES Asia, 2023-06-29. https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20230629VL204/china-smic.html

### Brand
- The Mate 60 Pro launch (30 August 2023, during US Commerce Secretary Raimondo's Beijing visit) with a SMIC-made 7nm Kirin 9000s made SMIC the foundry behind China's flagship "domestic chip" — a major standing gain with Chinese fabless customers. TechNode, 2023-09-05; Gizmochina, 2023-09-05 (URLs above).
- Strain: US Congressman Gallagher called for ending all technology exports to Huawei and SMIC, alleging possible FDP-rule violation — reputational/compliance exposure with US-aligned customers. Wikipedia summary citing public statements, 2023. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiconductor_Manufacturing_International_Corporation

### Markets
- FY2023 revenue decline of 13.1% was, per SMIC, a smaller adjustment than the industry average — relative resilience in a down market. SMIC 2023 Annual Report, 2024-04-09 (URL above); Design & Reuse, 2024-02-06 (URL above).
- Demand mix: consumer electronics and "others" (incl. industrial/auto) grew as smartphone share fell through 2022–Q1 2023 (smartphone 23.5% of Q1 2023 revenue vs 28.7% a year earlier). DIGITIMES Asia, 2023-06-29 (URL above).

## 2024 (sources dated ≤ 2025-06-30)

### Board
- No dated in-cutoff source found reporting any board change during 2024; Liu Xunfeng (appointed July 2023) remained chairman through the February 2025 reporting season — continuity only. EVIDENCE THIN for 2024-specific board performance/strain indicators (no positive or negative dated item located).

### Risk
- US Commerce Department sent letters in late 2023 (reported February 2024) suspending permission for US suppliers to sell to SMIC South, SMIC's most advanced fab; at least one supplier (Entegris) halted millions of dollars of shipments. Reuters via Silicon UK, 2024-02-21. https://www.silicon.co.uk/workspace/components/united-states-suspends-exports-to-chinas-top-chipmaker-smic-report-551112
- 2 December 2024 BIS package: controls on 24 SME types and 3 software-tool categories, HBM controls, two new FDPRs (SME FDP and Footnote-5 Entity List FDP), 140 Entity List additions and 14 modifications — the broadest extraterritorial tightening yet, reaching node-agnostic tooling. Morgan Lewis, 2024-12-20. https://www.morganlewis.com/zh-cn/pubs/2024/12/commerce-significantly-expands-controls-on-advanced-computing-and-semiconductor-manufacturing-items ; JD Supra, 2024-12-05. https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/commerce-significantly-expands-controls-8936062/ ; Thompson Hine, 2024-12-21. https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2024/12/bis-seeks-to-further-strengthen-export-controls-targeting-chinas-advanced-semiconductor-industry/
- Dutch government moved to comply with US request to stop servicing/maintaining ASML equipment for Chinese customers — threatening the installed DUV base SMIC's 7nm multipatterning depends on. DatacenterDynamics, 2024-04-08. https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/?tag=asml&term=china

### RD
- Q4 2024 R&D expenditure US$217 million (quarterly run-rate indicator; annual R&D reported in CNY terms in the A-share filing). Microsilicon Technology write-up of SMIC Q4 2024 results, 2025-02. https://en.microsilicontech.com/news_details/7.html
- Mechanism/strain: December 2024 rules explicitly targeted DUV multipatterning-adjacent tooling and FDPR coverage of advanced-node production — the basis of SMIC's N+2/N+3 roadmap — while no EUV remained available. Morgan Lewis, 2024-12-20 (URL above).
- Standing: Huawei's reliance on SMIC for 7nm continued (per Bloomberg, US officials "questioned SMIC's ability to make 7-nm chips at scale"), even as Huawei also sourced TSMC-made AI chips via third parties — indicating SMIC's advanced yield/scale remained a constraint. Bloomberg via SemiWiki forum, 2024-10-23. https://semiwiki.com/forum/threads/tsmc-cuts-off-client-after-discovering-chips-diverted-to-huawei.21306/

### Finance
- FY2024 revenue US$8,029.9 million, up 27.0% YoY (first time above US$8bn); gross margin 18.0% (down from 19.3%); profit attributable US$492.7 million (down from US$902.5 million); capex US$7,326 million; cash on hand US$15 billion; total assets US$49.2 billion. SMIC 2024 annual results announcement (mirrored), 2025-03-27. https://www.noveltech-hk.com/common/php/download.php?num=23 ; GuruFocus earnings-call summary, 2025-02-13. https://finance.yahoo.com/news/semiconductor-manufacturing-international-corp-shse-070053701.html
- A-share basis: net profit RMB 3.7 billion, down 23.3% YoY — revenue record but profitability fell on depreciation and reduced investment income. DIGITIMES Asia, 2025-03-28. https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20250328VL203/smic-wafer-ic-manufacturing-revenue-2024.html

### Operations
- FY2024: wafer shipments record 8.02 million 8-inch-equivalent units (+36.7% YoY); average annual utilisation 85.6% (from 75%); monthly capacity 948,000 wafers (from 806,000). DIGITIMES Asia, 2025-03-28 (URL above); Microsilicon, 2025-02 (URL above).
- Strain: blended ASP fell 4.7% to RMB 6,639 per wafer (from RMB 6,967) — growth was volume-led at lower prices amid depreciation headwinds. DIGITIMES Asia, 2025-03-28 (URL above).

### Workforce
- EVIDENCE THIN — no dated in-cutoff source found with a concrete 2024 workforce performance or attrition indicator. (The 2023 AGM figures from DIGITIMES, 2023-06-29, are the last dated workforce data within reach.)

### Brand
- Chinese-customer revenue grew 34% YoY in 2024 on localisation demand; SMIC consolidated its position as the domestic foundry of choice — strengthened standing with Chinese fabless firms (Huawei ecosystem). GuruFocus, 2025-02-13 (URL above).
- Global standing: Counterpoint ranked SMIC the world's No. 3 foundry by revenue in Q1 2024, despite US restrictions. Statista (Counterpoint data), 2024-07-19. https://www.statista.com/chart/32653/market-share-of-semiconductor-foundries-by-revenue/

### Markets
- Q4 2024 revenue by region: China 89.1%, United States 8.9%, Eurasia 2.0% — near-total dependence on the domestic market, a structural shift from 2021's ~60/23 China/US split. Microsilicon, 2025-02 (URL above).
- End-market mix 2024: consumer electronics 37.8% (largest, +12.8pp YoY), smartphones 27.8%, computers/tablets 16.6% — demand concentrated in consumer/localisation-driven segments rather than global high-margin leading-edge. DIGITIMES Asia, 2025-03-28 (URL above).
