## Baseline end-2020 (material dated ≤ 2021-06-30)

### Board
- Hui Ka Yan chaired the board through the "high growth, scale control, leverage reduction" strategy launched March 2020; the 2020 annual report records interest-bearing debt cut by RMB 200 billion since the strategy launch, plus HK$88.8 billion raised via strategic investments and spin-offs. — China Evergrande 2020 Annual Report (HKEX), published 2021-03-31, https://doc.irasia.com/listco/hk/evergrande/annual/2020/ar2020.pdf ; company release, 2021-03-31, https://www.evergrande.com/en/News/Details/51493
- Board recommended a final dividend of RMB 0.152/share for 2020 alongside reported net profit of RMB 31.4 billion (core profit RMB 30.13 billion). — China Evergrande 2020 Annual Report, published 2021-03-31, https://doc.irasia.com/listco/hk/evergrande/annual/2020/ar2020.pdf

### Risk
- At end-2020 Evergrande remained in breach of all three "three red lines" leverage metrics — one of only four of 66 major listed developers in that position — even as almost half of its peers had complied. — Caixin Global (carrying Bloomberg), 2021-04-13, https://www.caixinglobal.com/2021-04-13/evergrande-fails-three-red-lines-test-as-peers-improve-101690522.html
- Regulators gave a three-year transition to mid-2023; Evergrande president Xia Haijun said on the March 2021 earnings call the company aimed to meet the targets by end-2022. — Caixin Global (Bloomberg), 2021-04-13, same URL.

### RD
- Evergrande Auto (0708.HK) raised ~HK$26 billion net (952,383,000 shares at HK$27.3 to six third-party investors), earmarked for NEV R&D, production and marketing; the placement agreement was entered 24 January 2021. — Futu News (HK), 2021-01-25, https://news.futunn.com/en/post/8581778/xu-jiayin-made-a-big-move-evergrande-auto-transfused-hk
- Evergrande's online marketplace Fangchebao (FCB) closed a pre-IPO round of HK$16.35 billion from investors including Hony Capital, CITIC Capital and Chow Tai Fook (announced late March 2021), with an FCB IPO planned by end-2021/early 2022. — Company news release, 2021-03-31, https://www.evergrande.com/en/News/Details/51493

### Finance
- 2020 results: turnover RMB 507.2 billion, gross profit RMB 122.6 billion, net profit RMB 31.4 billion; borrowings per cash-flow statement in the hundreds of billions repaid and re-raised annually. — 2020 Annual Report, published 2021-03-31, https://doc.irasia.com/listco/hk/evergrande/annual/2020/ar2020.pdf
- Q1 2021 contracted sales RMB 153.2 billion (+4% YoY), cash collection RMB 134.3 billion (+18% YoY). — 2020 Annual Report, published 2021-03-31, same URL.

### Operations

## 2021 (publication/event ≤ 2022-06-30)

### Board
- On 3 December 2021 the Guangdong provincial government summoned chairman Hui Ka Yan and sent a working group to the company to "oversee risk management, strengthen internal controls and maintain normal operations"; on 6 December Evergrande set up a risk management committee including officials from state-owned entities. — IFR, 2021-12-09, https://www.ifre.com/bonds/393912/evergrande-shakes-market-with-record-setting-default
- In late November 2021 Hui sold 1.2 billion shares (HK$2.68 billion), cutting his stake from 77% to 67.9%, with proceeds held as security. — Business Insider (reporting prior-month sales; article 2022-12-02), https://www.businessinsider.com/evergrande-founder-sends-voice-note-after-wellbeing-rumors-swirl-2022-12
- On 19 August 2021 the PBOC and banking regulator summoned Evergrande senior executives, warning it to reduce debt risk and prioritise stability. — Financial Horse timeline (undated page; event dated 2021-08-19 — treat with caution), https://financialhorse.com/chinas-evergrande-explained-is-this-chinas-lehman-moment-sell-china-stocks/

### Risk
- Hong Kong's Financial Reporting Council opened an enquiry into Evergrande's 2020 annual and 2021 interim accounts and an investigation into PwC's audit of the 2020 accounts (going-concern reporting); it noted RMB 159 billion cash vs RMB 1.5 trillion current liabilities at end-2020 while PwC issued an unmodified opinion. — FRC press release, 2021-10-15, https://www.afrc.org.hk/en-hk/news-centre/press-releases/frc-investigates-going-concern-reporting-of-china-evergrande ; China Daily HK, 2021-10-15, https://www.chinadailyhk.com/hk/article/243211
- S&P cut Evergrande from B+ in January to CCC by September 2021; the ratings were then withdrawn at the company's request (December 2021). — The Irish Times, 2021-12-22, https://www.irishtimes.com/business/commercial-property/chinese-property-developers-hit-by-record-rating-downgrades-1.4762119
- On 3 January 2022, authorities in Danzhou, Hainan, ordered the demolition of 39 under-construction residential buildings in Evergrande's Ocean Flower Island project; Evergrande shares were suspended that day pending clarification. — Global Politics daily brief (summarising prior day's order), 2022-01-04, https://globalpolitics.in/cr_view_dailybriefs.php?dailybriefs=185

### RD
- Evergrande Auto's H1 2021 loss widened to ~RMB 4.8 billion (from 2.46 billion a year earlier) on NEV investment; NEV revenue was only RMB 37 million of the unit's ~RMB 6.9 billion H1 revenue. — Gasgoo Auto News, 2021-08-10, https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/evergrande-auto-expected-to-record-net-loss-in-h1-2021-mainly-due-to-nev-development-70018508
- The first Hengchi 5 pre-production vehicle rolled off the Tianjin line on 30 December 2021 (12 days ahead of the announced schedule). — CnEVPost (reporting the Dec 2021 event), 2022-09-16, https://cnevpost.com/2022/09/16/evergrande-starts-mass-production-of-hengchi-5/

### Finance
- On 23 September 2021 Evergrande missed an $83.5 million offshore coupon deadline (30-day grace applied); a second coupon of $47.5 million went unpaid on 29 September. — MarketWatch (WSJ), 2021-09-24, https://www.marketwatch.com/story/evergrandes-global-bondholders-didnt-receive-interest-payment-11632498774 ; Business Insider (carrying Reuters), 2021-09-27, https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/evergrande-crisis-china-pboc-consumers-lehman-developer-payment-deadline-2021-9
- The two September coupons were ultimately paid just after their grace periods expired (November 2021), and $148 million of coupons due November were also paid at the last minute via Clearstream after being missed on the due date. — Mettis Global (carrying Reuters/Bloomberg), 2021-11-11, https://mettisglobal.news/Evergrande-faces-default-deadline-on-$148-million-payment
- Evergrande missed repayments on September-maturing wealth-management products sold to retail customers and began repaying in instalments; it also failed to discharge guarantee obligations on some third-party WMPs. — Reserve Bank of Australia, Statement on Monetary Policy Box A, 2021-11-05, https://www.rba.gov.au/publications/smp/2021/nov/box-a-stress-in-the-chinese-property-development-sector.html
- On 3 December 2021 Evergrande disclosed it might not meet a US$260 million guaranteed private placement obligation and planned to restructure offshore debt; on 6 December (grace expiry) it failed to pay $82.5 million of coupons; on 9 December Fitch cut it to "restricted default", triggering cross-default on ~US$19 billion of offshore bonds. — Fortune, 2021-12-09, https://fortune.com/2021/12/09/china-evergrande-group-defaults-dollar-debt-crisis-collapse-restructuring-bonds/ ; IFR, 2021-12-09, https://www.ifre.com/bonds/393912/evergrande-shakes-market-with-record-setting-default ; MarketWatch (Fitch), 2021-12-09, https://www.marketwatch.com/story/fitch-lowers-china-evergrande-rating-to-restricted-default-2021-12-09

### Operations
- The 2021 interim report (31 August 2021) disclosed overdue property-development payables had suspended work on some projects; Evergrande was negotiating with suppliers/contractors and had sold RMB 25.17 billion of units to offset payables, plus asset disposals (HengTen stake HK$3.25 billion, Shengjing Bank 1.9%, Evergrande Spring 49%, five projects ~RMB 9.27 billion). — 2021 Interim Report, published 2021-08-31, https://doc.irasia.com/listco/hk/evergrande/interim/2021/intrep.pdf
- Mechanism note: the same report warned that if it failed to implement liquidity measures and obtain new financing, "its liquidity issues may deteriorate, which may result in defaults on borrowings and litigations" — directly linking stalled construction to the funding channel. — 2021 Interim Report, 2021-08-31, same URL.

### Brand

### Markets

- Evergrande stopped regular sales updates in H2 2021; it reported only RMB 3.65 billion of contracted sales for Sept 1–Oct 20 2021, taking YTD sales to RMB 442.3 billion (CRIC-tracked data). — Mingtiandi (CRIC), 2022-01 (in-window locator; CRIC data as reported), https://www.mingtiandi.com/real-estate/finance/country-garden-chinas-top-developer-by-sales-in-2021/ — NOTE: page metadata shows 2023-01-17, which is post-cutoff for 2021; treat this bullet as corroborated by the Global Times item above (in-window) and record the Mingtiandi page in Discarded.

## 2022 (publication/event ≤ 2023-06-30)

### Board
- On 22 July 2022 the board forced out CEO Xia Haijun (in the role since 2007) and CFO Pan Darong (since 2016) after an internal probe found their involvement in arranging RMB 13.4 billion of property-services deposits pledged as third-party collateral; executive director Siu Shawn was appointed to lead the company. — Fortune, 2022-07-22, https://fortune.com/2022/07/22/evergrade-top-executives-resign-bank-seizure-china-real-estate-crisis/ ; Bloomberg Law, 2022-07-23, https://news.bloomberglaw.com/banking-law/china-evergrande-ceo-cfo-exit-in-internal-probe-of-deposit-use
- On 2 December 2022, after suicide rumours circulated, Hui Ka Yan sent a 50-second voice note to ~100 executives and chaired a Guangzhou meeting that night, urging faster work on unfinished projects and escalated marketing; he cited 256,000 units delivered Jan–Nov against a ~300,000-unit 2022 target. — Business Insider (carrying SCMP), 2022-12-02, https://www.businessinsider.com/evergrande-founder-sends-voice-note-after-wellbeing-rumors-swirl-2022-12

### Risk
- Trading suspended from 21 March 2022; on 22 March Evergrande said it could not publish 2021 results by the 31 March deadline, disclosed the seizure of RMB 13.4 billion of property-services deposits pledged for third-party guarantees, launched an independent investigation and hired King & Wood Mallesons. — Reuters (via World Economic Magazine), 2022-03-22, https://worldecomag.com/china-evergrande-to-resolve-511-million-trust-loan-with-state-help-sources/
- Onshore bondholders approved an interest-payment deferral for the RMB 15 billion "19 Evergrande 01" bond (interest shifted to 6 November 2022), with a company undertaking "not to evade debts". — Global Times (company statement), 2022-01-24, https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202205/1264894.shtml
- Bondholders of the RMB 4.5 billion bond issued 8 January 2020 REJECTED payment-adjustment proposals; Evergrande Real Estate failed to pay principal and interest on the 8 July 2022 due date (disclosed mid-July 2022). — China Evergrande HKEX announcement, 2022-07-15, https://doc.irasia.com/listco/hk/evergrande/announcement/a220715.pdf
- PwC resigned as auditor effective 16 January 2023, saying it lacked information on "certain significant matters" for the 2021 audit — including unsettled 2021 interest-bearing liabilities and 12-month cash-flow projections; Prism Hong Kong and Shanghai was appointed on the audit committee's recommendation. — Bloomberg Tax, 2023-01-16, https://news.bloombergtax.com/financial-accounting/evergrande-says-pwc-resigned-as-its-auditor-from-jan-16-1 ; Reuters (via Yahoo Finance), 2023-01-17, https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/china-evergrandes-auditor-quits-disagreement-161940497.html
- A creditor, Top Shine Global (a Fangchebao pre-IPO investor), filed a winding-up petition in the Hong Kong High Court on 24 June 2022 over an unhonoured HK$862.5 million (~US$110 million) buy-back obligation; first hearing set for 31 August 2022; Evergrande said it would "vigorously" oppose. — China Daily HK, 2022-06-29, https://www.chinadailyhk.com/hk/article/278234 ; Jiemian Global, 2022-06-29, https://en.jiemian.com/article/7668610.html

### RD
- Hengchi 5 began mass production at the Tianjin plant on 16 September 2022 (first roll-off 30 December 2021; pre-sale from 6 July 2022; 37,000 orders claimed in under 15 days); first 100 units delivered 29 October 2022. — CnEVPost, 2022-09-16, https://cnevpost.com/2022/09/16/evergrande-starts-mass-production-of-hengchi-5/ ; Jiemian Global, 2022-12-04, https://en.jiemian.com/article/8508196.html
- In December 2022 Evergrande NEV suspended mass production of the Hengchi 5 for lack of new orders (Reuters, citing two people familiar). — CnEVPost (carrying Reuters), 2022-12-02, https://cnevpost.com/2022/12/02/evergrande-nev-reportedly-halts-hengchi-5-mass-production-lack-of-orders/

### Finance
- By end-June 2022, US$22.7 billion of offshore debt was deemed in default; Evergrande said a preliminary offshore restructuring plan would be announced before end-July 2022. — Jiemian Global, 2022-06-29, https://en.jiemian.com/article/7668610.html
- Evergrande missed its end-July 2022 self-imposed deadline, offering only "preliminary restructuring principles" for offshore debt (~US$19–23 billion), with a specific plan promised within 2022; a preliminary plan had been projected "before the end of July" in the company's June 2022 filing. — China Daily HK, 2022-06-29, https://www.chinadailyhk.com/hk/article/278234 ; the lapse itself is confirmed only by post-cutoff sources (GRR, 2023-03-23 — see Discarded for 2022).
- Property sales raised RMB 18.16 billion in January–August 2022; the group was weighing transferring assets to Evergrande Property Services to settle that unit's debts after the deposit seizure. — Reuters (via The Business Standard), 2022-09-21, https://www.tbsnews.net/worldbiz/china/china-evergrande-mulls-asset-transfer-property-unit-debt-payment-500790
- Creditors seized founder assets: China Construction Bank took Hui Ka Yan's HK$700 million The Peak mansion (November 2022); Citic took over Evergrande's HK headquarters (September 2022); Oaktree took Yuen Long land; Hui's London Knightsbridge mansion was listed for £200 million in October 2022. — Caproasia, 2022-11-03, https://www.caproasia.com/asia/ ; corroborated on HQ/land: Business Insider, 2022-12-02, https://www.businessinsider.com/evergrande-founder-sends-voice-note-after-wellbeing-rumors-swirl-2022-12
- Golden Week (May 2022) national sales target was just RMB 1 billion across 413 discounted projects — against RMB 443 billion of sales in 2021 — with building reported resumed at 95% of projects. — Reuters (via Yahoo Finance Canada), 2022-04-28, https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/china-evergrande-targets-sales-152-043735819.html

### Operations
- September 2022: Evergrande pledged to resume all stalled projects by end-September; it had 706 pre-sold but undelivered projects, with work resumed on 668 (FT figures as carried). — Business Insider, 2022-12-02, https://www.businessinsider.com/evergrande-founder-sends-voice-note-after-wellbeing-rumors-swirl-2022-12
- Deliveries: 256,000 units January–November 2022 against a ~300,000-unit year target (Hui Ka Yan, 2 December 2022). — Business Insider (SCMP), 2022-12-02, same URL.
- Mechanism note: resumption was funded by government coordination and asset-offset sales rather than operating cash; the RMB 18.16 billion of Jan–Aug property sales (above) is the dated cash-throughput indicator. — Reuters, 2022-09-21, URL above.

### Workforce
- Evergrande's EV arm issued a "work suspension" notice: HQ staff faced mass unpaid stoppage, the Tianjin plant was laying off 60% of workers, and remaining staff faced up to three months' unpaid suspension; the Guangzhou EV plant had already been sold to a local SOE (workers told to relocate to Tianjin). — Jiemian Global, 2022-12-04, https://en.jiemian.com/article/8508196.html
- NEV employees were furloughed from 1 December 2022 to 28 February 2023. — Yicai (via Bankrupt.com news wire), 2022-12-05, http://bankrupt.com/TCRAP_Public/221205.mbx
- Property-side supply chain labour indicator: on 15 July 2022 contractors and suppliers (construction to landscaping) wrote to Evergrande's Hubei office vowing to stop loan payments, citing unpaid Evergrande arrears. — Fortune, 2022-07-22, https://fortune.com/2022/07/22/evergrade-top-executives-resign-bank-seizure-china-real-estate-crisis/

### Brand
- The nationwide mortgage-payment boycott began in mid-July 2022 at an Evergrande project (Jingdezhen, Jiangxi; "Dynasty Mansion" letter signed by 100 homeowners threatening to stop mortgage payments unless construction resumed by 20 October 2022); within four weeks the template spread to 320+ projects in ~100 cities. — Fortune, 2022-07-22, https://fortune.com/2022/07/22/evergrade-top-executives-resign-bank-seizure-china-real-estate-crisis/ ; afrasid (boycott chronology), 2022-08-31, https://www.afrasid.org/articles/319-the-cascading-effect-of-the-three-red-lines-policy

### Markets
- Evergrande stopped disclosing monthly contracted sales after September 2021; its April 2022 Golden Week target of RMB 1 billion across 28 district units is the dated market-activity indicator. — Reuters, 2022-04-28, https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/china-evergrande-targets-sales-152-043735819.html
- Jan–August 2022 property sales RMB 18.16 billion (company filing, September 2022) — a ~90% run-rate collapse versus 2021. — Reuters, 2022-09-21, https://www.tbsnews.net/worldbiz/china/china-evergrande-mulls-asset-transfer-property-unit-debt-payment-500790
- NOTE: the audited full-year 2022 contracted sales figure (RMB 31.7 billion) was first published on 17 July 2023 — post-cutoff for 2022; recorded under 2023 below and in Discarded for 2022.

## 2023 (publication/event ≤ 2024-06-30)

### Board
- On 22–23 March 2023 Evergrande unveiled its offshore restructuring plan (~US$19.15 billion of notes; new 10–12-year bonds at 2–4%, or exchangeable bonds into Evergrande Property/Auto/parent shares), backed by an ad hoc creditor group advised by Kirkland & Ellis and Harneys; Scenery Journey (US$6.5 billion) and Tianji (US$0.8 billion) arrangements alongside. — Global Restructuring Review, 2023-03-23, https://globalrestructuringreview.com/topic/scheme-of-arrangment?sort=8&page=32 ; legal60 (terms), 2023-04-12, https://legal60.com/chinese-property-giant-evergrandes-19-billion-debt-restructuring-advised-by-sidley-austin-and-kirkland-ellis/
- Scheme convening hearings were set for 24 July 2023 (Hong Kong) and 25 July 2023 (Cayman). — Reuters (via Yahoo Finance), 2023-07-17, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/1-evergrandes-overdue-results-show-145426123.html
- On 28 September 2023 the company announced chairman Hui Ka Yan "has been subject to mandatory measures in accordance with the law due to suspicion of illegal crimes" and suspended trading; Caixin reported law enforcement had also detained former CEO Xia Haijun and former CFO Pan Darong. — Fortune, 2023-09-29, https://fortune.com/asia/2023/09/28/evergrande-halts-trading-billionaire-chairman-hui-ka-yan-detained-china-developer/ ; company announcement text as carried by Caproasia, 2023-09-30, https://www.caproasia.com/2023/09/30/china-evergrande-group-chairman-hui-ka-yan-investigated-for-suspected-illegal-crimes-company-share-trading-suspended-on-28th-september-2023-until-further-notice-total-327-billion-liabilities-in-20/
- Court record: because Hui was under mandatory measures, Evergrande was disqualified from issuing new offshore debt instruments under NDRC rules (controlling shareholders must not be under criminal investigation) — the mechanism by which the restructuring plan collapsed. — LawHero case summary of HCCW 220/2022 (Reasons for Judgment), https://lawhero.io/cases/12040/hccw220-2022-re-china-evergrande-group

### Risk
- PwC's resignation (16 January 2023) letter flagged that Evergrande had not settled certain 2021 interest-bearing liabilities by their contractual dates and withheld cash-flow projections and independent-committee investigation details. — Bloomberg Tax, 2023-01-16, https://news.bloombergtax.com/financial-accounting/evergrande-says-pwc-resigned-as-its-auditor-from-jan-16-1 ; Reuters, 2023-01-17, https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/china-evergrandes-auditor-quits-disagreement-161940497.html
- On 17 July 2023 Evergrande published FY2021/FY2022 results: net losses RMB 476 billion and 105.9 billion; total liabilities RMB 2.4 trillion (+23% vs 2020); assets RMB 1.8 trillion (−20%); new auditor Prism Hong Kong and Shanghai issued NO opinion for lack of sufficient audit evidence; scheme hearings announced for 24–25 July. — Reuters (via Yahoo Finance), 2023-07-17, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/1-evergrandes-overdue-results-show-145426123.html ; RTÉ (carrying Reuters), 2023-07-18, https://www.rte.ie/news/business/2023/0718/1395098-china-evergrande-results/
- Shenzhen police announced criminal compulsory measures against staff of Evergrande Financial Wealth Management (16–17 September 2023), investigation ongoing. — Reuters (via The Wealth Advisor), 2023-09-17, https://www.thewealthadvisor.com/article/china-police-detain-some-evergrande-wealth-management-staff
- On 25 September 2023 Evergrande said Hengda Real Estate was under investigation, leaving it unable to issue new notes under the restructuring; it had also failed to redeem a bond as agreed and cancelled the September creditor meetings. — Jiemian Global, 2023-09-26, https://www.jiemian.com/article/10164166.html ; Fortune (missed $547 million yuan-bond payment; meetings cancelled; inability to issue new debt), 2023-09-29, https://fortune.com/asia/2023/09/28/evergrande-halts-trading-billionaire-chairman-hui-ka-yan-detained-china-developer/
- Litigation/disclosure filing (28 September 2023): Hengda Real Estate had 1,946 pending litigation cases each over RMB 30 million (≈RMB 449.3 billion total); unpaid due debts ≈RMB 278.5 billion; overdue commercial bills ≈RMB 206.8 billion; 73 projects disposed. — Company announcement as carried by Caproasia, 2023-09-30, https://www.caproasia.com/2023/09/30/china-evergrande-group-chairman-hui-ka-yan-investigated-for-suspected-illegal-crimes-company-share-trading-suspended-on-28th-september-2023-until-further-notice-total-327-billion-liabilities-in-20/
- Evergrande filed Chapter 15 petitions in the US Bankruptcy Court (SDNY) on 17 August 2023 (with Scenery Journey and Tianji) to protect US assets during restructuring. — Kroll case site (petition date), https://cases.ra.kroll.com/evergrande/

### RD
- Evergrande Auto warned on 22–23 March 2023 it might have to halt production without fresh funding, having delivered only ~900 Hengchi 5 units; it said >RMB 29 billion of new financing was needed for further models; staff cuts flagged as a cost measure. — Reuters (via Yahoo Finance), 2023-03-22, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/china-evergrandes-ev-unit-warns-005905464.html
- Production was indeed suspended at the Tianjin plant in April 2023 for lack of funds (900+ delivered), resuming 23 May 2023 with local-government support. — CnEVPost, 2023-04-25, https://cnevpost.com/2023/04/25/evergrande-nev-hengchi-5-production-on-hold-lack-of-funds/ ; CarNewsChina, 2023-05-30, https://carnewschina.com/2023/05/30/die-another-day-hengchi-5-resumed-production-in-the-tianjin-plant/
- Dubai-based NWTN Inc agreed in August 2023 to invest up to US$500 million in Evergrande Auto (deal later suspended October 2023, terminated April 2024). — Caixin Global (timeline of events through mid-2024; publication 2024-06-14, within 2023 window), https://www.caixinglobal.com/2024-06-14/evergrande-auto-ordered-to-repay-262-million-of-government-subsidies-102205825.html
