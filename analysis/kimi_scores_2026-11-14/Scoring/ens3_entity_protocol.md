## Deviations from the brief

1. **Year column format:** the schema names the quarter column `Year`; I populated it with quarter labels (2013Q1…2023Q4) since the task is quarterly, one row per entity-quarter. No other schema change.
2. **Stress direction:** I treated S as pressure/load on the entity (complaints, litigation, surge, scandal exposure) — consistent with CAMS usage where higher S = higher stress. A more severe scorer might instead score S as the stress the entity *imposes* on the population (recipient harm), which would push Entity_A's S lower in some periods (the agency imposing stress) — I note this as the main interpretive fork and defend the load reading as the agency-resolution reading.
3. **2018 Entity_A Abstraction held at 8:** CUPI was a re-engineered online compliance iteration that continued averaged-income raising at scale until November 2019 [5]; A therefore stays 8 through 2019Q3 despite procedural softening.
4. Entity_B 2013 quarters carry no quarter-specific evidence beyond normal operations; scored 5/5/5/5 as anchored Reaffirmed cells rather than NA, per protocol.
