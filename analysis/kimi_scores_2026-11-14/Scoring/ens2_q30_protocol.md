## Blinding statement

No blinding is claimed or attempted. The comparison set identities (Rudd I, Gillard, Rudd II, Abbott, Turnbull, Morrison, Albanese) are known by design, and my prior knowledge of Australian politics is extensive. I ran 24 of my own web searches to verify and anchor the framing-calendar events before scoring, favouring primary sources (ANAO, Commonwealth Ombudsman, Senate committees, royal commissions, annual reports, budget papers, Bell Inquiry, AEMO/ACCC, High Court matters, contemporaneous news). This run is **retrospective and outcome-informed**: later findings (e.g. Amato 2019, Robodebt Royal Commission 2023, Bell Inquiry 2022) inform scores for the years in which the underlying conduct and evidence occurred, while annual-resolution discipline is applied to which year's cell carries which evidence, and within-segment adjacent-year steps are capped at ≤2 per metric as required. Symmetric standards were applied to both parties; no government was benchmarked against an ideology, only against the stated 5 = adequately functioning OECD state under normal conditions. Stress is scored as load/pressure on the node (higher = more stressed).

## Method notes

- Handover years (2013 ×3, 2015, 2018, 2022) scored once per adjacent government using only evidence from that government's own months in that calendar year.
- Events that occurred in one segment but were *revealed* in the next (secret ministries revealed Aug 2022; robodebt unlawfulness conceded Nov 2019) are attributed to the segment whose period contains the conduct, as the brief's framing calendar directs.
- The NEM suspension (15–24 June 2022) technically fell 3 weeks after the May 2022 handover; I attributed it to Albanese 2022 (Craft/Flow stress) while recognising in notes that its structural causes were a decade in the making. See deviations.

## Key-cell judgement notes (where I expect scorer disagreement)

1. **Rudd I Craft 2009 (C5 K5 S7 A6).** Contemporaneously, HIP ran "green/on track" through 2009 and insulated ~1m homes; the collapse (4 deaths, termination Feb 2010) was knowable mostly in hindsight. Being retrospective and outcome-informed, I score the 2009 delivery failure into 2009 itself (warnings were accumulating from Aug–Nov 2009). Scorers taking a strictly contemporaneous view would score 6–7 on C/K.
2. **Morrison Stewards 2020 (C5 K7 S9 A7) vs Morrison Stewards 2019/2022 (K5).** 2020 combines the largest welfare-delivery surge in Australian history (1.3m claims in 55 days; JobKeeper $88.8b judged "effective" by ANAO) with the robodebt refund admission ("shameful chapter", May 2020) and aged-care COVID failures. I split the difference: Capacity up, Coherence only moderate. Expect ±2 spread.
3. **Turnbull Archive 2016–2017 (C6 K6).** I treat Archive as *functioning* in these years: the Ombudsman report (Apr 2017) and Senate inquiry (Jun 2017) did expose robodebt, and the ABS published and was scrutinised over Censusfail. Scorers who code robodebt primarily as an Archive failure will go to 3–4 on C. My view: Archive records *oversight working*, not the policy itself (which sits in Stewards/Craft).
4. **Morrison Helm 2020 (C6 K7).** Strong early COVID executive coordination (National Cabinet, swift stimulus, borders) pulls up; the first two secret ministry appointments (Health, Finance — March 2020) pull down retrospectively. A scorer ignoring outcome-informed knowledge would score 7–8; one weighting the Bell findings heavily would score 4–5.
5. **Gillard Helm 2011–2012 (K7/K6 despite hung parliament).** Minority government produced an unusually productive legislative record (carbon price Nov 2011, NDIS Act 2013 pathway, Gonski, plain packaging). I score executive *capacity* well above *coherence* (C5). Scorers emphasising instability (Thomson/Slipper, Wilkie defection) may compress both to 4–5.
6. **Gillard Shield (C4, S8–9) vs Abbott/Turnbull Shield (C7, S6–7).** Symmetry check: Gillard's border regime was legally knocked over (M70), record arrivals and at-sea deaths; Abbott's OSB stopped arrivals but at high opacity, diplomatic cost and ethical charge. I score the *outcome and enforcement coherence* differential honestly while keeping Abbott Shield Abstraction at 6 and noting the human-rights trade-off in narrative, not in a party-favoured score inflation.
7. **Albanese Flow/Craft 2022 (S8).** NEM suspension (June 2022) and energy crisis sit in Albanese's first month; structural causes predate him. I carry the stress in 2022 but restore Craft to C6 in 2023 (gas cap/code, NEM stabilised, CIS). Scorers attributing the June 2022 suspension entirely to Morrison would shift one stress point across the boundary.

## Deviations from the brief

1. The NEM suspension (15–24 June 2022) occurred after the 23 May 2022 handover; per the handover rule I scored it in Albanese 2022 (Craft/Flow), not Morrison 2022, though the brief's framing calendar lists it under Morrison. Morrison 2022 Flow stress reflects instead: CPI 5.1%, first RBA hikes (May 2022), Ukraine war terms-of-trade shock, and election loss. Noted for the aggregator.
2. ATO garnishee controversy verified as **April 2018** (Four Corners 9 April 2018; whistleblower disclosure October 2017), i.e. Turnbull 2018, not 2017 — scored accordingly (Turnbull Archive/Flow 2018).
3. Robodebt scheme closed altogether 30 June 2020; income-averaged raising ceased 19 November 2019. For annual resolution I treat 2019 as the year the unlawfulness became undeniable (Amato concession) and 2020 as refund/closure — evidence from each government's own period.
4. No NA scores were used; evidence was sufficient for all 168 cells, though Gillard-era Hands and Archive rest partly on secondary synthesis rather than a single primary audit.
5. Search count: 24 web searches (brief requested ≥20).
