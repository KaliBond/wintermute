## Evidence detail

**Q01.** Frozen events E1 (2015-05-12, budget announcement) and E2 (2015-07-01, operational commencement). Entity_A K: 2013Q1–2015Q2 = 5; 2015Q3 = 4 and never returns above 4 until 2021. The agency signature begins in the same quarter as the executive change it responds to, one quarter after announcement. Register weakening condition (agency deterioration precedes executive change, or chronology reversed) does not occur. Direction resolved: executive change precedes/concurrent. *Caveat:* same-quarter concurrency means the 3-month sampling interval cannot resolve whether E2 caused the Q3 K drop or both reflect the same decision; this is the register's "same-period ambiguity", noted not hidden.

**Q02.** Window 2016Q3–2019Q3 (13 quarters, OCI operating). Helm C≥5 in 5/13; Helm K≥5 in 10/13 (K≤4 only 2017Q3, 2017Q4, 2018Q3); joint C≥5&K≥5 in 5/13 (frozen threshold 6.5 — not met). Entity_A K ranged 3–4 throughout. Neither the frozen prediction nor the frozen weakening condition (parallel collapse in most quarters) is satisfied → inconclusive as operationalised. On the register's original wording ("maintained or increased executive coordination or execution capability accompanies independently defined deterioration in lawful service"), the K evidence (10/13) supports it; the C evidence does not, and the register's own caution that coherence alone is not corroboration cuts both ways. Reported as split.

**Q03.** Measured gaps (Archive K − Helm K): 2017Q2 −1, Q3 0, Q4 0; 2018 all in {0, +1}; 2019Q1 0, Q2 −1, Q3 −1. Max |gap| = 1 during the failure-to-revise window → the frozen mismatch signature is absent. **Specification defect disclosed:** the frozen text wrote the prediction as "gap ≥ 2" while defining gap as Archive − Helm; the stated meaning was "oversight custody below executive execution", which is gap ≤ −2. The defect is reported, not silently fixed; applying the stated meaning does not change the verdict (no gap ≤ −2 before 2020 either). The gap −2 cells (2020Q2, 2021Q4) belong to the Tune-review-era custody failure already documented in the 2020–22 panel and are a different phenomenon from robodebt-era corrective feedback. Verdict stands: not supported as frozen; exploratory.

**Q04.** Every quarter 2017Q2–2019Q3 scores Entity_A K≤4 and S≥6 (computed check passed). Between W1 and W5 the agency received the Ombudsman report, a Senate report recommending suspension, five adverse AAT judgments, and the first successful court challenge, and continued raising income-averaged debts. Latency 31 months from W1; 29 months from W2. Receipt is proxied by the Senate response (mid-2017), distinguishing issuance from receipt per the register.

**Q05.** Baselines (2013Q1–2014Q4 mean K): Entity_A 5.00, Entity_B 4.75. Minima 2015Q1–2019Q4: Entity_A 3.00 (−2.0); Entity_B 4.00 (−0.75). In the operating window 2016Q3–2019Q3 Entity_B minimum was 4 (delta −0.8), within the frozen ±1 control band. Entity_B's eleven-year global K minimum is 4. The executive–agency pattern is therefore not replicated in a comparable compliance agency under the same executive, the same data-matching technology environment, and (2020) the same exogenous shock. Concentration in the robodebt-relevant institution: supported. *Caveat:* a single control agency cannot exclude a welfare-portfolio-specific explanation (DSS policy environment) rather than agency-specific failure; that distinction needs a second welfare-delivery comparator to resolve.

**Q06.** Cessation at 2019Q4; quarters 2020Q1–Q4: K 4, 3, 4, 4 — no recovery, and the window is exactly the COVID shock (C1: 1.3m claims in 55 days; S peaks at 9 in 2020Q2). Escape clause per the frozen addendum (concurrent shock → inconclusive; repair tested at first post-shock stabilisation): first K≥5 is 2021Q3. Whether earlier repair would have occurred absent COVID is unknowable from this design.

## Addendum — Ensemble robustness (same run date, 2026-11-14)

The masked panels were independently scored by two additional scorers (18 and 15 own web searches; 20 and 26 numbered sources; recognition recorded pre-scoring; inter-scorer blind; all retrospective/outcome-informed per the governing protocol note). Medians across the three scorers: `EntityAB_2013Q1-2023Q4_ensemble.csv`; ranges and raw triples: `EntityAB_2013Q1-2023Q4_envelope.csv`; full protocol: `EntityAB_ensemble_protocol.md`.

Spread: 94/352 cells unanimous (26.7%); maximum spread 3 (Entity_A 2019Q4 Stress, 5/6/8); disagreement concentrated in the 2020 COVID-surge quarters, in 2023 service conditions, and in Entity_B 2020 peak scoring.

Robustness of verdicts on ensemble medians:

- **Q01 unchanged — supported.** First median K≤4 at 2015Q3 under both strict (≤4.0) and lenient (<5.0) thresholds. The temporal-precedence result does not depend on any single scorer.
- **Q04 unchanged — supported.** The persistence pattern (K≤4, S≥6 across 2017Q2–2019Q3) holds on medians under both thresholds, all ten quarters.
- **Q05 unchanged — supported.** Median deltas: Entity_A −2.00 (baseline 5.00, minimum 3.00); Entity_B −0.75 (baseline 4.75, window minimum 4.00, never below 4 in eleven years). The specificity result does not depend on any single scorer.
- **Q02 unchanged — split.** Agency K range in the operating window is 3–4 on medians; the frozen joint-threshold failure and the register-wording support both replicate.
- **Q06 changes — scorer-dependent.** On medians, K recovers 4→5 by 2020Q3 (within the frozen 4-quarter window), which would satisfy the prediction; this rests on two of three scorers judging the pandemic surge as handled (backlog cleared by May 2020; ~12,000 staff mobilised; high payment-accuracy rates). Scorer 1's K3/S9 trough — the minority view — is what triggered the inconclusive escape clause. Reported as: supported on the median, inconclusive on the most severe scorer; the disagreement is substantive and documented, not averaged away.
- **Correction accepted from scorer 2:** the garnishee public break verified as April 2018 (Four Corners), not 2017Q2; affects Entity_B coherence cells only, not the K-based Q05 test.
- **Accepted against scorer 1:** the 2023 Entity_A service slide (1.35m backlog peak, ~30-minute average call waits per the 2023–24 annual report) scored K4 by scorers 2–3; the parent panel's 2023 K5 was too generous. The parent panel is not re-scored (freeze); the median carries the robust estimate.

Bottom line: the three tests this ensemble was commissioned to check — Q01, Q04, Q05 — are robust across three independent scorers. The two verdicts that move under ensemble (Q06, and the 2023 cells of the parent panel) are reported as scorer-dependent with the substantive disagreement on the record.
