## Blind rules applied

Yes. Prestige and labels ignored (founder fame, market caps, "national champion"
titles not scored as Capacity); the eight functions scored independently with no
cross-node reconciliation; each entity scored at its own scale. No other scorer's
output was seen. Firm-scale node interpretation, applied identically to all firms:
**Helm** = executive coordination of the firm; **Shield** = legal/regulatory
protection of the firm's boundary (licences, contract enforcement, insurance
channels, policy shelter); **Lore** = brand/employee symbolic cohesion;
**Stewards** = custody and allocation of capital and land/stores;
**Craft** = product/process technique; **Hands** = workforce mobilisation;
**Archive** = institutionalised know-how/records; **Flow** = movement of
capital, goods and orders. Stress scored as load throughout (higher = more pressed).
