## Blind rules applied

Yes. Prestige, labels, office and person fame ignored; each node scored at the scale the packet describes (national state for China; single firm for the seven companies); no cross-node reconciliation (e.g. China Stewards Stress 7-8 coexists with Helm Capacity 8 — inconsistency treated as data); no function inferred from another's strength or weakness. Firm Shield scored as protection/coercion available to the firm (state backing, legal defence, regulatory shelter); firm Flow as circulation of goods, components, capital and signals through the firm and its networks; firm Stewards as custody/allocation of stores (land, cash, capacity, reserves) tied or untied to real constraint; firm Hands as the labour/mobilisation bond; firm Archive as organisational memory that survives actor exit. Institution-counting guard applied (no score rises from counting new institutions, patents or plans alone).
