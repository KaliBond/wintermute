Archive rows revised for all 21 years. The audit found the function scored too flat: Capacity held at 5 ("barely reliable" anchor) for all 21 years and Coherence/Abstraction unmoved 1901–05 despite the construction of the national memory layer. Revision facts (corpus, high confidence unless noted):
- High Court established 1903: national precedent system begins (Archive C 6->7, A 5->6, 1903).
- Census and Statistics Act 1905; Commonwealth Bureau of Census and Statistics established 1906 under Knibbs (C 7 from 1905; K 5->6, 1906).
- Bureau of Meteorology Act 1906; national meteorological service operational 1907–08 (K 6 from 1906; the K anchor "core job done, little margin" better fits a national statistical+meteorological+high-literacy-press system than K5 "barely reliable").
- Patents Act 1903 (national IP registry); federal Parliamentary Library from 1901 (basis of the later National Library).
- Universities of Queensland (founded 1909, opened 1911) and Western Australia (founded 1911) extend the transmit-method layer.
- War Records Section established 1917 (Treloar); Commonwealth Serum Laboratories founded 1916; Advisory Council of Science and Industry 1916.
- Bean appointed official historian 1919 (Archive C 6->7, 1919).
- Institute of Science and Industry founded 1920 (Archive A 6->7, 1920; interpolation between anchors, no anchor text invented).
- Wartime: War Precautions Act 1914 censorship strains the press/record layer (S 4->5 in 1914, ->6 1915–18; C held 6 — the collection machinery kept working under strain).
All Archive moves are single-point; no new >=2 moves introduced; no new scores <5 or >8 introduced.

Lore 8->4 (1915->1916) supplementary anchors added per audit: referendum defeat 28 Oct 1916 (No 51.61%); Hughes's post-defeat scapegoating quote — "the selfish vote, and shirker vote and the Irish vote were too much for us"; documented class cleavage (working-class suburbs 70%+ No) and sectarian cleavage; even the AIF's own majority Yes vote of only ~13,000 margins showed the split ran through the society, not around it.

Event-window rule applied on review: a fact lands in the year its condition holds at 31 December. Shield A6 in 1910 rests on the Defence Act compulsory-training amendments assented December 1910 (scheme commencing January 1911); announced-but-not-yet-operative conditions do not move scores.
