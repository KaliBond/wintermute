## Era statements and quarter-level change notes

### Helm (executive coordination)
- **Era 1 — 2018:** The executive thrashed through spill week (Aug 2018) into minority government, unable to bind its own energy policy, then bought stability via the December two-thirds rule.
- **Era 2 — 2019 to 2020Q1:** A surprise May 2019 majority restored decision authority, which was then stress-tested — and initially failed — by Black Summer.
- **Era 3 — 2020Q2 to 2021:** National Cabinet and crisis fiscal powers made decisions bind quickly, until the 2021 vaccine rollout broke federal–state alignment.
- **Era 4 — 2022 to 2023:** Omicron, election defeat, an orderly transfer, then the Bell revelations and the referendum defeat bookended a chastened but functioning Albanese executive.
- Change notes: C falls 4→3 at 2018Q3 (spills; 70–68 adjournment vote); C 3→4 at 2019Q2 (21 May election, majority restored); C 4→3 at 2020Q1 (bushfire response); C 3→6, K 4→6, S 6→8, A 3→5 at 2020Q2 (National Cabinet + JobKeeper + biosecurity emergency); K/C step down 2021Q2 (rollout failure, premiers publicly blaming Canberra); C/K/A fall 2022Q1 (Omicron pivot); C 5→4 at 2022Q4 (Bell report, Nov 2022); C 5→4 at 2023Q4 (referendum lost).

### Shield (organised coercion and protection)
- **Era 1–2 (2018–2019):** The ADF remained a respected instrument with known structural gaps (submarine transition, surface fleet), on which no decision bound.
- **Era 3 (2020–2021):** Homeland mobilisation (fire assist, border support, quarantine) validated coordination; AUKUS (Sep 2021) finally bound a strategic reorientation.
- **Era 4 (2022–2023):** The Solomons–China pact and DSR's "not fully fit for purpose" finding formalised what strain had long implied, with an October 2023 austerity drive cutting into sustainment.
- Change notes: C 5→6 at 2020Q2 (ADF domestic mobilisation); C 5→6, S 5→6 at 2021Q3 (AUKUS announcement); S 6→7 at 2023Q2 (DSR: "most perilous since WWII" framing; Ukraine); K 5→4 at 2023Q2 (DSR readiness finding; Navy workforce; Oct 2023 cuts); A 5→6 at 2023Q2 (DSR as used strategy).

### Lore (symbolic coordination)
- **Era 1–2 (2018–2019):** Symbolic coordination eroded — revolving-door prime ministership, 41% dissatisfaction with democracy by 2019 — while surface rituals (Anzac, volunteering) still mobilised.
- **Era 3 (2020–2021):** Black Summer briefly unified the nation around firefighters and communal sacrifice, then vaccine federalism and mandates re-polarised belonging along state and "freedom" lines.
- **Era 4 (2022–2023):** The 2022 election realignment (record low major-party vote, teal wave) and the 60–40 Voice referendum defeat showed shared symbols no longer binding a majority — though turnout (90%) and peaceful process did.
- Change notes: C/K 4→3 at 2018Q3 (spill; fifth PM in eight years); S 5→7 at 2020Q1 (fire trauma; smoke over ~80% of population); C 4→5 at 2020Q2 (rally-round-flag; driveway Anzac); C 5→4, A 4→3 at 2021Q2 (blame game); C 4→3 at 2022Q1 (convoy protests); C 4→3, K 4→3 at 2023Q4 (referendum defeat in every state).

### Stewards (custody and allocation of stores and dependents)
- **Era 1–2 (2018–2019):** The store was strong (surplus approaching) but allocation to dependents was corrupted by Robodebt — unlawful income averaging against internal legal advice.
- **Era 3 (2020–2021):** Allocation machinery performed at unprecedented scale and speed ($89b JobKeeper, 3.6m/month through the ATO), holding households through the deepest downturn since the Depression.
- **Era 4 (2022–2023):** Custody faced inflation and mortgage stress (CPI 7.8% Dec 2022; twelve RBA hikes), while the Robodebt Royal Commission (July 2023) exposed the stewardship failure; the government accepted all 57 recommendations (Nov 2023) — repair begun, not complete.
- Change notes: C 5→4 at 2019Q4 (robodebt unlawful operation continuing; first court challenge); S 4→6 at 2020Q1 ($2b fire fund, NBRA); S 6→8, C/K up at 2020Q2 (JobKeeper surge); C/K step down 2021Q2 (JobKeeper ends Mar 2021); S 6→7 at 2022Q4 (CPI peak, rate shock biting); C 5→4, A 4→3 at 2023Q3 (royal commission findings); A 3→4 at 2023Q4 (all recommendations accepted).

### Craft (technique: make, repair, standardise, transmit)
- **Era 1–2 (2018–2019):** Technique was adequate but unremarkable — the NBN build completed while digital-government delivery stayed patchy.
- **Era 3 (2020–2021):** The pandemic exposed thin technique: the outsourced COVIDsafe app failed, and the single-supplier vaccine strategy collapsed; success returned only when established state-run and GP/pharmacy channels did the difficult known work.
- **Era 4 (2022–2023):** Post-pandemic technique held steady — industrial technique (shipbuilding, onshore mRNA construction) remained slow and dependent on foreign partners.
- Change notes: A 4→3 at 2020Q2 (COVIDsafe failure); C 5→4, K 5→4, A 4→3 at 2021Q2 (rollout logistics collapse); K/C 4→5, A 3→4 at 2021Q4 (GP/pharmacy/state channels lifted coverage above 90% by year end).

### Hands (collective bodily work and mass mobilisation)
- **Era 1–2 (2018–2019):** Mobilisation worked — drought and fire volunteering showed the bond intact — but on an under-resourced volunteer base.
- **Era 3 (2020–2021):** Australia mobilised at extraordinary intensity without the bond dissolving: firefighting, national lockdown compliance, mass vaccination; 2021 fatigue and repeated quarantine leaks dented method, not the bond.
- **Era 4 (2022–2023):** Omicron's "let it rip" turn, workforce collapse and the sharpest labour shortage in decades strained mobilisation; migration restart (cap 160k→195k) and high participation steadied it, with strikes marking normal contention rather than dissolution.
- Change notes: C 6→7, K 5→6 at 2020Q1–Q2 (fire mobilisation; lockdown compliance; JobKeeper held jobs); C 7→6 at 2021Q2 (fatigue); A 5→3 at 2021Q2 (quarantine leaks); C 6→5, K 6→5 at 2022Q1 (Omicron absenteeism; labour scarcity); S 4→8 at 2020Q1 (see move #8).

### Archive (usable memory)
- **Era 1–2 (2018–2019):** Reviews accumulated without remedy — Tune (2019) documented a decade of unfunded digitisation and at-risk collections; Thodey (2019) counted 18 APS reviews since 2003 with unlearned lessons.
- **Era 3 (2020–2021):** Crisis decision velocity outran record systems; the 2021 NAA rescue funding ($67.7m) treated symptoms; the Senate's APS Inc report documented hollowed-out in-house know-how.
- **Era 4 (2022–2023):** The secret-ministries scandal (revealed Aug 2022) showed executive action could escape the record altogether; the response — Bell inquiry, Ministers of State Amendment Act (Nov 2023), Robodebt royal commission reforms accepted — began rebuilding usable memory.
- Change notes: C/K 5→4 at 2019Q2 (Tune Review); A 4→3 at 2019Q2 (decade of unfunded proposals); C 4→3, K 4→3 at 2022Q4 (secret ministries; Bell); C 4→5 at 2023Q3 (royal commission exercises memory; findings and referrals); A 3→4 at 2023Q3 (57 recommendations accepted; notification now statutory).

### Flow (circulation of goods, value, persons, usable signals)
- **Era 1–2 (2018–2019):** Open, diversified flows; persons and goods moved freely.
- **Era 3 (2020–2021):** Beijing's coercion campaign (barley, wine, coal, lobster; ~A$20b of goods) disrupted but did not break circulation — exporters physically rerouted to India, Japan, Korea; iron ore kept flowing.
- **Era 4 (2022–2023):** The token system itself stuttered: the NEM's whole-market suspension (June 2022), CPI at 7.8% (Dec 2022), twelve RBA hikes, and failed RBA forward guidance degraded signal fidelity; 2023 brought thaw (barley tariffs lifted Aug 2023), easing inflation and repaired market rules.
- Change notes: C 6→5, S 4→6 at 2020Q2 (border halt; coercion begins); A 4→3 at 2022Q1 (RBA guidance failure); C 5→4, K 5→4, S 5→8 at 2022Q2 (NEM suspension; administered caps); K 4→5, S 8→7, A 3→4 at 2022Q3–Q4 (market restored; Gas Market Code with ACCC enforcement; crisis reviews); C 4→5 at 2023Q1 (trade thaw, market operating); S 7→6 at 2023Q2 (inflation easing from peak).
