## 2. Symmetry clause (binding)

Identical rules apply to every segment regardless of party. No Western-liberal-democracy benchmark is used; harm by any Australian government is scored as harm. The comparator events frozen in §4 include programmes of both Labor and Coalition governments chosen by the criteria, not by the analyst's preferences. If applying the frozen criteria produces precedents under a Labor government, that result is reported without mitigation.

## 3. Criteria — operational definitions and thresholds

A **qualifying event** is an operating government programme (not a proposal) with an official evidentiary basis (court judgment, ombudsman/audit finding, royal commission, or settled class action with admitted conduct), evidenced within the window. Robodebt's own values set each threshold; a comparator meets a criterion at robodebt-comparable severity if it crosses the same threshold.

| # | Criterion | Frozen threshold (robodebt value) |
|---|---|---|
| C1 Scale | Adverse effect on ≥ 400,000 persons OR ≥ $1.5b aggregate adverse financial effect | 433,000 persons; ≥ $1.763b asserted |
| C2 Unlawfulness | Official finding of unlawfulness of an operating programme affecting ≥ 1,000 persons | Federal Court 2019; settled 2021; RC 2023 ("unlawful from inception") |
| C3 Automation | Adverse state decisions (debt, penalty, denial) generated or substantially derived by automated data-matching or proxy method applied to ≥ 10,000 persons | Income averaging; ~783,000 interventions/yr at peak |
| C4 Intimidation | Documented practice of threats (prosecution, penalty escalation, or coercive deterrence of complaint/appeal/criticism) connected to the programme and directed at persons exercising rights | Prosecution warnings in compliance process; RC deterrence-of-criticism finding (disputed by Tudge) |
| C5 Targeting | Population selected by economic/social category (not individualised suspicion) for adverse treatment | Welfare recipients as a class |
| C6 Correction delay | ≥ 24 months from credible adverse signal reaching an authorised decision-maker to substantive remedy | 31 months (Ombudsman Apr 2017 → cessation Nov 2019) |

## 5. Screen decision rules (frozen)

1. For each criterion Ci: **precedent exists** if any segment other than Morrison's has a qualifying event meeting Ci at the frozen threshold; **robodebt is an outlier on Ci** if no other segment qualifies.
2. C4 caveat carried in the verdict text: the RC deterrence finding is disputed; C4 verdict reported as "outlier (contested)" if it rests on that finding alone.
3. Overall Q30 verdict: per-criterion table plus a summary line — the register's "no precedent" claim is **supported only for criteria on which robodebt is an outlier**; the claim fails for any criterion with an in-window precedent. The verdict states n = 7 segments and the boundary limitation (§1) explicitly; no absolute "no precedent" language is permitted.
4. The 8-node comparison panels are the supporting record (state condition during each event); the screen itself runs on the frozen event table. Panel disagreement among scorers does not alter a screen verdict and vice versa; each is reported on its own evidence.
5. Verdict classes: outlier / precedent exists / not determinable (insufficient evidence), per criterion.

## 6. Blinding and scoring discipline

Same disclosure as the 2026-11-14 entity run, one level weaker: the comparison set's identity (Australian governments 2007–2023) cannot be masked because the question requires it, so no blinding is claimed for the scorers; inter-scorer blindness and freeze-before-access still apply. Each scorer assembles independent evidence (own searches), scores all 672 cells, and records provenance. The parent scorer scores from the verified anchors in this addendum plus session knowledge.
