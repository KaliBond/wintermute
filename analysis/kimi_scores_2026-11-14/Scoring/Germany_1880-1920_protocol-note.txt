Evidence basis:

- Web searches run 2026-09-22, cited in commentary:
  1. Turnip Winter 1916–17 — War Food Office created 22 May 1916; official rations 1,560 cal/day summer 1917 falling to 1,000 in winter vs ~3,000 required; potato crop failure; blockade cut food imports ~33%; female mortality +11.5% (1916), +30% (1917); one contemporary Berlin physician reported 80,000 children starved in 1916; ≥400,000 excess deaths attributed to starvation across the war; black market supplied 1/5–1/3 of food; August 1916 Hamburg food riots (Watson, Ring of Steel, via Scientific American; standard historiography).
  2. November 1918 — sailors refuse orders 29–30 Oct; Kiel mutiny 3 Nov; workers'/soldiers' councils spread to ~300 towns by 7 Nov; abdication proclaimed 9 Nov (Scheidemann; Liebknecht counter-proclamation); Armistice 11 Nov; Ebert–Groener pact; January 1919 Spartacist uprising crushed by Freikorps (Luxemburg/Liebknecht killed); National Assembly elections 19 Jan 1919 (SPD/Centre/DDP majority); Weimar Constitution 11 Aug 1919; Bavarian Soviet Republic April–May 1919 suppressed.
  3. Kapp Putsch / Ruhr 1920 — 13 March 1920 Ehrhardt Brigade ~12,000 enter Berlin; Seeckt refuses to act ("Reichswehr does not fire on Reichswehr"); government flees to Dresden then Stuttgart; general strike ~12 million workers; civil-service non-cooperation; putsch collapses 17 March; Ruhr uprising: Red Ruhr Army 50,000–80,000 armed workers (~300,000 miners supportive), suppressed by Reichswehr/Freikorps (von Watter) by ~6–12 April, ~1,000 workers and ~600 soldiers killed; France briefly occupied Frankfurt/Darmstadt; SPD lost 62 seats in June 1920 election; Noske resigns 22 March.
  4. January 1918 strike (Januarstreik) — began Berlin 28 Jan; ~400,000 munitions/metal workers; spread to Kiel, Hamburg, Mannheim, Augsburg; >1 million at height; 414-member Action Committee; dissolved by government; ~50,000 strikers conscripted; leaders arrested/sent to front; martial law threatened 1 Feb; collapse 3 Feb; organised by Revolutionary Stewards outside union control; USPD/Spartacus agitation.
