# CAMS Universal Instruction Set v1.4 — Scorer 3 Protocol

- **Run:** Q30 comparison set, Australian federal government segments, 2008–2023, ANNUAL resolution
- **Scorer:** 3 of 3 (independent ensemble member)
- **Run date:** 2026-11-14
- **Blinding statement:** RETROSPECTIVE and OUTCOME-INFORMED. No blinding is claimed: segment identities (party, prime minister) were known to the scorer by design, and scoring was informed by evidence and findings that postdate the scored years (e.g. the 2023 Robodebt Royal Commission, the 2022 Bell inquiry into secret ministries). Scores are conditioned on evidence available from each government's own period within each year, with hindsight used only where the protocol's retrospective framing invites it.
- **Anchor:** 5 = adequately functioning OECD state. Full 1–10 range used where evidence supports it. No cell was scored NA; a small number of cells rest on thin year-end evidence and are flagged in the notes below rather than being given false precision.

## Method

Scorer 3 conducted 18 independent web searches (primary sources preferred: ANAO, Commonwealth Ombudsman, Senate committee reports, parliamentary library material, royal commission summaries, contemporaneous news), verified the framing-calendar events independently, then drew all scores as a fresh table. No other scorer's output and no other workspace file was read.

## Key judgement calls and expected-disagreement cells

1. **Stewards, Morrison 2020 (C7 K7):** I scored the COVID welfare surge (1.3m JobSeeker claims in 55 days, JobKeeper via ATO, poverty below pre-COVID levels) as the strongest Stewards performance in the set, despite the robodebt legacy and the Nov 2019 cessation having occurred on this government's watch. Scorers who weight the unlawful scheme's continuation into 2020 more heavily, or who discount surge performance as one-off, may land at 5-6. Step constraint forced a visible 2019→2020 jump (5→7); I read the cessation itself as partial correction, hence 2019 = 5 rather than lower.
2. **Shield, Abbott 2014-15 (C7 K6):** scored on border-enforcement effectiveness only (boat arrivals effectively ceased, sustained deterrence), deliberately quarantining the legitimacy and human-rights costs of offshore detention, which I price into Shield Stress (5) and Lore/Archive rather than into coherence of the function. Scorers who fold normative costs into function coherence may score 4-5.
3. **Stewards, Turnbull 2016-18 and Morrison 2018 (C3 K3):** robodebt treated as the dominant Stewards fact once at scale (unlawful debts to ~433,000 people, reverse onus, debt-linked suicides). Scorers who treat robodebt as an Archive/oversight failure only may keep Stewards at 5.
4. **Helm, Albanese 2023 (C5):** Voice referendum defeat (60-6, all states lost) priced as a significant strategic-direction failure even though I score the year's governing capacity higher (K6) given a legislatively productive parliament and DSR delivery. Some scorers may go to 4; others may excuse it as an inherent referendum-risk outcome and stay at 6.
5. **Archive, Morrison 2021-22 (S8):** secret ministries (Mar 2020–May 2021, five portfolios, concealed from cabinet and public) weighted heavily on year-end evidence as it stood (2021 appointments unknown but within that year's evidence per the retrospective framing; full revelation Aug 2022). Scorers confining evidence strictly to contemporaneously public information may score 2021 S at 6.
6. **Helm, Morrison 2020 (C6):** scored on crisis direction (National Cabinet, early border closures, JobKeeper design) while noting that hindsight (secret ministries, robodebt's unlawfulness) complicates the picture; some of that hindsight cost is priced into Archive and Stewards instead, to avoid double-counting.
7. **Flow, Rudd I 2009 (C7 K7) and Morrison 2020:** both crisis-response years with strong macro outcomes; scored up for demonstrated capacity under shock.
8. **Thin-evidence cells (scored 5, flagged):** Rudd II 2013 across most nodes (11-week caretaker-like period); single-year handover cells for Gillard/Abbott 2013 and Turnbull/Morrison 2018/Albanese 2022 rest on partial-year evidence by design.
