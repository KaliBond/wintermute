## Blinding statement

This run is **retrospective and outcome-informed**: the scorer knows how the 2013–2023 period ended (royal commission findings, court rulings, settlement figures) and cannot un-know it. Contemporaneous discipline was applied to the *timing* of scores: each quarter is scored only on the condition of the node as evidenced by information that was knowable by that quarter's end; later findings were excluded from the scoring rationale for earlier quarters and used only to date when conditions actually changed. Inter-scorer blinding maintained: no other scorer's outputs were read; no other files in the workspace were accessed; all evidence was gathered independently via web search (18 searches).
