CAMS Universal Instruction Set

Blind raw scoring of eight functions

Version 1.3 — 22 September 2026

Score eight functions separately. Do not assemble a system story. Do not use one node to adjust another. Do not identify the entity if the case is blinded. Do not compute (V), bonds, (\Theta), or type.

Scores are exploratory probes, not measurements. The rules exist to keep probes honest and comparable, not to make them precise. The durable product of a run is the audit trail — protocol note, anchors, status flags — because that is what later analysis, audit and revision feed on.

Output order: Helm, Shield, Lore, Stewards, Craft, Hands, Archive, Flow



Blind-test rules




No evidence packet exists yet. Until one does, "the packet" means what the corpus and web searches supply for the named function and year. Cite sources and years.




Read only the evidence packet for the named function and year.




Score that function as if the other seven did not exist.




If the packet names offices, parties, or famous persons, ignore prestige. Score performance of the function.




If the packet implies what the whole system "is" (empire, democracy, firm, troop), ignore the label. Score the function at the scale the packet itself describes.




Do not reconcile scores across functions. Inconsistency is data.




Do not infer a missing function from the strength or weakness of another.




Year means condition at 31 December, or at the packet's terminal date. Each annual score represents the evidenced condition at 31 December. Events occurring during the year may affect that year's score once their institutional consequences are observable; later consequences must not be back-cast.




Unchanged scores from a prior year are allowed only if this packet independently supports them. In corpus-plus-search mode, runs of unchanged scores are expected where year-specific evidence is coarse: state persistence, not scoring failure. Where year-specific evidence is coarse, distinguish the two honest ways a score can stay flat. A multi-year panel carries a Score_Status for every cell, in a sibling file: New_Evidence (score set or changed on facts examined this run), Reaffirmed (year-specific evidence examined this run, score held), Carried_Forward (no year-specific evidence examined this run, score held from the prior year). Relational analysis must not treat Carried_Forward cells as independent observations.

No decimals. No zero. Stress is load; never invert it. Title, budget, headcount, vote, price and fame are not Capacity. High Stress with high Capacity is overload of a working function, not collapse.

NA if the packet lacks both a performance fact and a strain fact for that function.

Move of 2 or more points, or any score <5 or >8, needs a fact in the packet that is about this function.

Institution-counting guard: do not score a function by counting new institutions. Capacity rises only where institutions produced reliable, reusable knowledge or capability at the scored scale. Abstraction rises only where records became classifications, statistics, precedent or explanatory synthesis. Coherence rises only where those systems were mutually legible and integrated at the scored scale. Institutions that existed without producing usable output move nothing. Stress may still rise while accumulated competence holds: strain and stock are different quantities.



The eight functions

Score the state of each function. The costume changes. The function does not.

Helm

Executive coordination: set direction, settle conflicts of priority, commit shared resources. Does a decision bind, and does it take effect?Mandate and charisma are not Capacity.

Shield

Organised coercion and protection: boundary, internal order, credible threat. Is force coordinated at the required scale?Numbers and spending are not readiness. Count coalition and effect, not incident frequency.

Lore

Symbolic coordination: belonging, legitimacy, ritual, us and them. Do shared symbols still bind action?Not truth. Not the mere existence of temples, schools, or slogans.

Stewards

Custody and allocation of stores and dependents across time. Who keeps the store, who is resourced, and is allocation tied to real constraint?Price, rent and valuation are not husbandry.

Craft

Technique: make, repair, standardise, transmit method. Can the difficult known work still be done, and taught?Sales and asset values are not technique.

Hands

Collective bodily work and mass mobilisation: convert energy, land, tools and persons into goods, services and common defence. Can enough people work together, at the required intensity, without the bond dissolving into flight or faction?Headcount and unemployment are not the bond.

Archive

Usable memory: keep, find, and pass on precedent and know-how. If present actors vanish, does the function still know what to do?Stored but unused records are not Archive.

Flow

Circulation of goods, value, persons and usable signals. Do tokens and messages arrive where action needs them, with enough fidelity?Intent and justification are not circulation.



Procedure

For a single function-year, in the order above:




State in one sentence what the packet shows the function doing.




Assign C, K, S, A from that sentence and the packet only.




Stop. Do not look at the other seven to "make sense" of the row.

For a multi-year panel, state in one sentence per era what the packet shows each function doing, and note the year-level evidence at every score change. Do not manufacture 168 sentences where eight will do; do not skip the year-level note where a score moves.



Output

Entity,Year,Node,Coherence,Capacity,Stress,Abstraction

Then eight rows per entity-year, canonical order. Integers 1–10 or NA. Nothing else inside the CSV.

If Entity is withheld, write BLIND. If Year is a span, use the terminal year.

Every scoring run carries a protocol note, separate from the CSV. Fields with no entries write "none". The note is not optional.

Protocol: CAMS Universal Instruction Set, version number.
Scorer: model and version.
Run date: date of the scoring run.
Entity: name, or BLIND.
Year(s): years as scored.
Evidence basis: sources and dates. If a packet exists, its source or description. If not, the web searches used and the corpus grounds, cited.
Blind rules applied: yes or no. If yes, confirm prestige ignored, labels ignored, no cross-node reconciliation.
Fact-anchor rule: every score below 5, above 8, or moved 2 or more points from the prior year, each paired with the packet fact that supports it.
NA justifications: each NA score, citing which fact type the packet lacks, performance or strain.

A panel run (multi-year) also carries a Score_Status sibling file: Entity,Year,Node,C_status,K_status,S_status,A_status, each cell New_Evidence, Reaffirmed or Carried_Forward, first year New_Evidence.

Revision loop: a run, once delivered, is versioned by its protocol note. Revisions change the CSV and append the note with revision number, date, cells changed and supporting facts. The CSV holds current state only; the note holds history. Keep the logbook even when the catch goes back.
