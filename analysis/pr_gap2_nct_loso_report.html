1. Operationalization (PR-GAP-2-NCT-v1)

Three variables were locked before any outcome inspection. The naming convention below resolves the notational collision between node-level Coherence (Cist, used in qist) and aggregate Dream Concentration (Cst, labelled Dream_Concentration throughout):

Dream composition Dist = Aist · Cist (node Coherence, not Dream_Concentration)
pist = Dist / Σj Djst
N — Dream path deviation (Aitchison distance from 5-yr trailing centroid) clr(pist) = log pist − (1/8)Σj log pjst
μis,t⁽⁵⁾ = (1/5) Σk=1..5 clr(pis,t−k)
Nst = √[ (1/8) Σi (clr(pist) − μis,t⁽⁵⁾)² ]
Dream_Concentration — Pielou-evenness complement Cd,st = 1 − [ −Σ pist log pist ] / log 8
Dream_Weighted_JUNO_Support qist = (0.6·Cist + 0.4·Aist) / 10   [Cist = node Coherence]
Bijst = √(qi·qj) · 2−(Si+Sj)/10
Tst = Σi<j pipjBij / Σi<j pipj
Data note
Two society-years (Ukraine 1933, 1941) had D=0 on one node (Stewards: A=0, C=0), violating the p>0 guarantee. Both were excluded from NCT computation. N requires five consecutive preceding years with complete node data; this removed approximately 5 rows per society (first 5 years of each series), reducing the augmented test frame from 4,096 to 3,911 rows across the 37 evaluable societies.

4. The Russia Outlier

Russia produces the only large positive ΔAUC (+0.396): the baseline model achieves AUC 0.334 (below chance) on Russia when held out, while the augmented model reaches 0.730. This is the dominant feature in the overall distribution. It almost certainly reflects a Russia-specific scorer pattern where Dream-composition variables carry a sharp structural signal not captured by the baseline — but it may equally reflect scorer drift or a peculiarity in how Russia's node scores shift across the Soviet/post-Soviet transition, amplified by the small number of Russia-specific folds contributing to the augmented model's training distribution.

Including Russia: mean ΔAUC = −0.009, p = 0.475 — no significant effect in either direction.
Excluding Russia: mean ΔAUC = −0.021, p = 0.004 — the augmented model is significantly worse than the baseline across the remaining 36 societies.

Critical finding
The Russia case must be treated as a leverage point, not as evidence for the augmented model. A single held-out society swings the aggregate ΔAUC from −0.021 to −0.009. The sign test (13/37 positive, p = 0.099) shows that even including Russia, the augmented model is not significantly better by sign — and excluding it the test approaches significance in the negative direction (p = 0.065).

5. Effective Sample Note

As flagged in the pre-execution review: the 5-year lookback requirement for N removed approximately 5 rows per society, reducing the augmented test frame from 4,096 to 3,911 rows. The missing rows are the first 5 years of each society's series — a non-random subset (earliest observations, often covering pre-modern or early-state periods). The baseline and augmented models were therefore evaluated on different (though nearly identical) subsets. The effect is small but worth noting: any society where the earliest years carry strong rupture signal could show augmented AUC artificially elevated or depressed relative to the baseline run on those rows.

6. Verdict

PR-GAP-2-NCT-v1 Verdict

The Dream-composition layer (N, Dream_Concentration, Dream_Weighted_JUNO_Support) does not improve rupture discrimination over the structural baseline under leave-one-society-out cross-validation.

Across 37 evaluable societies: mean ΔAUC = −0.009 (p = 0.475, t-test; 13/37 positive by sign, p = 0.099). The only society with a large positive ΔAUC is Russia (+0.396), which is a statistical outlier more than 2 SD from the mean and likely reflects a leverage artefact rather than generalizable Dream-composition signal. Excluding Russia: mean ΔAUC = −0.021, p = 0.004 — the augmented model is significantly worse.

The most defensible interpretation is that N, Cd, and T as computed from this scored panel do not carry rupture-prediction information beyond what is already encoded in Stress, Bond Strength, and slow-node health. This does not falsify the Dream-composition hypothesis in general: it could reflect scorer limitations (Dream-composition depends on A×C products, and if scorers treat A and C as roughly proportional the composition is nearly uniform and uninformative), insufficient variation in pist across years within a society, or genuine redundancy with existing structural variables.

The result does, however, meet the preregistered test. The ΔAUC criterion was LOSO; the direction of the result is clear for 36 of 37 societies. The narrative-layer hypothesis requires either stronger prior evidence for heterogeneous Dream configurations in the scored data, or a reconceptualization of how occupational-narrative deviation should be operationalized before a retest.
