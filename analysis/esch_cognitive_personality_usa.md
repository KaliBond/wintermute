## Framework

Under the ESCH Extended Social Cognition Hypothesis, a society's 8 CAMS nodes are treated as semi-autonomous **sub-brains** — distributed cognitive modules each processing a distinct domain of social reality. The year's **personality** is emergent from the collective Focus and Energy state of these modules.

Two derived dimensions per node:

| Dimension | Formula | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| **Focus** | A × C | Directed cognitive sophistication: how coherent (C) the node's internal models are, scaled by their abstraction depth (A). Focus = 0 when either coherence or abstraction collapses. |
| **Energy** | K − S | Net available fuel: capacity surplus after stress load. Positive = actionable surplus; negative = running on reserve. |

**Node as sub-brain:** a node with high Focus and positive Energy is functioning as a coherent, capable cognitive module. A node with low Focus and negative Energy is dim and depleted — it cannot sustain directed coordination.

The **personality** of a year is the pattern across all 8 sub-brains: which are focused/energised, which are scattered/depleted, and how the clusters relate to each other.

## Crisis Typology — ESCH Interpretation

The cluster-gap rule (economic cluster V vs EPI cluster V at threshold 1.0) identifies crisis *type* from which cluster loses V first. The cognitive model explains the mechanism:

1. When a sub-brain loses **Focus** (A×C → 0), it can no longer sustain coherent internal models. It continues generating outputs but cannot direct them.
2. When it also loses **Energy** (K−S → negative), it can no longer sustain even reactive function.
3. The sequence — Focus loss first, Energy depletion second — means the typology rule (which uses V = C+K−S, not Focus) is a *lagging indicator* of the cognitive collapse that the Focus dimension captures earlier.

**Implication:** A leading-indicator variant of the typology would monitor the Focus differential (EPI Focus minus ECO Focus) rather than the V differential. Focus drop precedes Energy drop, which precedes V drop. The current dataset shows EPI Focus beginning its collapse in 2015–16, the V gap becoming classifiable at threshold 1.0 in 2016, and V entering the extreme range by 2019–2026.

## Shield Consistency

Across every crisis type in the MARKER_USA dataset, Shield maintains Focus and positive Energy:
- 1968: Shield 35.0 Focus, +2.2 Energy (while Helm and Lore are depleted)
- 1979–80: Shield 30.0 Focus, +1.4 Energy (while system energy goes negative)
- 2008: Shield 35.0 Focus, +2.0 Energy (while Flow collapses to 12.8)
- 2016–2026: Shield holds 31–35 Focus and +2.0–3.0 Energy throughout

The security sub-brain is structurally more robust than any other node. This may reflect the asymmetry between the institutional durability of coercive apparatus vs the more fragile coupling between legitimacy/meaning systems (Lore) and material circulation (Flow). It may also reflect scorer bias toward stable military/security assessments relative to the contested territory of governing legitimacy. Both explanations should be tested before treating Shield consistency as a structural finding.
