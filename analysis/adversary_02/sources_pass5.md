## Limitations
- I scored from memory only, without retrieval. Figures and dates can be wrong by months, and cited sources are reconstructions.
- **Hindsight contamination risk is material.** I know about Evergrande's 2021 default, SMIC's later 7nm work, Huawei's 2021+ trajectory and CATL's later dominance. I tried to hold 2020 scores to what was knowable by mid-2021. Even so, Evergrande Finance and Risk in 2016–2020 and the strength of BYD/CATL in 2020 may carry some bias from later knowledge.
- Integer scales and a single scorer give low resolution. Stress scores try to separate internal breakdown from external geopolitical pressure (US actions against Huawei and SMIC), but that line is a matter of judgement.
- The gating decisions (NA) reflect how much I can recall, not necessarily how much public evidence existed. A retrieval-based scorer would probably have fewer NA values.
