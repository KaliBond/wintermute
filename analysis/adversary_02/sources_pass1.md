## Limitations

- **Memory only.** All evidence comes from the model's training memory. Dates and figures are approximate and were not verified, and some secondary dates may be off by weeks or months.
- **Hindsight contamination.** The scorer knows of the 2021 Evergrande collapse, Huawei's later recovery, SMIC's 7nm news, BYD's 2021–2023 surge, and CATL's later dominance. I tried to discipline against this:
  - Evergrande Finance 2020 is 3/4/8/3, not collapse-level.
  - BYD 2020 is not inflated.
  - Vanke and Longfor were not boosted for their later "survivor" status, although knowledge that Longfor stayed solvent longer may still leak in subtly.
  - Residual leakage is likely in the direction of lower Evergrande Board/Risk scores before 2020 and higher BYD RD scores.
- **Opacity.** Coverage is uneven. Huawei (private), Longfor, and pre-IPO CATL are thinly documented, and the NA counts reflect this.
- **Coverage bias.** Western coverage can frame Chinese state and firm events in hostile terms. External geopolitical pressure (the US entity listings) was not treated as internal Stress, except where an internal mechanism existed: Huawei supply exhaustion in Operations and RD in 2020, and SMIC Risk in 2020.
- **Granularity.** Integer granularity and the "2+ point change requires two pieces of evidence" constraint compress year-to-year variation. Some identical consecutive scores remain after re-check where no material functional change was identifiable.
