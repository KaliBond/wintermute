## Limitations
- Every source is cited from memory with approximate dates. None was retrieved or checked.
- Hindsight contamination risk is high. Later knowledge cannot be erased, including the late-2021 Evergrande default, SMIC and Huawei developments after 2021, and BYD and CATL's later dominance. I tried to keep Evergrande 2020 at mid-2021 knowledge, SMIC 2020 at the Dec 2020 listing and Huawei at the Dec 2020 boundary. The Evergrande 2017-2019 Finance and Risk scores and the BYD and CATL 2016-2020 Capacity scores may still be influenced by outcomes I know. Vanke's 2020 scores likely benefit from knowing it stayed solvent, though not its later troubles.
- Scores are single-pass judgements. The upstream constraints on 2-point changes were applied by judgement, not by auditing evidence counts.
- China node-level scoring is coarse, and several years have near-identical Hands, Craft and Archive rows after re-check.
