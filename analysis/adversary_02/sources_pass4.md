## Limitations

1. **Memory-only evidence.** No source was retrieved or checked. Dates and figures are from memory and may be off by days to weeks, or occasionally misattributed. Sources I was unsure of were left out.
2. **Hindsight contamination.** I know what happened after mid-2021: Evergrande's 2021 default, SMIC's later 7nm news, Huawei's later recovery, the Vanke/Longfor stress after 2021, and CATL's later dominance. I tried to exclude it, for example by keeping Evergrande 2020 Stress below the collapse anchor for Board and Operations, and by not scoring later SMIC process milestones. Even so, the Evergrande Risk/Finance trajectory in 2015–2019 and the Longfor/Vanke "conservative" framing were very likely shaped by knowing the outcome. Treat the Evergrande–comparator divergence in this pass as possibly exaggerated by hindsight.
3. **External vs internal stress.** Separating the effects of geopolitical pressure (Huawei, SMIC) from internal breakdown is a judgement call. I raised Stress only where I could name an internal functional consequence.
4. **Opacity asymmetry.** Huawei (private) and CATL (pre-IPO) have uneven disclosure. CATL's NA rate reflects that. Huawei passes the gate because of its published audited ARs, but node-level internal detail is limited.
5. **Repetition.** Identical consecutive rows appear where I re-checked and found no material functional change, mostly Vanke/Longfor 2018–2020 and Huawei 2014–2017. They were not carried forward by default, but the re-checks were done from memory.
6. **China (national) scale.** Nodes are aggregates of very large systems. Scores carry substantial interpretive uncertainty, especially for Shield, Hands and Archive.
