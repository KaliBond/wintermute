## Limitations

1. **No retrieval.** All evidence comes from model memory. Dates and figures may be off by months or by modest amounts. The citations are recalled, not verified.
2. **Hindsight contamination risk.** It is serious, especially for:
   - Evergrande 2018–2020: knowledge of the 2021 default likely pulls Finance, Risk and Board downward.
   - Huawei 2020 and SMIC 2020: later sanctions outcomes may colour these scores.
   - CATL and BYD 2019–2020: their later dominance may pull scores upward.

   I tried to anchor each year on events dated before the cutoff, but cannot guarantee independence from later knowledge.
3. **Opacity.** Huawei is private, CATL had no public filings before 2018, and data on Chinese state organs (Shield, Helm) is opaque. The gate was applied conservatively for the companies. NA is not available for China under the v2.1 rubric, so the opaque China nodes carry wider implicit uncertainty.
4. **Framing.** I tried to separate external geopolitical pressure (entity listings, tariffs) from internal Stress, as the rubric requires. Some residual judgment remains in where that line falls, e.g. HiSilicon's loss of foundry access in 2020 was treated as partial internal RD/Operations degradation.
5. **Scale compression.** Many company scores cluster at 5–8. Distinctions of 1 point should not be over-interpreted.
