# Parsimonious Phase-Space Location in Complex Adaptive Systems: CAMS Metrics as Evolutionary Coordinates of Societies and Organisations

---

## Abstract

The CAMS framework scores societies and organisations on eight functional nodes using four metrics per node (Coherence, Capacity, Stress, Abstraction). Despite this extreme dimensional reduction, anthropomorphic readings of the resulting scores recover recognisably complete institutional states. We treat this recovery as diagnostic. Drawing on the analogy of the Hertzsprung–Russell diagram, we argue that the completeness of the portrait is evidence that the eight nodes track the system's effective degrees of freedom and thereby locate it within a constrained adaptive phase space. The paper formalises the forward mapping from metric configuration to institutional portrait and the inverse mapping from attested institutional description to expected metric signature. It examines narrative as a dual-aspect process that functions simultaneously as load-bearing signalling and as exhaust, and proposes empirical protocols capable of distinguishing phase-space location from contingent historiography. The central claim is that the parsimony of the coordinate system, together with the robustness of the portraits it generates, constitutes evidence that CAMS readings capture more than historical description: they record the system's instantaneous position along a forced evolutionary trajectory.

Empirical results from the P4 Corporate Study — a nine-firm, three-pair case-control design spanning 1990 to 2026 — are presented as tests of four predicted properties: blind portrait recoverability, trajectory coherence, comparative functional completeness, and fuel/exhaust dissociation. All four predictions are confirmed in the direction expected, with Theranos providing the canonical instance of fuel/exhaust decoupling, Boeing providing the canonical trajectory-coherence test, and the full nine-firm set providing a comparative completeness test across case, primary control, and secondary control tiers.

## 1. Introduction

Complex adaptive systems that must maintain leadership, security, memory, normative order, stewardship, technical skill, labour and exchange generate trajectories through a limited repertoire of functional states. Conventional historical and organisational accounts record the contingent events and self-descriptions that accompany these trajectories. The CAMS framework was constructed to measure something else: the behavioural couplings among eight functional nodes, scored independently of what the system says about itself. The methodological wager was that self-report is systematically incomplete relative to observed coordination under load.

An unexpected result followed. When the low-dimensional CAMS vector is read through the archetypal functions, the portrait that appears is not a selective or thin historical summary. It is a recognisable institutional state — Helm-led weakness accompanied by Shield elevation, sustained stress on Hands with ceilinged Stewards, Late Abstraction Collapse signatures in which memory and meaning-making lose load-bearing capacity. The completeness of these portraits under extreme parsimony raises a precise question. If the nodes were arbitrary historiographical conveniences, reading them as simultaneous functional pressures should produce incomplete or contradictory sketches. That they do not suggests the coordinates are tracking the system's effective degrees of freedom.

The analogy is the Hertzsprung–Russell diagram. A star's position is fixed by a handful of observables. That position does not narrate the star's history; it locates the object relative to a forced evolutionary track. Departure from the main sequence is evidence that core conditions have changed and the object now occupies a different regime. We propose that CAMS coordinates perform an analogous function for societies and organisations. They do not reconstruct the path that brought the system to its present configuration. They record where "present" is within the constrained adaptive phase space the system can actually occupy. Historiography and phase-space location are complementary. Neither substitutes for the other.

This paper unpacks the mappings that make the analogy operational. It defines the forward and inverse relations between metric configurations and institutional portraits, treats the completeness of those portraits as an empirical claim rather than an interpretive preference, and specifies the conditions under which the claim would fail. Section 5 presents experimental results from the P4 Corporate Study bearing on all four protocols.

## 2. Theoretical Background

### 2.1 Constrained state spaces in dissipative systems

Dissipative systems with limited repertoires of stable states generate forced sequences. A cyclone is a heat engine whose life cycle — formation, peak intensity, decay upon loss of warm water or encounter with land — is written by thermodynamics. A river's profile from source to delta is written by gravity and erosion. Stellar evolution proceeds through a sequence of core compositions and envelope structures whose order is fixed by nuclear physics and hydrostatic equilibrium. In each case the observer can locate the system at a particular stage without reconstructing the full contingent path that produced it. The location itself carries information about available energy, internal organisation and likely near-term transitions.

Societies and organisations are dissipative systems that must continuously process material, energy and information while maintaining coordination among functionally differentiated parts. The eight CAMS nodes were selected because they correspond to roles that any adaptive system of this scale must fill: direction and decision (Helm), protection and enforcement (Shield), retention and retrieval of information (Archive), normative and symbolic order (Lore), allocation and oversight of resources (Stewards), specialised technical capacity (Craft), execution and labour (Hands), and material and informational exchange (Flow). These are not claimed to be the only conceivable coordinates. They are claimed to be a sufficient set for locating the system within the region of phase space it can actually occupy.

### 2.2 The CAMS coordinate system

Each node is scored on four metrics. Coherence records the internal consistency and mutual alignment of the node's operations. Capacity records the resources and organisational slack available to the node. Stress records the load placed upon it relative to its capacity. Abstraction records the degree to which its operations are mediated by formal, symbolic or high-level representations rather than direct material process. From these raw scores a Node Value is computed and an 8×8 bond matrix is derived that records the strength and quality of coupling between every pair of nodes. The resulting state vector is low-dimensional relative to the number of actors, documents, transactions and events that constitute the system.

In the corporate application, the eight nodes map to functional domains as follows: Helm to board governance and executive decision authority; Shield to legal, compliance and risk management; Lore to culture, narrative, R&D and institutional knowledge production; Stewards to ownership, capital allocation and financial oversight; Craft to engineering, operations and core technical delivery; Hands to workforce and labour capacity; Archive to records, audit and institutional memory; Flow to revenue, markets and commercial circulation. The node mapping is structural, not nominal: a node is assigned to the function actually performed, which may differ from the function nominally claimed.

Prior work has shown that these coordinates support longitudinal tracking, detection of phase transitions and cross-system comparison. The present argument concerns a different property: the capacity of the same coordinates, when read anthropomorphically, to recover institutional states whose functional tensions match those independently attested by historical or organisational sources.

### 2.3 Narrative as dual-aspect process

The stories a system tells about itself — legitimation of Helm authority, justification of Hands' share of load, claims of Archive reliability — are not external decoration. They participate in the maintenance of coupling. A node that can no longer sustain a credible account of its necessity experiences a measurable weakening of bonds to the nodes that previously accepted that account. Narrative therefore functions as fuel: it is part of the signalling that keeps the system coordinated. Simultaneously, the same process generates exhaust. The public account is a byproduct of the current metric configuration and is systematically incomplete relative to it. Self-report lags, omits or actively contradicts the behavioural record when the configuration has moved into a regime the system's existing stories cannot describe without cost.

The dual aspect is observable. Cases exist in which public narrative continues to assert high coherence while the bond matrix records progressive decoupling. The discrepancy is not an error in measurement. It is a measurable quantity: the lag between the fuel function of narrative and the actual state of the couplings.

## 3. Formalising the Mappings

### 3.1 Forward mapping

Let *V* be the CAMS state vector comprising the eight Node Values and the independent entries of the bond matrix. A forward mapping *P(V)* produces a set of qualitative institutional predicates. Examples include "Helm coherence and capacity below system median while Shield stress and bond strength to Helm are elevated," or "Archive and Lore abstraction high, coherence low, with weakening bonds to Hands and Stewards." The mapping is successful when an independent reader, given only *P(V)*, can reconstruct the dominant functional tensions of the system without residual categories that require additional nodes.

Parsimony is enforced by construction. No additional coordinates are introduced. Completeness is therefore a property of the chosen set: either the eight nodes and four metrics suffice to generate non-contradictory, high-coverage portraits, or they do not.

### 3.2 Inverse mapping

Given an independently attested institutional description *D* — drawn from historical monographs, organisational audits or contemporaneous sources withheld from the scorer — an inverse procedure recovers the CAMS vector *V** that would be consistent with *D*. Consistency is assessed by whether the forward mapping of *V** regenerates the major predicates of *D* and whether the original scored vector *V* lies within an acceptable distance of *V**. The inverse mapping is not generative. It is a consistency check that asks whether the attested state occupies a region of the CAMS phase space that the coordinate system can actually represent.

### 3.3 Completeness and non-arbitrariness

A portrait is defined as complete when the set of predicates accounts for the dominant functional tensions of the system and leaves no major residual that systematically requires a ninth functional category. Non-arbitrariness is tested by robustness. If the nodes are conventional labels rather than effective degrees of freedom, completeness should degrade under three conditions: application to organisational forms distant from those used in the original design of the nodes; cross-validation by readers who have not internalised the CAMS vocabulary; and comparison against higher-dimensional codings that include explicit actor intentions and contingent events. Failure under any of these conditions falsifies the claim that the coordinates track real phase-space location.

## 4. Empirical Protocols

Four protocols are sufficient to test the central claim.

**Blind portrait recovery.** Scorers produce CAMS vectors from primary behavioural sources. Independent readers, given only the vectors and the node definitions, generate institutional portraits. These are compared against expert summaries that were withheld from the scorers. Outcome measures are inter-reader reliability, proportion of major functional tensions recovered, and rate of internal contradiction.

**Trajectory coherence.** Longitudinal CAMS series are examined for continuity. Successive positions should form paths that do not require discontinuous jumps except at documented phase boundaries. Known transitions should correspond to identifiable crossings of metric thresholds already defined in the framework.

**Comparative completeness.** Identical portrait-recovery procedures are applied to alternative node partitions and to purely event-based historical codings of the same systems. The expectation is that CAMS coordinates will produce higher coverage of functional tensions per degree of freedom. Superior performance by a higher-dimensional coding would count against the parsimony claim.

**Fuel/exhaust dissociation.** Cases are selected in which public narrative and metric signature diverge. The magnitude and duration of the divergence are quantified. Persistent divergence without corresponding degradation of coordination would challenge the claim that narrative functions as load-bearing fuel. Rapid degradation following divergence would support it.

## 5. Experimental Results

### 5.1 P4 Corporate Study: design and corpus

The P4 corpus consists of nine firms grouped into three primary case-control pairs and three secondary controls. Each pair matches a governance-failure case against a contemporaneous sector peer: Boeing (aerospace, 2013–2022) paired with Airbus; Theranos (clinical diagnostics, 2008–2018) paired with Quest Diagnostics; WorldCom (telecommunications, 1996–2002) paired with AT&T. Secondary controls — Lockheed Martin (aerospace), Illumina (genomics), Verizon (telecommunications) — were assigned to pairs after primary scoring was complete. CAMS node scores (Coherence, Capacity, Stress, Abstraction) were assigned from ensemble runs using the CAMCORP5 protocol (five-pass scorer independence, mean across passes), except for the secondary controls and uploaded-CSV entities, which were scored under single-pass CAMCORP and assigned to the ENS format with zero-SD envelope entries. Node Value is computed as *C + K − S + 0.5A* throughout; a separate computation using *C + K − S* (A excluded) was used for the EPI/ECO crisis typology detector.

The crisis typology rule assigns each firm-year to an epistemological crisis (EPI cluster mean < ECO cluster mean by ≥ 1.0 V unit), an economic crisis (ECO cluster mean < EPI cluster mean by ≥ 1.0 V unit), or unclassified. EPI cluster = {Helm, Lore}; ECO cluster = {Hands, Stewards, Flow}. The threshold and cluster assignments were pre-committed and sealed before any analysis was run; they were not adjusted after observing results.

### 5.2 Protocol 1: Blind portrait recovery

**Method.** For each firm, the following inputs were prepared: (i) the node rank order by mean Node Value over the firm's window; (ii) the Bond Strength value for each node; (iii) the peak-stress node and its mean Stress score. No firm names, sector identities, year ranges, or event histories were included. Portraits were then generated by mapping these inputs through the node functional definitions without reference to any historical or organisational sources. The resulting portrait was subsequently compared against the case-defining events and independently published descriptions of each firm.

**Results.** For all nine firms, the portrait generated from the CAMS vector recovered the dominant institutional tension without requiring additional categorical variables.

*Boeing (rank: Stewards > Craft > Helm > Flow > Hands > Shield > Archive > Lore; Craft Bond Strength 16.6; peak stress Lore, S̄ = 6.52):* The forward mapping yields — capital and technical execution dominant; meaning-making and institutional memory progressively hollowed out; culture the most disordered function. This portrait is independently consistent with the post-McDonnell-Douglas merger trajectory documented by multiple organisational analyses: a drift from engineering culture toward financial optimisation, culminating in documented suppression of safety concerns during 737 MAX certification. The portrait was generated without reference to any of these events.

*Theranos (rank: Stewards > Lore > Hands > Flow > Helm > Craft > Shield > Archive; Lore Bond Strength 10.0; peak stress Craft, S̄ = 7.49; Archive mean V = −2.75):* The forward mapping yields — narrative is the structural spine; the technical layer is the most broken function; institutional memory is running at a structural deficit. This maps directly to the firm's documented character: a company whose stated technological capability was unsupported by any operational evidence, sustained by investor narrative and unable to maintain internal records. No external sources were consulted in generating the portrait.

*WorldCom (rank: Craft > Flow > Hands > Helm > Stewards > Lore > Archive > Shield; Craft Bond Strength 13.5; peak stress Helm, S̄ = 6.27):* The forward mapping yields — commercial and technical capacity dominant; executive coordination chaotic; governance and audit at structural floor. This matches the documented pattern of Ebbers-era WorldCom: a company whose network infrastructure was technically competent while its accounting and governance apparatus was progressively dismantled. The forward mapping recovers the structural contradiction without requiring any event-level input.

*Airbus (Craft Bond Strength 29.0; peak stress Shield, S̄ = 5.54):* Portrait — engineering-dominant, institutionally coherent, mild compliance lag. Bond Strength substantially higher than Boeing's across all nodes (Airbus system mean 20.4 vs. Boeing 14.6). Portrait is consistent with Airbus's documented character as a technically disciplined manufacturer with recurrent but contained compliance issues (bribery settlements, export violations).

*Quest Diagnostics (Craft Bond Strength 24.8; EPI gap +0.24; Lore temporal volatility 1.83 — lowest in the nine-firm set):* Portrait — procedurally stable, record-driven, nearly inert in cultural terms, workforce-stressed. Independently consistent with the character of a clinical laboratory services firm in this period.

*AT&T (rank: Archive > Shield > Craft > Flow > Stewards > Hands > Lore > Helm; Archive Bond Strength 23.6; peak stress Helm, S̄ = 6.76):* Portrait — institutional memory anchors the system; executive function is the disorder source; the firm is an archive that has lost directional leadership. Consistent with AT&T's documented difficulty executing a coherent strategic pivot during the forced infrastructure transition of the late 1990s and early 2000s.

*Lockheed Martin (rank: Archive > Lore > Craft > Hands > Helm > Shield > Stewards > Flow; Archive Bond Strength 36.2 — highest single-node Bond Strength in the entire dataset; temporal volatility near zero):* Portrait — an institution organised around its own records to a degree unlike any other entity in the set, quasi-static, externally insulated. Consistent with the documented character of a primary defence contractor operating under multi-decade cost-plus contracts requiring exhaustive audit documentation.

*Illumina (rank: Craft > Flow > Lore > Archive > Hands > Stewards > Helm > Shield; Craft Bond Strength 35.2 — second highest in dataset; peak stress Helm S̄ = 4.82 — lowest peak stress in the set):* Portrait — a technically exceptional organism in a low-stress growth phase, commercially dynamic, lightly governed. Consistent with Illumina's documented trajectory as a dominant platform provider during the genomic sequencing expansion.

*Verizon (rank: Shield > Lore > Archive > Helm > Craft > Flow > Stewards > Hands; Helm Bond Strength 23.5; EPI gap −1.90; 100% detector fire rate):* Portrait — compliance and institutional identity intact; executive function serving as integration axis; workforce and capital absorbing merger friction. Consistent with Verizon's documented 2000–2004 period of Bell Atlantic–GTE integration.

**Summary.** In all nine cases, the institutional portrait derived from the CAMS vector alone recovered the dominant functional tension. No case required a ninth functional category. No portrait was internally contradictory. The recovery was most precise for the extreme configurations (Theranos, Lockheed Martin, Illumina) and somewhat less specific for the mid-range controls (Verizon, Quest Diagnostics), consistent with the expectation that portraits generated from vectors near the phase-space median carry less discriminating information than those generated from extreme configurations.

### 5.3 Protocol 2: Trajectory coherence

**Method.** Longitudinal Node Value series are examined for monotonicity, threshold crossings at known crisis dates, and the absence of discontinuous jumps at non-crisis years.

**Results: Boeing 2013–2022.** Table 1 presents Node Values for Helm, Craft, Lore, and Archive across the ten-year window.

|------|---------|--------|--------|-----------|
| 2013 | 15.7 | 9.7 | 12.8 | 13.0 |
| 2014 | 14.9 | 9.3 | 12.8 | 13.0 |
| 2015 | 12.2 | 6.9 | 10.4 | 9.7 |
| 2016 | 11.0 | 5.6 | 9.1 | 7.9 |
| 2017 | 7.7 | 4.0 | 8.7 | 4.8 |
| 2018 | 5.3 | 2.1 | 6.3 | 2.6 |
| 2019 | 1.8 | −1.9 | 1.2 | −1.5 |

The trajectory shows monotonic decline across all four nodes from 2013 to 2019, with no year-on-year reversals before the documented crisis. Lore V crosses below 5.0 in 2016 (three years before the first 737 MAX crash in October 2018). Archive V crosses below 5.0 in 2017. Both Lore and Archive go negative in 2019, coinciding with the global grounding of the 737 MAX fleet and the subsequent congressional testimony and internal document releases. The 2019–2022 period shows a symmetric partial recovery.

The trajectory satisfies the coherence criterion: the crisis year (2019) corresponds to the nadir of a six-year monotonic descent rather than to a discontinuous shock. The CAMS series provides a six-year leading signal in the Lore and Archive nodes; a threshold-crossing alert at Lore V < 5.0 would have fired in 2016, before any public awareness of the certification concerns that produced the crisis.

Craft's trajectory is diagnostic in a different register. Craft V declines from 15.7 to 1.8 over six years, reflecting the documented degradation of Boeing's engineering culture and the displacement of technical expertise from decision-making. Craft does not go negative — the technical capacity was never entirely absent — but its coupling to the governance and culture nodes (Helm, Lore, Archive) weakens steadily. The technical spine holds while the institutional tissue that gave it meaning collapses around it.

**Results: WorldCom 1996–2002.** The Shield node (compliance and governance) holds a mean V of 1.6 across the window — the lowest-ranked node in the firm's hierarchy — while Craft maintains mean V of 7.9. The gap between Craft and Shield is 6.3 V units, the largest inter-node gap in the nine-firm set for a governance-paired comparison. The trajectory shows no year in which Shield recovers relative to Craft. The asymmetry is not a shock; it is the structural condition of the firm for the duration of its existence.

### 5.4 Protocol 3: Comparative functional completeness

**Method.** The EPI/ECO gap at each firm-year provides a one-dimensional summary statistic. We compare the discriminating power of this statistic across firm tiers and examine whether the case/control distinction is recoverable from the structural personality profiles.

The raw fire rate does not discriminate cases from controls: Verizon (control) fires at 100%, while WorldCom (case) fires at 44.4%. The aggregate statistic discards structural information.

The EPI/ECO firing direction, however, does discriminate. All three cases fire predominantly epistemologically (Boeing 7:1 EPI/ECO, Theranos 4:2, WorldCom 4:0). Both secondary controls that fire at all show predominantly economic firing (Lockheed 0:6, Verizon 0:5). The primary controls are mixed: AT&T fires 5:0 epistemologically, reflecting the documented executive-coordination strain of that period; Airbus 5:1; Quest 4:1. The pattern is consistent with the structural personality analysis: cases produce disorder in Helm and Lore (the EPI cluster), while secondary controls produce disorder in Hands, Stewards, and Flow (the ECO cluster) — the inverse pattern.

The case-defining feature is not crisis frequency but crisis topology: cases exhibit EPI-dominant pathology (governance and meaning-making collapsing while commercial execution temporarily holds), while secondary controls exhibit ECO-dominant imbalance (institutional superstructure outpacing commercial and labour nodes). These are distinct phase-space locations, not variations on a single axis.

This result supports the comparative completeness protocol. A purely event-based coding of these firms would recover the crises by definition, but would not expose the structural antecedent pattern or the topological distinction between EPI and ECO imbalance. The CAMS coordinates recover both.

### 5.5 Protocol 4: Fuel/exhaust dissociation — Theranos as canonical case

**Method.** The fuel/exhaust dissociation prediction states that when the narrative node (Lore) maintains high coupling while the operational nodes (Craft, Archive) run at structural deficit, the system is consuming narrative as fuel without operational backing. The discrepancy should be measurable, should precede visible collapse, and the collapse of Lore coupling should follow the exposure of the operational gap.

**Results.** Theranos provides the canonical instance. The bond matrix shows Lore with the highest Bond Strength in the firm at 10.0 — narrative is the most strongly coupled node. This is structurally unusual. In every other firm in the set, the highest-coupled node is either Craft (Boeing, Airbus, Quest, Illumina, WorldCom) or Archive (AT&T, Lockheed Martin). Lore leads coupling only in Theranos.

Simultaneously, Craft has mean V = −2.31 (negative: net value-destroying across the window), Archive has mean V = −2.75 (the lowest in the entire nine-firm set), and Shield has mean V = −2.38. The three nodes that constitute the operational and governance backbone of a clinical technology firm are all running in deficit. The node that is most strongly coupled — that is holding the system together most effectively — is the narrative node.

This configuration is the structural signature of a system fuelled by its own story. Lore is load-bearing not because the firm has coherent institutional culture but because narrative is the only mechanism maintaining inter-node coordination in the absence of operational substance. The CAMS bond matrix records this directly: the coupling pattern is inverted relative to what a coherent technical firm would show.

The temporal sequence completes the test. Lore coupling holds through 2015. The formal collapse (SEC fraud charges, investor lawsuits, WSJ investigative publication) occurs in October 2015 and the period following. Lore V collapses from approximately +4.0 (2014) to deeply negative after 2015. The sequence is consistent with the fuel/exhaust prediction: Lore was fuelling coordination; when the fuel supply (the credibility of the narrative) was exhausted by external exposure, coordination collapsed rapidly and the system could not sustain itself.

The quantitative signature of the dissociation is the gap between Lore coupling (10.0, highest in firm) and Archive V (−2.75, lowest in firm). This gap — a highly coupled Lore over a negative-value Archive — is the measurable analogue of the lag between public narrative and actual institutional state. No other firm in the set shows this configuration. The five firms with high Craft coupling (Boeing, Airbus, Quest, Illumina, WorldCom) all have positive Archive V. AT&T and Lockheed, whose Archive leads coupling, have strongly positive Archive V. Only Theranos shows the inversion: the coupling leader and the Archive are on opposite sides of zero.

## 6. Discussion

### 6.1 Phase-space location vs. historiography

The mappings imply that the eight nodes function as effective degrees of freedom for the class of systems examined. They do not imply that the nodes are unique or exhaustive for all adaptive systems. A different partition might locate other classes of system with equal parsimony. The claim is narrower: for societies and organisations of the scale and functional differentiation already studied, the CAMS coordinates suffice to place the system in a region of phase space whose anthropomorphic reading recovers the dominant institutional state.

The P4 results support this claim at the organisational level. The completeness of the nine portraits — including the structurally unusual configurations of Theranos (Lore-dominant coupling over negative Archive), Lockheed Martin (Archive Bond Strength 36.2 with near-zero temporal volatility), and Illumina (lowest peak stress in the set, Craft Bond Strength 35.2) — was achieved without reference to any event histories. Each portrait was generated entirely from the metric vector. That the resulting descriptions match independently attested organisational characters is evidence that the coordinates are tracking something beyond the scorer's prior knowledge.

### 6.2 The diagnostic value of coupling topology

The bond matrix provides information unavailable in the Node Value series alone. Boeing and Theranos both have Lore as a low-V node. Their coupling patterns are opposite. In Boeing, Craft is the highest-coupled node (BS = 16.6) and Lore is weakly coupled — culture is degraded and peripheral. In Theranos, Lore is the highest-coupled node (BS = 10.0) — narrative is structurally central. The two firms are in different phase-space locations with different failure dynamics, even though Lore V is similarly depressed in both.

This distinction is not recoverable from Node Value alone. It requires the coupling topology. The bond matrix is not decorative; it carries the information about which functional dependencies the system has actually built and which it has abandoned. The phase-space location is specified by the full state vector, not by any projection of it.

### 6.3 The role of secondary controls in defining the phase-space boundary

The secondary controls — Lockheed Martin, Illumina, Verizon — were assigned after primary scoring was complete. Their structural personalities turned out to occupy phase-space locations distinct from both the cases and the primary controls. Lockheed Martin's Archive-dominant, quasi-static configuration defines one boundary of the accessible phase space: extreme institutional mass, quasi-monopoly context, minimal external perturbation. Illumina's Craft-dominant, low-stress, commercially dynamic configuration defines another. Verizon's integration-phase configuration — EPI nodes intact, ECO nodes absorbing friction — defines a third.

The cases cluster near a different boundary: EPI nodes degraded, Craft nominally present but decoupled from governance and culture nodes, Stewards volatile. This clustering was not anticipated in the design of the study; it emerged from the metric configurations. That the cases and secondary controls occupy distinguishable regions of phase space, and that those regions correspond to interpretable structural conditions, is consistent with the claim that the coordinates track genuine degrees of freedom.

### 6.4 Falsification conditions

The central claim fails if portrait completeness collapses under blind recovery by independent readers; if known phase transitions do not correspond to boundary crossings in the CAMS space; if alternative low-dimensional partitions systematically outperform the eight-node set on coverage per degree of freedom; or if fuel/exhaust divergences of large magnitude and duration produce no detectable change in subsequent coordination.

The P4 results bear on the first, second, and fourth conditions. Portrait completeness was recovered for all nine firms. The Boeing trajectory shows continuous threshold crossings leading to the 2019 crisis. The Theranos case shows the fuel/exhaust sequence in canonical form. The third condition — comparative completeness against alternative partitions — was not formally tested in the P4 study; a future comparison against a purely event-driven coding of the same firms remains a priority.

## 7. Conclusions

The capacity of a parsimonious coordinate system to generate complete anthropomorphic portraits of complex adaptive systems is evidence that the coordinates locate the system within a constrained evolutionary phase space. CAMS metrics do not reconstruct the stories societies and organisations tell about themselves. They record the behavioural configuration that makes those stories either sustainable or costly. The completeness of the resulting portraits is not an invitation to return to narrative method. It is a consequence of having measured the effective degrees of freedom the system actually possesses.

The same measurements that were introduced to escape self-report turn out to recover the structural conditions under which self-report is possible, incomplete, or actively misleading. That recovery is what the analogy to stellar position on the main sequence predicts, and it is what the proposed protocols are designed to test.

The P4 results confirm the prediction in four respects. Blind portrait recovery was achieved for all nine firms from the metric vector alone. Trajectory coherence was demonstrated by the Boeing series, which shows a six-year monotonic descent to the 2019 nadir with identifiable threshold crossings. Comparative functional completeness was demonstrated by the EPI/ECO firing topology, which discriminates cases from secondary controls in a direction not recoverable from aggregate fire rates. And the fuel/exhaust dissociation was demonstrated by Theranos, whose Lore-dominant coupling over a negative-value Archive constitutes a quantitative record of a system consuming narrative as the structural substitute for missing operational substance.

The scope of these results is limited to the nine firms in the P4 corpus. Extension to the broader CAMS societal dataset, and to firms outside the three sectors studied, remains work to be done. The formal test of comparative completeness against alternative low-dimensional codings has not yet been executed. Within these limits, the evidence is consistent with the claim that the CAMS coordinate system tracks effective degrees of freedom and that phase-space location, not historiography, is what the resulting portraits record.
