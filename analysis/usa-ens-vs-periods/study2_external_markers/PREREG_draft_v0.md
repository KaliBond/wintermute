## 1. Why this exists
The earlier test (`/workspace/analysis/usa_ensemble_vs_periods/`, H2) used 14 conventional transition dates. Kari agrees those dates are themselves coordination-stress signals, so H2 was confounded: the markers were partly defined by the thing the drift measure detects. This design replaces them with markers dated by outside procedures (a committee, an election count, a ratification), not by strain narratives. H2 results are neither revised nor reinterpreted here.

## 2. Question and hypothesis
**Question.** Do shifts in how the eight nodes relate to one another in the canonical annual USA_ENS precede externally dated markers more often than chance?
**H_E (primary).** Annual drift upcrossings of the state-based measure X^S fall in the lead window T−5..T−1 of NBER business-cycle peaks more often than expected under the circular-shift and random-date nulls, and more often than in the lag window T..T+4.
The test is about temporal order only. It makes no claim that node shifts cause the markers.

## 5. Drift measures
**Primary: X^S (state-based).** With states s_0, s_1, … in order, for any year t in state s_k with k ≥ 3: X^S_t = 1 − cos(θ(s_k), mean of θ over the previous min(5, k) distinct states). The value is carried forward across all years of the state. Rationale: the annual X of the frozen run mixes real shifts with repeats of the same state (X can be exactly 0 or a flat plateau), so lead timing inside a plateau is not information. X^S can upcross only in a year a new state begins, which is the only year new information exists. Minimum 3 previous states is a design choice (open decision D5); it gives X^S support from 1910 and first upcrossing-capable year L = 1911.
**Secondary measures (reported beside the primary, never replacing it):**
- X^A: the frozen annual X (mean of previous 5 calendar years). Continuity with H2; stepwise artefacts acknowledged.
- X^C: X^S evaluated on change-years only with P90 over change-years only. Diagnostic; expected to give about 5 events, so flagged as underpowered, not a test.
Threshold: P90 of X^S over its support (1910–2025), via the frozen percentile call. Upcrossing: X_{t−1} < thr ≤ X_t.

## 6. Procedure (reuses frozen machinery from `/workspace/analysis/usa_ensemble_vs_periods/run.py`, PREREG sha256 `3603e474ada3652e2d665e8787ca9c4bb08ba3e38bd23a8614e69611c3aea2fe`)
6.1 **Eligibility.** Marker T is scored if L+6 ≤ T ≤ 2021 (L = first upcrossing-capable year). This is the frozen 1912–2021 window re-expressed so it adapts to X^S support. Currently [1917, 2021].
6.2 **Hits.** S_lead = number of scored markers with ≥ 1 upcrossing in T−5..T−1. S_lag likewise in T..T+4. A marker counts once per window.
6.3 **N1 circular shift.** Shift the upcrossing years circularly over L..2025, excluding shifts s with min(s, N−s) ≤ 2. Preserves event spacing and clustering. Null for S_lead and S_lag.
6.4 **N2 random dates.** Draw n distinct years uniformly from [L+6, 2021] with minimum spacing m = min(4, max(1, smallest gap in the observed scored set)), B = 10000, seed 20261029. p = share of null S_lead ≥ observed (with the frozen +1 convention).
6.5 **Multiplicity.** One confirmatory test (E1 primary, X^S), α = 0.05, no adjustment. The secondary family (E2, E3) uses Holm over both tests (each needs p_N1 and p_N2, reported with the larger p used for Holm). Secondary results are labelled supportive only.
6.6 Report for every set: n, S_lead, S_lag, null means, p_N1, p_N2, hit rate, list of markers with hit events and lead years. Events and markers are tabulated in full, not only summarised.

## 7. Support rule and falsification
**Supported (E1 primary only)** requires all of: S_lead/n ≥ 0.50; S_lead > S_lag; p_N1 ≤ 0.05; p_N2 ≤ 0.05; and none of F4–F7 triggered.
**Falsified or not supported if any:**
- F1: S_lead/n < 0.50.
- F2: S_lead ≤ S_lag (drift is not earlier than after).
- F3: p_N1 > 0.05 or p_N2 > 0.05.
- F4: the C1 census control shows S_lead/n ≥ 0.50 with p_N1 ≤ 0.05. The result is voided as a calendar or method artefact.
- F5: E1 passes under X^S but not under X^A in the same direction (S_lead > S_lag and p_N1 ≤ 0.05). Labelled "measure-dependent", not supported.
- F6: the N1 null-mean hit rate for E1 is ≥ 0.45. The test is declared uninformative, not supported, because chance alone nearly meets the threshold.
- F7 (anchoring check): in E1, upcrossings at T itself (window T..T) ≥ the best single lead year count. Read as rater anchoring on the marker year; result labelled "anchored" and not supported.
- F8 (power floor): fewer than 5 X^S upcrossings. The test is declared uninformative.
Failing the primary does not license promoting a secondary set. A secondary pass with a primary failure is reported as exploratory only.
