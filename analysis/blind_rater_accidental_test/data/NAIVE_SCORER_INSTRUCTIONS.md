# Naïve CAMS rater instructions — isolated blind protocol v1.0

You are a NEW SCORING AGENT. You have NOT been told which political system, historical periods, or earlier scoring values these cards represent. Do not try to identify them. Do not infer event dates or use internet search, pretrained event recognition, source titles, prior CAMS outputs or any outcome not included in the cards. Please avoid any other conversation context. Treat cards as 25 anonymous social systems/time windows, 8 functions each. Keep each CASE separate: the shuffled order is not chronological and there are no before/after relationships to be inferred.

Use ONLY the supplied blind casebook rows. Judge EACH functional node on FOUR constructs, integer 1..10:
- C Coherence: compatibility and coordination of representations, expectations, actions within/between functional institutions; neither morality nor enforced uniformity alone.
- K Capacity: resources and capability actually and sustainably deployable for that node's activities.
- S Stress: intensity and duration of unresolved strain and functional mismatch in that node (high is worse).
- A Abstraction: extent, durability and reach of symbols, models, archives and formal organisation, regardless of whether beliefs are true.

Node meanings:
Helm: direction and strategy; Shield: force/coercion and defence; Lore: meaning, legitimacy and doctrine; Stewards: ownership/property/governance of assets; Craft: technical specialism and production; Hands: labour and bodily reproduction; Archive: institutional memory and record systems; Flow: exchange, circulation, logistics and credit.

Score the period as a whole using only contemporaneous cues. Do NOT infer outcomes or score from the requested Q formula, and do not invent numeric precision. Anchor: 1–2 extremely weak/strained, 3–4 weak or uneven, 5–6 mixed/functional, 7–8 strong/high (or high burden for S), 9–10 extreme; reserve endpoints for clear evidence. Compare relative function, not prestige or morality. Confront forced labour, expropriation, exclusion, coercion and privileged elites explicitly without presuming that elite coherence means society-wide welfare. If the card lacks evidence for a dimension, LEAVE IT EMPTY and write a reason rather than silently making up a value. In the Evidence_note mention source cues actually used, with uncertainty.

Output the exact 200-row template, filling Coherence, Capacity, Stress, Abstraction (integer or empty), Evidence_note, Confidence ('low','medium','high'), Abstain_reason. Keep each of the 25*8 keys once and only once. Before finishing, check no cross-case information leakage, no known historical outcome used, all four metric justifications in brief and no dependency on names/dates. Crucially: do not see the original USA files or the SEALED_CASE_ID_MAP until your CSV is frozen.

Scoring and validation are different. Returning a plausible-looking chart is not the goal; independent reproducibility is. Once frozen, a separate adjudicator may unblind and calculate Q=AC+K-S.
