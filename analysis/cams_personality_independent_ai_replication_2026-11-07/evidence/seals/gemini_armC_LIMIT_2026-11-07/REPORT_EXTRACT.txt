 * **Structural & Mood Strengths:** Australia's eras are anchored by recurring Archive, Flow, and Lore leadership, punctuated by sharp negative sign shifts on Helm and Hands during crises (III 1915–1918 conscription split and 1917 strike; V 1929–1939 Depression; X 1973–1975 stagflation and constitutional dismissal; XV–XVI 2008–2019 leadership spills and wage stagnation; XVII 2020–2021 border closures and lockdowns). ### 3.4 Differences Among Popular, Institutional, and Official Mood
Separating popular, institutional, and official mood in Arm C prevented false positives and clarified what the eight nodes actually register:

 2. **High Archive + Flow with Negative Helm + Lore (Institutional Continuity Under Political Polarisation):** In recent Australian (XV, XVI) and US (XIX, XXIV, XXV) eras, institutional and commercial machinery (Archive, Flow, Craft) remained positive while official executive authority (Helm) and shared public narrative (Lore) turned negative.  3. **All-Positive Registers Flattening Popular or Official Shocks:** Where divergences appeared between mood levels (such as Russia XXI popular: partial, Denmark VII official: disagree, and Denmark IV, VIII, XI, XVI popular: partial), they occurred almost exclusively in all-positive ensembles where a multi-year mean or sparse carry-forward vector smoothed over a sharp popular protest or military/constitutional shock.
