Name-masking is not anonymity. The society names are removed from the packet, but the data is public and its patterns — including the historical knowledge built into the scoring — can suggest or confirm identity to a reader with contextual knowledge. The person choosing the dataset (you) knows the identity; the packet protects only the downstream reader, such as a fresh AI session or a colleague starting blind.
