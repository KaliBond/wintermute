# Erratum: bond scale in the USA CAMS Outlook 2027 to 2030

25 October 2026.

The USA CAMS Outlook, dated 19 September 2026, reports bond strength of 7.8 falling to 5.8 in 2020, and a projected 4.48 by 2030. Those figures cannot be right on the published CAMS scale. The bounded bond, sqrt(q_i q_j) · 2^(−(S_i+S_j)/10) with q = (0.6C + 0.4A)/10, cannot exceed 1.

The cause is on record. The source file, USA_CAMS_mfin_2015-2026 block 1, used a different formula from its methodology note: B_ij = [0.6 C_i C_j + 0.4 A_i A_j] × exp(−(S_i+S_j)/20). It multiplies raw 0 to 10 scores, takes no square root, and does not divide by 10, so it is unbounded. The delivered values do not match even that formula. The reconciliation of 17 September 2026 found all 12 years failing, at 0.317 to 0.437 of the specified values.

We are not deleting the Outlook. Its bond figures stand with this note beside them. Read them as values on an unbounded and undocumented scale, not as CAMS bonds.

On the bounded formula, using the v3 US series (an annual series, 1900 to 2026, now a retained alternative), the network mean over 28 node pairs is 0.288 in 2020 and 0.256 in 2026, against 0.419 in 2019. The pattern the Outlook described, a drop in 2020 and a further decline into 2026, survives on the bounded scale. The numbers it printed do not.

The Outlook's word "forecast" is also withdrawn. Its 2027 to 2030 paths are damped extrapolations of one scoring run. They belong in the discovery register, not the prospective register. Re-run on v3 mean V, with the Outlook's stated weights, the path runs from 8.83 (2026) to about 7.5 (2030). That is a different series, and a different level, from the Outlook's 7.42 to 6.16, which is a reminder that the US series do not agree with one another.

As of 25 October 2026 the canonical annual US series is the v1.2-OPT "hi-fi" ensemble, data/nations/USA_v12opt_hifi_block1.csv (commit 31cc441, claude-sonnet-4-6, cams-raw-scorer-v1.2-opt, five passes, 1900 to 2025). Its original Bond Strength column runs from 6.783 to 35.068, so it has the same scale problem. That column is kept, unchanged, and is now documented as legacy and unbounded. Beside it sits a new column, Bond_Strength_Bounded, generated by scripts/bounded_bond.py. On that column the network mean bond is 0.159 in 2020 and 0.194 in 2025, with a maximum of 0.473 across the series.

The hi-fi and v3 series agree only loosely on Shield and Helm (r about 0.65, mean absolute gap about 3 in V). USA_ENS and v3 are retained as alternatives. No result should mix them with the canonical series. See juno/CANONICAL_STATUS.md.

A bond can't be 9. Now the record says so.

Peace, people.
