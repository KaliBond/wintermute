Method note. Ports the fixed-and-stabilised cams_network_skeleton.py ODE engine to JavaScript (fixed-step RK4, dt=0.05): the canonical JUNO node-value formula V = C+K−S+0.5A on an 8-node × 4-metric (32-dimensional) system, with a general derivative clamp keeping every metric inside [0,10] regardless of the interior terms. The example bond-topology matrix and coefficients are unchanged from the Python skeleton and have not been calibrated against real institutional data — see the source skeleton for the documented stability history (three unbounded-growth bugs found and fixed) and known calibration gap (Capacity has no interior self-damping term; long trajectories can settle at a degenerate boundary equilibrium rather than an interpretable fixed point).
