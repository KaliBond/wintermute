# Sources — external series

## Trade share (China % of Australian merchandise exports)
1. World Bank WITS Country Profile summaries, Australia, years 2006–2023  
   URL pattern: `https://wits.worldbank.org/CountryProfile/en/Country/AUS/Year/{YYYY}/Summary`  
   Indicator: Export Partner Share (%) with China.  
   Local extract: `wits_china_shares.csv`
2. UN Comtrade public preview API (calendar 2024):  
   `reporterCode=36` (Australia), `partnerCode=156` (China) vs `partnerCode=0` (World), `flowCode=X`, `cmdCode=TOTAL`  
   Values used: China 102,629,123,796.603 / World 340,854,851,740.669 → **30.11%**
3. Cross-checks (not used as primary series): AMP Econosights (ABS-based commentary); DFAT top-15 partners FY PDFs (Akamai 403 from this box); USSC note of 27.6% in 2022.
