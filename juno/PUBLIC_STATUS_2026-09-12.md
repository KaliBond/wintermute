# What CAMS is claiming in public (12 September 2026)

This note records the shrunk public contract for CAMS / JUNO v1.2. It does not replace `CANONICAL_STATUS.md` or the OOS freeze; it states how newcomers should read Explore.

## The claim

CAMS is a **state monitor**, a **diagnostic grammar**, and an **uncertainty sensor**: a thermometer for institutional health, not a crystal ball. It does not predict events, name the date of a crash, or prescribe interventions.

## What survived hard testing (compressed)

1. **Face validity.** Instrument floors and contested years repeatedly coincide with independently recognised catastrophes (e.g. Cambodia 1975–1978; Netherlands 1940–1945; Tesla 2008; BHP at Samarco; Huawei 2021). Historical knowledge feeds the scores — face validity, not clairvoyance.
2. **Compression.** Real trajectories occupy a narrow region of the available eight-function space. Whether that is genuine coupling or partly data smoothness remains under test.
3. **Disagreement is information.** Independent AI-rater disagreement peaks in contested and disrupted periods.
4. **Self-refutation.** Attractive claims have been killed in public: crises do not preferentially strike the weakest function; three composite claims failed all of their preregistered tests.

## Universality from giving ground

The biggest win from shrinking the claim was not marketing humility. It was **portability**. Once CAMS stopped trying to explain everything and predict nothing, the same eight-node grammar showed up as a usable instrument across nations, firms, and cities — Operator Portability, corporate panels, and the blind morphospace work. Giving ground cleared the fog enough to see that the framework travels.
